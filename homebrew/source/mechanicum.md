# Liber Ingenium for The Horus Heresy 2.0 — Homebrew Conversion

Nov 1, 2026 · @Isaac

## Introduction

This doc restructures *Liber Ingenium: The Marching Forge* (v1.3, released 30 May 2023 by the Liber Panoptica Development Team) into the same layout as the other homebrew conversion docs. Liber Ingenium is already written for The Horus Heresy 2.0, so the rules are carried over as written rather than converted.

Liber Ingenium adds new units and options for Taghmata Omnissiah (Mechanicum) armies, plus new units and options for the Questoris Households and Titan Legions Divisio Tactica. You still need the Horus Heresy: Age of Darkness rulebook, *Liber Mechanicum*, and the Warhammer Community PDFs to use it. Page references to *Liber Mechanicum* are kept as the source gives them.

If Games Workshop releases official rules for any unit or item here, the official rules take priority unless both players agree otherwise. The Liber Panoptica team recommends using its FAQ, Errata and Balance Changes alongside these rules.

Liber Ingenium is unofficial, not for profit.

## Mechanicum Special Rules

These rules work alongside the special rules in *Liber Mechanicum* and the Horus Heresy: Age of Darkness rulebook.

**Archaeotechnological Marvel.** *A true Machina Opus, this machine is the brainchild of a single magos who wishes to be known forever as a supreme lord of machines.* You may only include one of these models in your army, in games of 2,000 points or more where the detachment also includes an Archmagos Prime. If the Archmagos Prime has the Reductor High Order of Techno-Arcana, you may include two instead of one.

**Attack Wing.** *These craft soar through the skies in great wings, diving upon enemies to deliver their doom.* A unit with this special rule containing more than one model must be deployed together, but once deployed its models act as separate units, including for Victory Points and Units Destroyed.

**Auxiliary Gunners.** *These gun turrets are slaved to manual firing systems, so individual targets can be tracked.* A weapon with this special rule may fire at a different target from the model's other weapons at no penalty.

**Command Node.** *The aura of command exuded by the Skitarii's leaders shines like a beacon in the noosphere.* A friendly unit with the Skitarii Sub-type wholly within 12" of a model with this special rule may use its unmodified Leadership for any Morale or Pinning tests.

**Command Protocols (X).** *With a merest thought, the War-Leader impels their soldiers to ever greater acts of bravery.* This rule always has a qualifier in brackets:

- **Kill-Clade:** this model, and any unit it has joined, may re-roll the dice when rolling Charge distance. If a model with this special rule is your Warlord, all Sicarian Kill-Clades in the same Detachment gain the Line Sub-type for as long as the Warlord has not been removed as a casualty.
- **Skitarius:** once per game, at the start of the controlling player's Shooting phase, nominate one unit with the Skitarii Sub-type within 12" of this model. That unit may re-roll To Hit rolls of 1 during that Shooting phase.

**Cybernetica Data-Web.** *A sprawling mass of cogitators and synthetic brain tissue, its sapience is thought by some too great a risk to use.* A model with this special rule may ignore the Programmed Behaviour restrictions of the Cybernetica Sub-type while a friendly model equipped with a cortex controller is within 24". It may always make Reactions, ignoring the Automata Sub-type's restrictions on Reactions, and it can never be affected by Cybertheurgy from any source, friendly or enemy.

**Datasphere Uplink.** *The uplink connecting all Skitarii and their war machines allows orders to change on the fly.* During deployment, if your army includes any models with this special rule, select one of the Protocols below. At the start of any Game Turn you may activate it; it lasts until the start of the next Game Turn, and all models in your army with this special rule gain its effect.

- **Predatory Engrams:** +1 Attacks and +1 Initiative.
- **Scrapcoded Exultation:** Shrouded (6+).
- **Self-Repair Programming:** models that have lost a Wound or Hull Point but remain in play regain 1 Wound or 1 Hull Point, up to their starting value.
- **Targeting Cryptogram:** +1 Ballistic Skill.

**Defensor-Grid Projector.** *These huge shield projectors cast a vehicle's void shields out over a far wider area.* Friendly units wholly within 6" of a model with this special rule are protected by its Void Shields. While it has Void Shields remaining, Shooting Attacks against protected units strike the Void Shields instead. If an attack collapses the last Void Shield, the rest of it strikes the originally targeted unit.

**Ejector Blowout.** *Runaway reactions and failed failsafes can be explosively fatal.* A weapon with this special rule counts all Gets Hot results as AP2.

**Fabricatus-Primus.** *Kelbor-Hal only.* Kelbor-Hal has the Artificia Machina, Artificia Cybernetica and Ephemera Incursus Cybertheurgic Arcana, with all their Rites and weapons, plus the unique Cybertheurgic weapon Exiguo Maelifica. He also has Bonds of Vassalage and Master Technomancer (*Liber Mechanicum*, page 98).

**Gravitic Implosion.** *This weapon's strikes are strong enough to pulp flesh and grind armour to paste.* Instead of rolling To Wound, a non-Vehicle model hit by this weapon must roll under its Strength on 2D6 or suffer a Wound; a double 6 always fails. Armour Saves and Damage Mitigation rolls may be taken as normal, except Shrouded. Against targets with an Armour Value, roll 4D6 for armour penetration. If the weapon also has the Blast type, leave the Blast marker in place after resolving all Wounds, or otherwise mark the area: it counts as Difficult and Dangerous Terrain until the start of the attacking player's next turn.

**High Techno-arcana: Noospherica.** *Koriel Zeth only. The Noosphere, newly pioneered, let its masters render their will in pure data.* Friendly models with the Cybernetica or Servitor Sub-type within 6" of Koriel Zeth gain +1 to their To Hit rolls. At the start of the controlling player's Game Turn, select one friendly unit with the Cybernetica Sub-type wholly within 12" of her: until the start of that player's next Game Turn, it may ignore any Unit Type or Sub-type restrictions on making Reactions.

**High Techno-arcana: Technoarchaeologica.** *Arkhan Land only. Magi who recover lost technologies are held in both mistrust and awe.* If Arkhan Land is holding an objective marker at the start of the controlling player's turn, he may investigate it. If he does, he may not move or charge that turn but may shoot as normal. At the end of the turn, if he is within 3" of the objective marker, the controlling player gains 1 Victory Point.

**Hovercraft.** *Riding a cushion of high-density gas, these craft cross terrain with ease.* A model with this special rule ignores all penalties for moving over Difficult and Dangerous Terrain. However, if it starts or ends its move in Difficult or Dangerous Terrain, it must take a Dangerous Terrain test.

**Makeshift Weapon.** *Not every tool of the 31st millennium was meant for combat, but in a pinch it will serve.* A model attacking with this weapon may make only a single attack with it, at Initiative step 1, and may not gain bonus Attacks for charging or from any special rule.

**Marksman Teams.** *Paired operators and gunners could achieve a precision others could not.* At the start of the controlling player's Shooting phase, a unit containing models with this special rule that did not move in the preceding Movement phase may focus its fire. If it does, those models add +1 to their To Hit rolls for Shooting Attacks, but may not use any variant of Firing Protocols (X) in that Shooting phase. The effect ends at the end of the Shooting phase.

**Master Cybertheurgist: Domina Ignis.** *Koriel Zeth only.* Koriel Zeth has all the Rites and weapons of the Artificia Machina and Ephemera Incursus arcanas.

**Master Cybertheurgist: Magister Machinis.** *Arkhan Land only.* Arkhan Land has all the Rites and weapons of the Artificia Machina and Artificia Cybernetica arcanas.

**Methodical Advance.** *This Automata's ponderous advance is easy to predict and avoid.* A model with this special rule may never make Sweeping Advances.

**Modular.** *These machines can be rearmed and refitted at a moment's notice.* Some options for a model with this special rule cost both points and Module Slots. Its wargear list shows how many Module Slots it has, and it may not buy upgrades needing more Module Slots than it has.

**Neurostatic Aura.** *The air around these hunters shimmers with malign data-djinn and corrosive code.* An enemy unit locked in combat with a model with this special rule subtracts 1 from its Weapon Skill and Initiative. This is not cumulative: a model suffers the penalty only once, however many models with this rule it is engaged with.

**Null-Rounds.** Successful Invulnerable Saves against Wounds caused by a weapon with this special rule must be re-rolled.

**Open-Topped.** *Some hulls are open to the elements, letting their occupants fire at the cost of protection.* A unit embarked on a Transport with this special rule may fire up to five ranged weapons, measuring range from the vehicle's hull. If it does, it counts as having moved, which may stop some weapon types firing. Rules that let a unit count as stationary for shooting (such as Relentless) do not apply while it is embarked on this vehicle. Rolls on the Vehicle Damage table against a model with this special rule add +1.

**Overload (X).** *A hit from this weapon feels like a barrage to anyone unlucky enough to be struck.* When a weapon with this special rule hits, a To Hit roll of X inflicts three automatic hits instead. For Blast and Template weapons, each successful hit counts as three hits on the unit.

**Overseer Transport.** *A mode of transport reserved for the upper echelons of Martian society.* Any model with the Mechanicum Faction allegiance and the Independent Character Sub-type may buy this model as a Dedicated Transport and embark on it before the game. Another unit may join it, counting as though the character had joined that unit, as long as it fits in the Transport Capacity and the character could join it.

**Paired Weapons.** *Some weapons must be used together to be fully effective.* A model armed with a weapon with this special rule gains +1 Attack.

**Praetorian Retinue.** *Some magos demand attendants of the highest quality and absolute obedience.* A Praetorian Servitor Maniple may only be included as a Retinue Squad, in a Detachment that includes a model with the Independent Character Sub-type and the Feudal Hierarchy or Command Node special rule. That model must be the Retinue Squad's Leader: it deploys with the Maniple and may not voluntarily leave it during play. A Maniple taken this way does not use a Force Organisation slot and counts as part of the same unit as its Leader.

**Prey-Snare (X).** *Barbed projectiles on unbreaking chains reel their victims in to their doom.* If a weapon with this special rule hits but does not remove the target model, both players roll a D6 and add Strength: the firer uses the weapon's Strength, and the target uses its unmodified Strength, or its highest Armour Value if it has no Strength. Re-roll ties. If the target loses, place it in base contact with the firing model by the shortest route; it counts as having been charged by the firing model in the following Assault phase. If the target wins, it breaks free. Buildings and Zooming Flyers are never affected. If a model is pulled out of coherency, its unit must move back into coherency with it as soon as it can, and the whole unit counts as locked in combat. If fired as an Overwatch Reaction and a model is snared, the rest of its unit may still charge, as a Disordered Charge with the snared model as the initial model. The qualifier limits what can be snared:

- **Majoris:** only affects a model whose starting Hull Points or Wounds are no more than double the firing model's.
- **Maxima:** may only target Vehicles or models with 8 or more Wounds, and has no effect on a model whose starting Hull Points or Wounds are more than double the firing model's.
- **Minoris:** cannot affect units with the Knight, Titan or Gargantuan Unit Type or Sub-type.

**Prototype Construct.** *Built on imperfect prototype technology, these engines can flare, jolt, explode or shut down for good.* A model with this special rule must take a Dangerous Terrain test whenever it Runs, but counts its Initiative as 2 higher for movement made by Running or as part of a Reaction.

**Rad-Saturation.** *Bathed in radiation, these Skitarii trust the Omnissiah to protect them.* An enemy unit locked in combat with one or more models with this special rule subtracts 1 from its Toughness, to a minimum of 1. This does not stack with rad grenades or Rad-Phage, but does affect Instant Death thresholds. In addition, attacks targeting a unit with this special rule have their Strength reduced by 1.

**Repulsor Grid.** *Atomantic shield matrices can rebound a perfectly angled shot straight back at its source.* Each time a model with this special rule passes an Invulnerable Save on a roll of 6, the unit that fired immediately suffers a hit with the profile of the weapon that fired. Weapons that do not roll To Hit cannot activate it.

**Sepketar Maniple.** *These malicious automata are hard to restrain and pursue prey despite their magos.* When deployed, at the start of the battle or arriving from Reserves, all models in the unit must be placed in unit coherency. Afterwards they operate independently and are not a single unit; each counts as its own unit for Victory Points, Units Destroyed and similar rules.

**Shieldbreaker.** *A warp-skip drive in these munitions lets them pierce shielding.* Invulnerable Saves may not be taken against Wounds or Hull Point damage from a weapon with this special rule. It also ignores Void Shields (X) and flare shields.

**Sonic Pulse.** *Like the sonic destructors of the Ordinatus Ulator, this pulse shakes even the strongest armour apart.* Place the Blast (3") marker touching the firing weapon's barrel. Instead of scattering it, move it in a straight line away from the firing model, within the weapon's 45° forward arc, until it reaches maximum range or leaves the battlefield. Every model it passes over suffers one automatic hit. Flyers cannot be hit. Against Knights, Titans, Super-heavy Vehicles, Buildings and Fortifications, the hit's Strength becomes 8.

**Tank Desant.** *Troops ride on this vehicle's armoured hull, guarding it as they go.* A model embarked on a model with this special rule may fire its ranged weapons as though it were not embarked, measuring range from the Transport's hull.

**Thermal Riders.** *These Skitarii will soar back into the sky at a moment's notice, only to plunge on their enemies later.* At the start of the controlling player's Movement phase, a unit with this special rule that is not locked in combat may be placed back into Reserves. If it is, it may make a Deep Strike Assault as normal in your next player turn.

**Unified Assault.** *Skitarii vehicles and aircraft share the cohorts' unity of mind.* During deployment, a unit may split itself across the Transport Capacity of a squadron with this special rule, as long as it does not exceed the squadron's total capacity. While split, the unit must keep coherency, ignoring rules that would let it separate, until it has fully disembarked. If one part of the unit disembarks, the rest must also disembark that phase, within coherency of each other. If one transport suffers Crash and Burn, the models inside it are destroyed; the rest disembark within coherency as normal. Models removed this way cause no Leadership tests.

**Zenith Protocol.** *Peak performance comes at the cost of neural burnout, so it is mostly reserved for expendable servitors.* While the Praetorian Retinue's Leader is alive, all Praetorian Servitors in the Maniple gain +1 Weapon Skill and +1 Ballistic Skill.

## The Orders of Techno-Arcana

Liber Ingenium adds Lesser Orders for Magos Primes and a new Higher Order for Archmagos Primes. No Order of either kind may be chosen more than once in an army, however many Detachments it has.

### The Orders of Lesser Techno-Arcana

*Where the Higher Orders command legions of automata or rebuild machines at a thought, the Lesser Orders focus on the individual magos' own power.*

Every Magos Prime and Magos Prime on Abeyant has The Orders of Lesser Techno-Arcana special rule, and must select one Lesser Order during army selection. No model may take more than one. Each Order grants special rules or wargear and may change the model's Unit Type, Sub-types or Characteristics, as its description says.

If your army includes more than one Magos Prime (with or without an Abeyant), each must take a different Lesser Order. A Magos Prime on Abeyant may only take Lachrimallion, Macroteknia or Ruinor.

#### Order of Lesser Techno-Arcana: Lachrimallion

*The lesser members of the Lacyraemarta ordo, thrall-drivers and whip-lords, knew no greater honour than 'encouraging' their Forge's workforce to ever greater acts of self-sacrifice.*

A model with this Order gains the Opus Viscera and Incorruptible Flesh special rules (*Liber Mechanicum*, page 99).

#### Order of Lesser Techno-Arcana: Macroteknia

*Apprentices to the true Macrotek forge-lords, sent to oversee work their masters deemed beneath them.*

A model with this Order gains the Rector Aedificii (*Liber Mechanicum*, page 100) and Machinatus Primus special rules.

**Machinatus Primus.** Before deployment, select one vehicle without the Knight or Titan Sub-type from the same Detachment. If it is part of a squadron, only one model gains the bonus, and it must be clearly marked. That vehicle gains +2 Movement and +1 Hull Points.

#### Order of Lesser Techno-Arcana: Malaghrae

*Scions of the order Malagra not yet fully ascended: assassins of machines and flesh, master of neither, and hungry for command.*

A model with this Order gains the Mech-Assassin special rule.

**Mech-Assassin.** The model gains Preferred Enemy (Characters), Precision Strikes (6+) and Monster Hunter. Its Weapon Skill becomes 5 and its Attacks become 3, and it is equipped with a prehensile dataspike. It may exchange one power weapon for a paragon blade for +20 points.

#### Order of Lesser Techno-Arcana: Myrmidae

*Masters of shot, shell and plasma, eager to destroy their foes in pursuit of field-test data.*

A model with this Order gains Hatred (Everything!), Bulky (3), Firing Protocols (3) and Precision Shots (6+), and its Wounds become 4.

#### Order of Lesser Techno-Arcana: Ruinor

*Minor priests of the Ordo Reductor who commanded no Taghmata of their own, but whose arts of destruction were much needed.*

A model with this Order gains Wrecker, Sunder and Secrets of Annihilation (*Liber Mechanicum*, page 101) and the Cybertheurgist Sub-type. Any unit it joins also gains Wrecker and Sunder while it remains in the unit.

### The Orders of Higher Techno-Arcana

An Archmagos Prime or Archmagos Prime on Abeyant may select an Order below instead of a Higher Order from elsewhere. No model may take more than one. Each Order grants special rules or wargear, may change the model's Unit Type, Sub-types or Characteristics, and may grant new options or special rules to its Detachment. Unless stated otherwise, those Detachment effects apply only to units in the same Detachment as the model. Unlike Warlord Traits, they persist if the model is removed from play.

#### Order of Higher Techno-Arcana: Skitariiax-Primus

*In rare, militant forge worlds, some Skitarii walked a learned path of Techno-Arcana, rising as Tech-Lords to the esteemed rank of Magos.*

Only an Archmagos Prime may select this Order. It gains the Skitariiax-Protectorate and Data Inviolate special rules.

**Data Inviolate.** When choosing Datasphere Uplink Protocols before the battle, select two different Protocols instead of one. You may activate both during the battle, but not in the same Game Turn.

**Skitariiax-Protectorate.** The Archmagos Prime gains Datasphere Uplink, Command Node, Feel No Pain (5+) and the Skitarii Sub-type. Its Detachment's Compulsory Troops choices must be Skitarii Ranger Cohorts or Skitarii Vanguard Cohorts. Triaros Armoured Conveyors and Tech-Priest Auxilia in the Detachment gain Datasphere Uplink.

## Warlord Traits

*As the Heresy ground on, Skitarii increasingly led the warhosts of the Mechanicum, presenting a new challenge to those who faced them.*

If your Warlord has the Skitarii Sub-type, it may choose one of the Legiones Skitarii Warlord Traits below instead of a Common Trait from the rulebook or a Mechanicum Warlord Trait.

**Warlord Trait: Accursed Scrapcode.** *Kelbor-Hal only.* The controlling player may give the Corrupted Sub-type, at no points cost during list building, to up to three units from the Warlord's Detachment made up entirely of Infantry, Monstrous or Automata models. The army also gains two additional Reactions in the opponent's turn, usable in any combination of phases, while the Warlord has not been removed as a casualty.

**Legiones Skitarii Warlord Trait: Encoded Hate.** *Engrams of utter fealty carry a flipside: an all-consuming hate for the enemy that spills into the Warlord's charges.* The Warlord and any unit it has joined gain Preferred Enemy and Counter-Attack (1). The army may make one additional Reaction in the opponent's Assault phase while the Warlord has not been removed as a casualty.

**Legiones Skitarii Warlord Trait: Full Frame Rebuild.** *A survivor of wounds that would have slain a lesser being, if at the cost of a little of themselves.* The Warlord has Eternal Warrior and It Will Not Die (6+). Its unit may not make Reactions in the Movement or Assault phases, but may make one additional Reaction in the opponent's Shooting phase. If the Warlord is removed as a casualty, the additional Reaction is lost and the unit may react normally from then on.

**Legiones Skitarii Warlord Trait: Uplink Override.** *The War-Leader activates protocols usually reserved for a commanding Magos.* While the Warlord is alive, all units in its Detachment with Datasphere Uplink gain Stubborn. The army may make one additional Reaction in either the opponent's Movement phase or their Shooting phase, not both, while the Warlord has not been removed as a casualty.

## Mechanicum Unit Sub-types

Liber Ingenium adds three Sub-types. They follow the rulebook's rules for Unit Types and Sub-types.

**Crawler.** *Ponderous war machines on great, non-bipedal legs that advance regardless of terrain.*

- May fire Heavy and Ordnance weapons and count as Stationary even if it moved in the preceding Movement phase.
- Is never slowed by Difficult Terrain and automatically passes Dangerous Terrain tests.

**Servitor.** *The bulk of the Mechanicum's labour force: simple, fearless soldiers without self-determination.*

- Successful Wounds from attacks with Poisoned or Fleshbane must be re-rolled against this model.
- Has Fearless.
- May fire all of its weapons in each Shooting Attack, including as part of a Reaction.
- May not use the Withdraw, Evade or Hold the Line Reactions.
- May fire Heavy weapons and count as Stationary even if it moved in the preceding Movement phase, and may declare Charges as normal whatever Shooting Attacks it made that turn.

**Skitarii.** *The standing forces of the Forge Worlds, called to the front lines during the Heresy.*

- Infantry have Feel No Pain (6+), unless they have a better version from another source.
- Vehicles have It Will Not Die (6+), unless they have a better version from another source.
- May re-roll Feel No Pain rolls against weapons with Poisoned (X) or Rad-Phage.
- Has Datasphere Uplink.
- A Detachment may not include more models with this Sub-type than without it, unless its Compulsory HQ choice has the Skitarii Sub-type.

These items work alongside the wargear in *Liber Mechanicum*.

### Auxiliary Systems (Ordinatus-Exemplar)

**Cascade Shield Projector.** The model is equipped with Void Shields (2) and a flare shield.

**Reinforced Superstructure.** The model gains Ferromantic Invulnerability (*Legacies of the Age of Darkness*, page 24), and its Hull Points and Armour Values each increase by 1.

**Self-Repair Systems.** The model gains It Will Not Die (5+).

**Supercharged Reactor.** Once per game, the model may fire all its weapons twice in the controlling player's Shooting phase, at the same target or two different ones, resolving the attacks one after the other. Afterwards it suffers D3 Hull Points of damage with no saves or Damage Mitigation rolls allowed, and may not fire any ranged weapons until the end of the controlling player's next Game Turn.

### Data-Tethers

**Broad-Spectrum Data-Tether.** Friendly models with Datasphere Uplink within 12" of the bearer add 1 to their Leadership, to a maximum of 10. This does not stack with any other data-tether.

**Enhanced Data-Tether.** All models in the bearer's unit add 1 to their Leadership, to a maximum of 10. This does not stack with any other data-tether.

### Electoos

**Corpuscarii Electoo.** All attacks made by the model gain Overload (6+).

**Fulgurite Electoo.** The model's melee attacks gain Disruption (6+). If a unit containing a model with this electoo destroys an enemy unit in close combat, including by Sweeping Advance, all of that unit's voltagheist fields give a 3+ Invulnerable Save for the rest of the battle. If several units with this electoo fight in the same combat, resolve each unit's attacks separately so you know which one earned the bonus.

### Energy Fields and Deflectors

**Atomantic Pulse Reactor.** The model has a 5+ Invulnerable Save. When it loses its last Wound or Hull Point, before it is removed or replaced with a Wreck, every model within 6+D6", friendly or enemy, suffers a hit at Strength 7+D3, AP4, measured from the model's hull. Every unit hit must take a Pinning test (only one per explosion).

**Conversion Field.** The bearer has a 4+ Invulnerable Save. At the end of a phase in which it passes one or more of these saves, every enemy unit within 6" must test as if hit by a Blind weapon. Friendly units are not affected.

**Emanatus Forcefield.** The bearer has a 6+ Invulnerable Save, improved by 1 for each other model with an emanatus forcefield within 5", to a maximum of 4+. A bearer with a Wounds Characteristic that suffers an unsaved Instant Death wound loses D3 Wounds instead of being removed. When the bearer loses its last Wound or Hull Point, before it is removed or replaced with a Wreck, every model within D6+2", friendly or enemy, suffers an automatic hit at Strength 8, AP -.

**Voltagheist Field.** The bearer has a 5+ Invulnerable Save.

### Motive Systems (Ordinatus-Exemplar)

**Crawler Systems.** The model gains the Crawler Sub-type and its Movement becomes 8.

**Hover Nacelles.** The model gains the Slow and Skimmer Sub-types.

**Track Units.** The model gains the Slow Sub-type.

### Sicarian Modules

**Aggression Stim-Injectors.** The model gains Furious Charge (1) and Rage (2).

**Stealth Suite Module.** The model gains Infiltrate, Pathfinder, Scout and Shrouded (5+).

### Serberys Modules

**Preysight Cogitators.** The model gains Precision Shots (5+) and Scout.

**Sulphurite Reservoirs.** The model gains Fleet (2) and Rad-Saturation, and is equipped with a sulphur ejector.

### Tarantula Deployment

**Concealed Positions.** The unit has Shrouded (4+) until it first fires any of its weapons.

**Drop Capsules.** The unit has Deep Strike.

**Forward Deployment.** The unit has Scout, and cannot be placed into Reserves.

### Other Wargear

**Battle-Automata Cradles.** The model may transport a single Castellax or Vorax Battle-Automata unit of no more than two models, even if it does not have the Transport Capacity for them or has rules that would otherwise prevent it.

**Battle Servitor Control.** The model gains Sunder.

**Hyperios Command Platform.** While it is on the table, the model gives Split Fire to all Hyperios air-defence launchers in its unit.

**Ironstrider Engine.** The model's Unit Type becomes Cavalry, keeping its Sub-types, and it gains the Skirmish Sub-type. Its Movement becomes 12, and it gains Firing Protocols (2), Fleet (2), Hammer of Wrath (1) and Prototype Construct. A model with a Wounds Characteristic that suffers an unsaved Instant Death wound loses 2 Wounds instead of being removed. When it loses its last Wound or Hull Point, before it is removed or replaced with a Wreck, every model within D6", friendly or enemy, suffers an automatic hit at Strength 7, AP -.

**Magno-Gravitic Thrusters (Remiare).** The model is not slowed by Difficult Terrain and automatically passes Dangerous Terrain tests.

**Mel-Cerebrae Striderframe.** The model's Movement becomes 9, and it gains Fleet (2) and the Light Sub-type.

**Omnispex.** A unit with at least one omnispex gains Night Vision. While the bearer is alive, Cover Saves against the unit's attacks are made at -1. This does not stack with a second omnispex in the unit.

**Personal Void (Koriel Zeth).** Koriel Zeth has a 3+ Invulnerable Save and ignores the first wound she suffers from a failed Invulnerable Save in each battle. She may also re-roll failed Characteristic tests other than Leadership tests, Psychic checks and Dangerous Terrain tests.

**Sapien (Arkhan Land).** Gives Arkhan Land all the benefits of a cyber-familiar. Once per turn he may also make one additional attack with the Sapien's Sting profile (see Named Character Weapons); no other attacks may be made with it.

**Scapuli-Pattern Thruster Pack.** At the start of the controlling player's Movement phase, or when deployed by Deep Strike Assault, the model may activate the pack: its Movement becomes 14 for that turn, with every benefit of that value, including the Charge bonus. While activated, it ignores terrain when moving and charging, but must take Dangerous Terrain tests when starting or ending a move in Dangerous Terrain. It may move over friendly and enemy models without penalty but must end at least 1" from models in other units.

**Teleportation Transponders.** The unit counts as having Deep Strike.
