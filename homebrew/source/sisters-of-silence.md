# Sisters of Silence Horus Heresy 2.0 - Homebrew Conversion

Oct 26, 2026 · @Isaac

## Introduction

This conversion adapts the Sisters of Silence section of Liber Imperatus v1.2 for Horus Heresy 2.0. It adds a special character, new Cadres, Rhino transports, aircraft and walkers, plus extra wargear options for existing units. It is used alongside the official Sisters of Silence army list, not instead of it.

The Silent Sisterhood were the Emperor's null-maidens, hunters of witches, mutants and the daemonic. As the Heresy spread, their Chambers took to the field in ever greater strength against the psychic horrors the traitors unleashed.

## Army-wide Special Rules

Every special rule unique to this expansion is defined here; datasheets list them by name. Core and official Sisters of Silence rules (Silent Sisterhood (X), Bitter Duty, Ex Oblivio and so on) are listed in the Rules Checklist.

### Abominant Aura

Traitor models within 6" of a unit containing a model with this Special Rule suffer -1 Initiative and -1 Leadership. This is cumulative with other effects. Stubborn does not ignore this penalty, but other rules that ignore Leadership penalties do.

### Armoured Doors

At the start of the Controlling Player's Movement phase, they decide whether the doors are open. If they are, the model gains Assault Vehicle and Open-Topped (4) until the start of their next Movement phase, when the doors close automatically.

### Burst (X)

Each Hit from a weapon with this Special Rule becomes X Hits.

### Gravitic Repulsion

A unit declaring a Charge against a model with this Special Rule suffers -2 to its Charge roll.

### Harbingers of Judgement

A unit that includes any models with this Special Rule may not join or be joined by any model without it. The unit never counts as a Scoring or Denial unit, whatever other rules apply, but its models may re-roll failed Shrouded Damage Mitigation rolls.

### Impale

A unit may re-roll Charge rolls against an enemy unit that it Hit with an Impale weapon earlier in the same player turn, even if no Wounds were caused. A unit that suffers Wounds from an Impale weapon has -1 Initiative until the end of the following Assault phase.

### Open-Topped (X)

Up to X models embarked on a Transport with this Special Rule may make a Shooting Attack in the Controlling Player's Shooting phase, measuring from the hull, and count as having moved. They may not use abilities that replace a Shooting Attack (such as Psychic Powers), and rules that let them count as stationary (such as Relentless) do not apply while embarked. They may target a different unit from the Transport, but Reactions to their shots target the Transport.

### Ponderous Aim

A weapon with this Special Rule cannot be fired as part of a Reaction or used to make Snap Shots.

### Psykana-Battery

Whenever a model suffers a Wound from Gets Hot while firing a weapon with this Special Rule, it must take a Leadership Check. If it fails, the model is slain, and a Blast (3") marker is placed as close as possible to its final position and treated as a Vortex marker.

### Repair

In any of the Controlling Player's Shooting phases, instead of shooting with this model, roll a D6. On a 4+, remove an Immobilised result it has suffered. No Hull Points are restored.

### Silence Descends

At the start of the battle, before deployment, a model with this Special Rule may be placed into Combat Air Patrol. It stays in Reserves but makes no Reserves rolls; instead its Controlling Player gains the Combat Air Patrol Advanced Reaction.

#### Advanced Reaction: Combat Air Patrol

- Trigger: an enemy Flyer Vehicle enters the battlefield from Reserves.
- Once that model has finished moving, nominate one model assigned to Combat Air Patrol. It enters play from any battlefield edge as if from Reserves.
- When it has finished moving, it may immediately make a Shooting Attack at the triggering model if it has line of sight.
- Only Flyer Vehicles may make this Reaction. It uses a point of the Reaction Allotment as normal.

### Skilled Rider

A model with this Special Rule may Run in the Movement phase and still fire one ranged weapon in the Shooting phase. These shots are made at BS2 and their To Hit rolls cannot be improved by any source. A unit that uses this Special Rule may not benefit from Firing Protocols (X) in the same Game Turn.

### Sudden Strike (X)

In a turn in which a model with this Special Rule Charges, it adds X to its Initiative until the end of the Assault phase. It gains no benefit after a Disordered Charge.

### Ungainly

A weapon with this Special Rule may only be used in a turn in which its bearer Charges (even a Disordered Charge), and gains no bonus attacks for Charging or from any Special Rule.

### Vigilant Covenant

A Vigilant Covenant is bought like any other unit and uses a Force Organisation slot. Before the first turn, each of its models must be assigned to a different unit from the same Detachment.

- Each may only join a unit made up entirely of Infantry models with the Silent Sisterhood (X) Special Rule, and not one that includes an Independent Character or Unique model (though such models may join its unit later as normal).
- No more than one may be assigned to any unit. Once assigned, it is part of that unit, may never leave it, and is removed if the unit is.
- No Victory Points are ever scored for removing it as a casualty.
- It gains the unit's Special Rules (except those that forbid it, such as Bitter Duty) and Unit Sub-types, but not its wargear options.

### Vortex

Once an attack with this Special Rule is resolved, leave its Blast markers in place for the rest of the battle as Vortex markers. Each is Area Terrain with these effects:

- No line of sight may be drawn into, out of or through it.
- A model that enters it, or begins its Controlling Player's Movement phase inside it, suffers one Str 10, AP 1 Hit with Instant Death and Armourbane (Melee). A Vehicle suffering a Penetrating Hit from this adds +3 to its Vehicle Damage roll.
- It grants no Cover Save, and models inside it may not use Cover Saves from any other source.

## Unit Sub-types

### Anathema

- A unit with at least one Anathema model may not be directly targeted by Psychic Powers, nor use Psychic Powers or Psychic Weapons. Powers that would affect it through area effects or scatter have no effect on it, but still affect other units.
- Wounds from Psychic Weapons (any weapon granted by a Psychic Discipline) automatically fail to Wound an Anathema model, and their special rules do not trigger. Force weapons gain no benefit from Force, Achean Force or Soul Tear against it. None of this protects a Transport carrying Anathema models unless the Transport is Anathema itself.
- Non-Anathema models in a unit with an Anathema model suffer -2 Leadership; Anathema models suffer -1. Any unit with a model within 6" of an Anathema model suffers -1 Leadership on its non-Anathema models, or -2 if it includes Corrupted, Psyker or Daemon models. Only the worst of these applies, but they stack with other modifiers such as Fear (X).
- Embarked units suffer no Anathema Leadership penalties. Stubborn does not ignore these penalties, but other rules that ignore Leadership penalties do.

### Sycophantica

- A unit with Sycophantica models may make Reactions, whatever its Unit Type, while it includes at least one Character.
- Sycophantica models may fire all their weapons in each Shooting Attack, including Reactions.
- They may fire Heavy and Ordnance weapons counting as Stationary after moving, and may Charge regardless of any Shooting Attacks made that turn.
- They ignore Initiative penalties for Charging into or through Difficult or Dangerous Terrain.

## Wargear

Wargear defined in Liber Imperatus. Other wargear uses its official rules; see the Rules Checklist.

### Alkonost Combat Bike

An Alkonost Combat Bike has a Twin-Linked Vratine Bolter.

- The model becomes Cavalry (Skirmish), keeping its other Sub-types, and has Movement 16.
- It gains Battle-Hardened (1), Firing Protocols (2), Hammer of Wrath (1), Shrouded (6+) and Skilled Rider.
- If it Runs, its Shrouded becomes (5+) until the start of its Controlling Player's next turn.

### Halian Heavy Jetbike

A Halian Heavy Jetbike has a Heavy Snare Cannon. The model gains Shrouded (6+) in any turn it moved, or Shrouded (5+) if it Ran.

### Rad Grenades

In a turn in which a unit with at least one model with Rad Grenades Charges or is Charged, models in the enemy unit(s) suffer -1 Toughness (minimum 1) until the end of the following Fight sub-phase. This affects Instant Death thresholds.

### Suspensor Web

A Heavy weapon with a Suspensor Web may be fired as an Assault weapon at targets within half its maximum range (e.g. a 36" Heavy 4 Heavy Bolter can fire as 18" Assault 4). Range-based Special Rules such as Armourbane (Melta) still use the weapon's original range.

### Vigilant Standard

- A unit with at least one model with a Vigilant Standard adds +1 to its Wounds total when working out who won a Combat in the Assault phase.
- Before rolling its Fall Back distance, the unit may choose to Fall Back D6" instead of 2D6", and stops 1" from the battlefield edge rather than leaving it.

### Vratine Chainmail

Vratine Chainmail gives a 4+ Armour Save.

### Vratine Voidplate

Vratine Voidplate gives a 2+ Armour Save. A unit with any models in Vratine Voidplate suffers -1 to Sweeping Advance rolls.
