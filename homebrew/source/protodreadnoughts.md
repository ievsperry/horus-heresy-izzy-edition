# Protodreadnoughts for The Horus Heresy 2.0 — Homebrew

2026-11-19 · @Isaac

## Introduction

Protodreadnoughts are hulking, piston-driven war-walkers from the Unification Wars and the early Great Crusade, piloted by living, heavily augmetic warriors rather than entombed veterans. This document adds three Imperial patterns for the Legiones Astartes, Mechanicum and Imperialis Militia, and three Leagues of Votann equivalents.

**All Protodreadnoughts** have the **Relic (2)** and **Support Squad** special rules. Legion Protodreadnoughts gain the **Legiones Astartes (X)** special rule. Votann Protodreadnoughts gain all of the Leagues of Votann army-wide special rules (**Eye of the Ancestors** and **League (X)**).

Points for the first model include the squadron; each further model costs the Squadron Expansion price.

## Special Rules

**Relic (X).** If a unit with this special rule is destroyed by any means, the opposing player immediately scores X additional Victory Points, in addition to any Victory Points normally scored for destroying it.

**Unstable Arc-Reactor.** No innate Invulnerable Save unless upgraded with Dreadnought Extra Armour (or Void-Hardened Extra Armour). When reduced to 0 Wounds it automatically Explodes (do not roll a D6): D6 Strength 8, AP – hits on all units within 6" before the model is removed.

**Unstable Dual-Core Reactor.** As Unstable Arc-Reactor, but the explosion is D6 Strength 9, AP 4 hits within 6".

**Unstable Darkstar-Core.** As Unstable Arc-Reactor, for Votann models (Void-Hardened Extra Armour).

**Clumsy Hydraulic Actuators / Heavy Hydraulic Pistons.** Subtract 1" from Run and Charge distance rolls (to a minimum of 1"); cannot make Sweeping Advances.

**Siege-Breaker Chassis / Subterranean Siege-Chassis.** +1 to all Armour Penetration rolls against Fortifications and Buildings in the Assault phase; Dangerous Terrain tests caused by ruined buildings or barricades are automatically passed.

**Recoil-Braced Firing Stance.** If the model remained stationary in its preceding Movement phase, it may re-roll To Hit rolls of 1 with its Primary Gun Arm and Missile Pod Arm in its Shooting phase.

## Wargear

**Dreadnought Extra Armour / Void-Hardened Extra Armour.** Grants a 2+ Armour Save and a 6+ Invulnerable Save against all shooting and melee attacks. Enemy attacks targeting this model do not benefit from To Wound re-rolls granted by Armourbane (X), and the value of any Brutal (X) special rule is reduced by 1 (to a minimum of 1).

**Helical Targeting Array / Pan Spectral Targeting Array.** Grants Skyfire and Interceptor to all weapons fired by this model if it remained stationary in its preceding Movement phase. (In the data the Imperial one is named "Protodreadnought Helical Targeting Array", to keep it apart from the official Helical Targeting Array.)

## Data notes

- The doc gives no Force Organisation slot; the Lucifer-Pattern and Ironkin Ancestor-Rig are Elites, the others Heavy Support.
- The Mechanicum swaps are priced from the weapon they replace: a Lucifer's Mauler Bolt Cannon is +10 from the fist (Gravis Bolt Cannon +5, then +5), its Kheres +30 and Darkfire +35 (from the Gravis Lascannon, +15). An Iron Warriors Shrapnel Cannon is priced as the Gravis Bolt Cannon it replaces.
- The Questoris Household upgrade is offered in Mechanicum and Questoris Household detachments; taking it moves the squadron to the Troops slot with the Line sub-type.
- Legion swaps are priced from the weapon they replace, as the Mechanicum ones are: a Lucifer's Graviton Shredder-Cannon is +20 from the fist (Gravis Bolt Cannon +5, then +15), its Obsidian Thermal-Lance and Helfrost Cannon +30 (from the Gravis Melta Cannon, +15), its Iliastus Assault Cannon +15, its Molecular Acid-Cannon +35; a Furibundus's Obsidian Thermal-Lance +40 (from the Cyclonic Melta Lance, +25), Twin-linked Iliastus +30, Molecular Acid-Cannon +40 (from the Heavy Conversion Beamer, +20); a Unification's are priced from each arm's own weapon. No swap costs less than 0: on the Missile Pod Arm, whose Cyclone Missile Launcher is the dearer weapon, the Gravis Bolt Cannon, Gravis Autocannon, Mauler Bolt Cannon and Shrapnel Cannon are free.
- The Carapace Phosphex Discharger is Iron Warriors only on every pattern; elsewhere the Furibundus's Carapace Weapon is the Havoc Launcher (or the Death Guard's Bile-Caster Mortar).
- The Furibundus, whose guns are Twin-linked, takes Twin-linked versions: the Twin-linked Graviton Shredder-Cannon for its Twin-linked Gravis Bolt Cannon or Autocannon, and the Twin-linked Helfrost Cannon for its Cyclonic Melta Lance (it has no Gravis Melta or Plasma Cannon). A twin-linked swap costs twice the single one, as the Twin-linked Iliastus Assault Cannon does: +40 and +55 from the Siege Claw.
- The Hexagrammaton Sub-type is a pick of its own on the squadron, required in a Dark Angels detachment; the wing-only upgrades and weapons show once the squadron has the matching wing.
- The doc's Asphyx Bolt-Cannon (36" S5 AP4 Heavy 4, Twin-Linked, Shred), Banestrike Bolt Cannon (24" S5 AP4 Heavy 4, Twin-Linked, Breaching (6+)), Iliastus-Pattern Assault Cannon, Mauler Bolt Cannon (36" S6 AP3) and Heavy Mining Laser (AP2, Armourbane (Ranged), Sunder) are the official weapons, with their official profiles (shown above). The Furibundus takes Twin-linked versions of the official bolt cannons and Heavy Mining Laser at twice the swap cost, as with the earlier Legions. The Warpfire Plasma Cannon is new: its plasma profile does not match the official Warpfire Cannon (36" S6 AP4 Heavy 1, Blast (3"), Breaching (4+), Pinning).
- A squadron takes at most one upgrade from its Legion's (or the Militia's) list, as with the earlier Legions; the Militia's Over-Clock shares that pick with the Provenance upgrades.
- Wherever an official weapon exists, the data uses it: the Siege Claw and Siege Drill are the Leviathan Siege Claw and Siege Drill, the Cyclone Missile System is the Cyclone Missile Launcher (with its Flak missile), the Furibundus's Twin-linked Gravis Bolt Cannon and Autocannon are the official Gravis Bolt Cannon and Autocannon (Twin-linked already), the Carapace Phosphex Discharger is the Phosphex Discharger, and the Shrapnel Cannon, Twin-Linked Shrapnel Cannon, Dragon's Breath Heavy Flamer, Iliastus Assault Cannons and Plasma Repeater are the official ones, with their official profiles (shown above). The Mech Library cannot link the Legiones Astartes library, so the weapons only that library has are exact copies (their rules with them; not the Hammer of Olympia reroll, which belongs to that Rite of War). Weapons with no official counterpart keep the profiles given here.
