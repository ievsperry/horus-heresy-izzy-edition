# Agents of the Warmaster: Corrupted Clade Assassins

Nov 14, 2026 · @Isaac

## Introduction

While the Regent of Terra unleashes the Officio Assassinorum from the shadows of the Imperial Palace, Horus Lupercal and the Dark Mechanicum of Xana-II and Sarum deploy their own twisted counterparts. Drawn from renegade temple-cells, warp-mutated flesh-vats, and heretek augury-shrines, these solitary killers hunt down Loyalist Praetors, Librarians, and commanders with profane efficiency.

A Detachment with the Traitor Allegiance may include 0–1 Corrupted Clade Assassin as an Elites choice, or up to 3 (no Clade twice) if the Detachment is an Army of Dark Compliance or uses The Coils of the Hydra (Alpha Legion) or The Black Reaving (Sons of Horus) Rite of War.

## Special Rules

### Agent of the Warmaster

A model with this special rule occupies an Elites Force Organisation slot (up to three in a Detachment that is an Army of Dark Compliance or uses The Coils of the Hydra or The Black Reaving Rite of War, with no duplicate Clades). An Agent of the Warmaster may never be selected as the army's Warlord, and may only join units composed entirely of models with the Traitor Allegiance.

### Ignores Invulnerable Saves

Invulnerable Saves may not be taken against wounds inflicted by a weapon with this special rule.

### Neural Agony

A weapon with this special rule rolls To Wound against the target's Leadership Characteristic instead of its Toughness (against non-Vehicle models only).

### Psyker-Bane

Wounds from a weapon with this special rule have the Instant Death special rule against models with the Psyker Unit Sub-type.

### Soul Blaze

At the end of each turn, roll a D6 for each enemy unit that suffered one or more unsaved wounds from a weapon with Soul Blaze that turn: on a 4+, the unit suffers D3 Strength 4, AP 5 hits with the Ignores Cover special rule.

## Wargear

### Alchemical Hazard-Weave

Confers a 4+ Armour Save and a 4+ Invulnerable Save.

### Blind Grenades

The bearer's unit counts as having defensive grenades: enemy units charging it do not gain the charge bonus to Initiative.

### Cyber-Familiar

The Operative may draw line of sight and measure range from any friendly Traitor model within 18".

### Inverted Etherium Shroud

Confers a 4+ Invulnerable Save.

### Psyk-Out Grenades

Enemy models with the Psyker Unit Sub-type charging or charged by the bearer's unit suffer -2 Initiative in that Assault phase.
