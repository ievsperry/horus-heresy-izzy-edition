# Saturnine Terminators and Dreadnought for The Horus Heresy 2.0 — Conversion

Oct 4, 2026 · @Isaac

## Introduction

The Saturnine Terminators, the Praetor in Saturnine Terminator Armour and the Saturnine Dreadnought were released for The Horus Heresy 3.0 in 2025, in the Age of Darkness: Saturnine box. This document converts them to 2.0 so they can be used alongside the official Legiones Astartes lists.

The conversion was built from Games Workshop's published previews and reviews, not the full 3.0 datasheets. The previews give the key traits: Saturnine Terminators move 5", strike at Initiative 3 with WS4 and 2 Attacks, and have 3 Wounds; the plasma bombard works like an indirect-fire plasma cannon; a Saturnine Praetor lets the squad Deep Strike with him; and the Dreadnought's thermal diffraction field weakens Las, Plasma, Melta and Flame weapons and protects it from its own plasma overload. Everything else is scaled to 2.0's characteristics, weapon rules and points.

**Conversion principles:**

- 3.0's Damage characteristic has no 2.0 equivalent. High-Damage weapons gain Rending, Sunder or Murderous Strike instead, and 3.0 Wounds are scaled down to 2.0 levels (3 Wounds becomes 2).
- 3.0 Overload becomes 2.0's Gets Hot.
- Weapons that ignore line of sight in 3.0 gain Barrage.
- Weapons with AP 1 or AP 2 use Rending rather than Breaching.
- Points are scaled to official 2.0 Terminator and Dreadnought costs; treat them as playtest values.

Rules marked † lean on exact 2.0 wording; the checklist at the end lists them.

## Saturnine Terminator Armour

**Saturnine Terminator Armour.** A model in Saturnine Terminator armour has a 2+ Armour Save and a 4+ Invulnerable Save, and adds 1 to its Toughness (already included in the profiles below). It gains the Heavy sub-type and the Bulky (3), Firing Protocols (2) and Slow and Purposeful special rules, and is immune to the Concussive special rule.

**Relic (X).** Ancient artifacts of irreplaceable cultural significance, standard-bearing icons of legendary crusades, or individual warriors carrying the heavy burden of a Legion's legacy—the loss of such prizes is a catastrophic blow to morale and a major triumph for the foe. If a unit with this Special Rule is destroyed by any means, the opposing player immediately scores a number of additional Victory Points equal to the value of X specified in brackets (e.g., Relic (2) scores 2 additional Victory Points). These Victory Points are awarded in addition to any Victory Points normally scored for destroying the unit (such as those gained from the Price of Failure, Slay the Warlord, or standard Mission-specific objectives). If multiple units with this Special Rule are destroyed during a single battle, the opposing player scores the additional Victory Points for each unit individually.

## Rules Checklist (†)

These entries rely on 2.0 wording that should be confirmed. Where your book differs, keep the intent and use the book's mechanic.

- [ ] Legiones Astartes (X), Master of the Legion, and how Terminators are transported and moved in 2.0 (Slow and Purposeful or its equivalent).
- [ ] Bulky (3), Firing Protocols (2) and Slow and Purposeful (Saturnine Terminator Armour).
- [ ] The Dreadnought unit type, and Dreadnought drop pods and hunter-killer missiles for Legion Dreadnoughts.
- [ ] Weapon traits such as Las, Plasma, Melta and Flame, and modifying the Strength of incoming attacks (Thermal Diffraction Field).
- [ ] Graviton, Barrage, Brutal, Unwieldy, Specialist Weapon and Concussive.

## Sources

The unit roles, movement, Initiative, Wounds, wargear names and abilities come from Games Workshop's published Warhammer Community previews of the Saturnine Terminators and Saturnine Dreadnought and from published reviews of the Saturnine box (2025). The full 3.0 datasheets were not available, so every profile and points value here is a conversion to 2.0 scale; treat them as playtest values. The Journal Tactica: The Forges of Saturn adds Centurions and Command Squads in Saturnine armour. The Centurion here takes its Legiones Consularis options from Liber Centura's Legion Saturnine Centurion; Command Squads are not converted.
