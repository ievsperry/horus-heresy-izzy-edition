# Chaos and Ruinstorm Fortifications for The Horus Heresy 2.0

Four Fortifications for the Traitor armies and the Daemons of the Ruinstorm, added to the game system (`2022 - Horus Heresy.gst`) beside its standard Fortifications by `homebrew/add_chaos_fortifications.py`.

## Introduction

**Available Factions.** Any army with the Traitor allegiance, and the Daemons of the Ruinstorm. Loyalist and Xenos armies may not take these Fortifications.

## Army-wide Special Rules

**Chaos Fortification.** A Chaos Fortification is deployed during the Fortifications deployment step within your Deployment Zone. It cannot move, declare Charges, make Reactions (unless granted by a specific special rule), control or deny Objectives, or take Morale or Pinning tests. Any automated weapon on it fires at BS 3, or uses the Ballistic Skill of an embarked model firing it manually.

### Sacrificial Circle — 105 pts

_A jagged, blood-stained rune-circle carved into the earth where dark pacts are sealed and warp-energy feeds nearby heretics. Also called a Warp-Altar. Inspired by the Dawn of War Sacrificial Circle._

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sacrificial Circle | – | 3 | 11 | 11 | 11 | 3 | – (Impassable) | Use model |

**Unit Composition**

- 1 Sacrificial Circle

**Unit Type**

- Fortification (Emplacement)

**Special Rules**

- Chaos Fortification
- Blood Tithe Aura: whenever an enemy unit is destroyed within 12" of a Sacrificial Circle, friendly Traitor Legiones Astartes or Ruinstorm Daemon units within 12" immediately gain +1 Attack in the following Assault phase
- Symbol of Chaos: friendly Character or Daemon models within 6" of the Sacrificial Circle re-roll failed To Hit rolls of 1 in melee
- Unholy Fortitude: models with the Bound or Ruinstorm Daemon special rule within 6" of this Fortification gain Feel No Pain (6+) (or improve an existing Feel No Pain roll by +1, to a maximum of 4+)

## Wargear

### Cursed Vox-Amplifier

A Fortification with a Cursed Vox-Amplifier has the Cursed Vox-Amplifier special rule: enemy units within 12" of it suffer a -1 penalty to their Leadership when taking Morale or Pinning tests.

### Daemonic Consecration Runes

A Sacrificial Circle with Daemonic Consecration Runes has a 5+ Invulnerable Save.

### Reinforced Armoured Plating

The Fortification's Front, Side and Rear Armour Values are 13.

### Warp-Screamer Horn

While a Daemon Pit with a Warp-Screamer Horn is on the battlefield, friendly Ruinstorm Daemon and Bound units may re-roll their Reserves rolls.
