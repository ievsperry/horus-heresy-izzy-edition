# Imperialis Militia Horus Heresy 2.0 - Homebrew Conversion

Oct 28, 2026 · @Isaac

## Introduction

This conversion adapts the Imperialis Militia section of Liber Imperatus v1.2 for Horus Heresy 2.0. It adds the Armies of Dark Compliance detachment, new and modified Provenances of War, new units and extra wargear options for existing units. It is used alongside the official Imperialis Militia army list in Legacies of the Age of Darkness, not instead of it.

The Militia were the mass levies of the Imperium's worlds, raised in their billions from every culture and circumstance. In the Heresy they fought for both sides, and on worlds taken by Dark Compliance they served as fodder for their Traitor Astartes masters.

## Army-wide Special Rules

Every special rule unique to this expansion is defined here; datasheets list them by name. Core and official Imperialis Militia rules (Among the Flock, Strength in Numbers, Muster of Worlds and so on) are listed in the Rules Checklist.

### Astra Telepathica

A model with this Special Rule and the Psyker Sub-type must select one Psychic Discipline: Divination, Telekinesis or Telepathy (Core Rulebook).

### Astropathic Communication

While a model with this Special Rule is on the battlefield (not in Reserves), the Controlling Player may re-roll all Reserve rolls.

### Auxiliary Vehicle Bay

A model with this Special Rule may carry one Vehicle with a starting Hull Points value of 4 or less. It Embarks and Disembarks as normal, from any point of this model's base (or hull, if it has no base).

### Bunker Drop

A model with this Special Rule starts the game carrying one Deployable Bunker, which uses no Force Organisation slot.

- The bunker is a Fortification (Building) with an Access Point on each side, Armour 12 on all facings, 4 Hull Points, Fire Points (Front, 6) and Transport Capacity 12. It may take two Emplacement Mounted Heavy Stubbers for free.
- Models may start Embarked in it, but neither the bunker nor its occupants may act or disembark until it is deployed.
- Whenever the Sky-Talon could let models Disembark, it may deploy the bunker instead, in base contact with itself and not within 1" of an enemy model, Objective Marker or Impassable Terrain. It then stays put for the rest of the game as a Fortification.
- If the Sky-Talon suffers Crash and Burn while carrying the bunker, resolve an extra Blast (3") marker using the Crash and Burn rules, then remove the Flyer, the bunker and anyone inside it.
- The bunker should be about the size of a 120x92mm base.

### Combat Transporter

If a model with this Special Rule is carrying a Vehicle or Dreadnought in its Auxiliary Vehicle Bay, it suffers -1 to its Countermeasures Saves.

### Defender

A unit hit during an Overwatch Reaction by a weapon with this Special Rule suffers -1 To Hit on its attacks in the following Assault phase.

### Engine Overcharge

When a unit with this Special Rule moves as part of a Reaction, it may move +6" further, but every model must then take a Dangerous Terrain test.

### Exposed Compartment

Add +1 to any Vehicle Damage table roll against a model with this Special Rule.

### Exposed Plasma Conduits

Each time a model with this Special Rule suffers a Penetrating Hit, add +1 to all its rolls on the Vehicle Damage table.

### Feeding Frenzy

At the end of an Initiative step in which models with this Special Rule slay enemy models in close combat, roll a D6 for each model slain. On a 6, a model with this rule in the attacking unit regains 1 Wound, up to its starting Wounds.

### Follow-Up Attack

Before the game, one Infantry unit without Bulky (X) from the same Detachment may be assigned to each Hades Drill as if Embarked on it, whatever its size.

- When the Drill arrives by Subterranean Assault, place a Blast (3") marker directly beneath it. The marker is Difficult Terrain for the rest of the game, and the Drill gains Shrouded (5+) for that turn.
- In any later Movement phase of its Controlling Player, the assigned unit may deploy as if disembarking, measuring from any point of the marker. It may move up to its Movement and may Run.
- It cannot be targeted by Interceptor in the first Movement phase it could deploy, and may still deploy if the Drill has been destroyed.
- If there is not enough room, it stays in Reserves and arrives next turn.

### Immobile

A model with this Special Rule may never move, Run, Charge, Fall Back or make Reactions that move it. If it would be forced to move, it is destroyed.

### Impale

A unit may re-roll Charge rolls against an enemy unit that it Hit with an Impale weapon earlier in the same player turn, even if no Wounds were caused. A unit that suffers Wounds from an Impale weapon has -1 Initiative until the end of the following Assault phase.

### Independent Fire Control

After a model with this Special Rule (or a unit made entirely of such models) resolves a Shooting Attack, any weapons that did not fire may make Secondary Shooting Attacks, all together or split between different targets. Each weapon fires only once, normal range and line of sight apply, and these attacks suffer -1 To Hit. Instead, in a turn it remained Stationary or moved at Combat Speed, it may make Shooting Attacks that Shooting phase as if it had the Super-Heavy Sub-type.

### Inspiring Rhetoric

A unit containing a model with this Special Rule rolls an extra dice for Morale checks and Pinning tests (not Psychic checks) and discards the highest result.

### "It's Dark In Dere!"

If a unit with this Special Rule takes a Dedicated Transport, a model without the rule (such as a Discipline Master) must be assigned to it before the game. While such a model is part of the unit, models with this rule may Embark on a Transport with Infantry Transport despite Bulky (X), which still counts against Transport Capacity.

### Militia Venetaris

When a unit with this Special Rule is deployed (at the start of the battle or from Reserves), all its models must be placed in unit coherency. After that, each model operates independently and counts as its own unit for Units Destroyed and similar rules.

### Mindless Aggression

A unit with this Special Rule must Charge in the Assault phase if possible (the Controlling Player picks the target), and must always attempt a Sweeping Advance if able.

### Open Crew Compartment

Hits against a Vehicle with this Special Rule in close combat, including from Death or Glory, are resolved against its lowest Armour facing.

### Open-Topped (X)

Up to X models embarked on a Transport with this Special Rule may make a Shooting Attack in the Controlling Player's Shooting phase, measuring from the hull, and count as having moved. They may not use abilities that replace a Shooting Attack (such as Psychic Powers), and rules that let them count as stationary (such as Relentless) do not apply while embarked. They may target a different unit from the Transport, but Reactions to their shots target the Transport. Add +1 to any Vehicle Damage table roll against the Transport.

### Overload (X)

A To Hit roll of X with a weapon with this Special Rule inflicts three automatic Hits instead of one. For Blast and Template weapons, every Hit counts as three Hits.

### Paired Weapons

A model armed with a weapon with this Special Rule gains an extra Attack.

### Ponderous Aim

A weapon with this Special Rule cannot be fired as part of a Reaction or used to make Snap Shots.

### Provenance (X)

A unit with this Special Rule may only be taken in a Detachment that has the Provenance of War named in brackets.

### Rapid Insertion

If a model with this Special Rule has moved at Cruising Speed or made a Zoom move, its passengers may still disembark: nominate any point it moved over that turn, place one model from the unit there, and scatter it.

- If it scatters, every model in the unit takes a Dangerous Terrain test.
- A unit split across several transports (Unified Assault) makes one scatter roll for the whole unit.
- Place the rest of the unit in coherency with that model. Any model that cannot be placed in coherency and at least 1" from enemy models is destroyed.

### Regenerating Horror

A model with this Special Rule that suffers an unsaved Wound with Instant Death is not removed outright; it loses 3 Wounds for each such Wound instead.

### Repair

In any of the Controlling Player's Shooting phases, instead of shooting with this model, roll a D6. On a 4+, remove an Immobilised result it has suffered. No Hull Points are restored.

### Resonance (X)

To Wound and Armour Penetration rolls of X or more with a weapon with this Special Rule are resolved at AP 1. Against Buildings and Fortifications with Hull Points, this triggers on an Armour Penetration roll of 2+.

### Restricted Trajectory

A weapon with this Special Rule may not be fired directly.

### Shoot Sharp and Scarper

Shooting Attacks made as Reactions in the Shooting phase against a unit made up entirely of models with this Special Rule are resolved as Snap Shots.

### Spotted!

A model with a weapon with this Special Rule gains Split Fire and ignores Night Fighting penalties when shooting, but enemy units ignore the 24" Night Fighting line of sight limit when shooting at its unit. Any unit hit by this weapon may be targeted by other units that Shooting phase ignoring the Night Fighting BS penalty and 24" limit.

### Sudden Strike (X)

In a turn in which a model with this Special Rule Charges, it adds X to its Initiative until the end of the Assault phase. It gains no benefit after a Disordered Charge.

### Techno-Arcana: Enginseer

While its unit contains any Servo-Automata, an Enginseer Adept's Battlesmith (X) improves to Battlesmith (4+).

### The Warmaster's Due

When a model with this Special Rule would get a Cover Save from intervening models that include Militia from the same Detachment, it may take +1 to that Cover Save (maximum 4+). If it does, once all saves are made, the intervening Militia unit closest to the firer along the line of fire suffers D3+1 Wounds with no saves or Damage Mitigation of any kind, allocated as if the enemy had shot it, to Militia models first. The bonus cannot be used against weapons that do not roll To Hit. A model with this rule may not join a unit with Expendable models, and Expendable models may not join a unit with this rule.

### Titanic Weight

A weapon with this Special Rule always strikes at Initiative 1, regardless of other modifiers.

### Traitor

A model with this Special Rule may only be included in an army with the Traitor Allegiance.

### Underground Advance

During deployment, a unit with this Special Rule may gain Subterranean Assault and be placed in Reserves.

### Unified Assault

During deployment, a unit may be split between the transports of a squadron with this Special Rule instead of one transport, up to their combined Transport Capacity.

- The squadron's models must stay in unit coherency (ignoring any rule that would separate them) until the passenger unit has disembarked from all of them.
- If any part of the passenger unit disembarks, all of it must disembark that phase, within coherency.
- If one transport suffers a Crash and Burn result, everyone inside it is destroyed; the rest of the unit must still disembark in coherency. No Leadership tests are caused by models removed this way.

### Unrestrained Power

A model with this Special Rule suffers D6 Wounds from Perils of the Warp instead of D3. The first time each Game Turn it makes a Psychic check, it may roll three dice and discard the highest. If it does, every later Psychic check that Game Turn rolls three dice and discards the lowest.

### Volatile Plasma Containment

If a model with this Special Rule loses its last Hull Point to Gets Hot, it also suffers an Explodes result. Whenever it suffers an Explodes result, the Hits it causes are Strength 10.

## Armies of Dark Compliance

*Worlds forced to the Warmaster's cause, garrisoned by mortal levies who served as fodder for their Traitor Astartes overlords.*

An Army of Dark Compliance Detachment is a Primary Detachment using the Crusade Force Organisation Chart, built from both the Legiones Astartes and Imperialis Militia army lists.

Effects

- All its units count as the same Faction for all Core and Special Rules, including wargear, transports and special rules.
- It may not take Legiones Astartes Rites of War, but an Imperialis Militia Force Commander may take Provenances of War as normal.
- Any unit with the Militia Sub-type and without Independent Character may be given Expendable; a unit with Expendable loses the Line Sub-type.
- All models with Legiones Astartes (X) gain The Warmaster's Due.

Limitations

- It may only be a Primary Detachment in an army using the Crusade Force Organisation Chart, and the army must be Traitor.
- All its Legiones Astartes units must share the same Legiones Astartes (X) variant, and the army may not include an Allied Detachment of that Faction. Otherwise it counts as the Imperialis Militia Sub-faction of the Imperial Army for the Allies Matrix.
- It may not take a Primarch, and the Warlord must be a model with Legiones Astartes (X).
- It may never have more units with Legiones Astartes (X) than units with the Militia or Third-Line Sub-types.
- Models with Legiones Hereticus (X) may not be taken.

## Provenances of War

These extra Provenances of War may be used by any Detachment that includes a model with the Muster of Worlds Special Rule.

### Provenance of War: Acolytes of the Saviour Emperor

*Desperate souls who clung to the belief that the Emperor was their divine saviour.*

- Only an army with the Loyalist Allegiance may take this Provenance.
- All eligible units are immune to Fear and gain Crusader and Hatred (Corrupted, Daemons).
- Every Compulsory Troops choice must have a Demagogue or Mounted Demagogue assigned before the game; each unit taken through Strength in Numbers needs its own.
- Imperialis Militia Discipline Master Cadres may not be taken.
- Legiones Astartes Detachments of any variant count this Detachment as one Alliance level lower.
- May not be combined with Mark of the Aeldari.

### Provenance of War: Knight World

*Household troops raised alongside a world's Knights and conditioned to obey their steel-clad masters.*

- If this is the Primary Detachment's Provenance, the army must include at least one Knight, in a Questoris Household, Questoris Crusader Host or Lord of War Detachment. A Knight in a Lord of War Detachment may take a Household Rank as if in a Questoris Household.
- Militia models gain +1 To Hit in their Shooting phase against an enemy unit already shot that phase by a Knight.
- The Detachment may take one Armiger Helverin Talon or Armiger Warglaive Talon as Heavy Support, which loses Armiger Talon.
- The Detachment and units with the Armiger Unit Type or Knight Sub-type count each other as Sworn Brothers.
- On an Aegis Militant chart, this Detachment unlocks one Knight as a Lord of War choice, as if it had the Mechanicum Faction.
- May not be combined with Abhuman Muster, Kinfolk Helots, Ogryn Conscripts or Ratling Enclave.

### Provenance of War: Mark of the Aeldari

*Human populations who believed the Aeldari their allies, or at least a source of curios.*

- Infantry Squads, Grenadier Squads and Command Cadres may exchange Lasguns for Lasblasters for free.
- Grenadier Squads and Command Cadres may exchange Lasguns for Shuriken Catapults (+20 points per unit) and Laspistols for Shuriken Pistols (+10 points per unit).
- A Force Commander may exchange a Laspistol for a Shuriken Pistol (free) or a Fusion Pistol (+10 points).
- Any model with Frag Grenades may exchange them for Plasma Grenades for free.
- The Detachment counts as Sworn Brothers with Aeldari Detachments of any Sub-faction. As an Allied Detachment it counts as Xenos Allegiance and may join an army whose Primary Detachment is any Aeldari Sub-faction.
- Legiones Astartes, Imperial Army, Agents of the Emperor and Agents of the Warmaster Detachments count it as By the Emperor's/Warmaster's Command, except Traders Militant, which count it as Sworn Brothers.
- May not be combined with Acolytes of the Saviour Emperor, Debased Rabble or Tainted Flesh.

### Provenance of War: Mining Colony

*Rugged miners who turned their tools into weapons of war.*

- Eligible units ignore the Night Fighting -1 BS and Leadership penalties.
- Grenadier Squads may take a Militia Hades Breaching Drill or a Goliath Heavy Vehicle with an Auxiliary Crew Bay as a Dedicated Transport; Command Cadres may take a Militia Hades Breaching Drill or Goliath Heavy Vehicle.
- Any all-Infantry unit without Monstrous or Mechanised may take up to two of these, in any combination (repeats allowed): Heavy Rock Saw (+5 points each), Heavy Rock Drill (+10 points each), Heavy Rock Cutter (+15 points each).
- Any all-Infantry unit may exchange a Heavy Stubber for a Seismic Cannon (+10 points each) or a Mining Laser (+15 points each).
- Any all-Infantry unit (Mechanised included) may exchange a Lascannon for a Heavy Seismic Cannon or Heavy Mining Laser for free.

### Provenance of War: Ratling Enclave

*Abhuman marksmen pressed into service from Ratling homeworlds.*

- Infantry and Cavalry models gain +1 BS and +1 Initiative but suffer -1 Movement, WS, Strength and Toughness (minimum 1). Models with Movement 0 or - are unaffected, as are Ratling Squads.
- Imperialis Militia Ratling Squads may be taken as Troops.
- Force Commanders may take a Longlas or Sniper Rifle for +10 points.
- Legiones Astartes and Solar Auxilia Detachments count this Detachment as Distrusted Allies.
- May not be combined with Kinfolk Helots, Knight World or Ogryn Conscripts.

### Provenance of War: Trailblazers

*Light infantry, sharpshooters and rangers from overgrown or sparsely peopled worlds.*

- Reconnaissance Squads lose Support Squad, gain the Line Sub-type, and gain +1 BS (maximum 4).
- Any number of models in Command Cadres and Grenadier Squads may exchange their Lasgun for a Longlas or Sniper Rifle for +10 points each.
- May not be combined with Debased Rabble or Voidborne Raiders.

### Provenance of War: Voidborne Raiders

*Boarding specialists from system-spanning militias or outright pirates.*

- All Infantry gain the Heavy Sub-type and lose Light.
- A Force Commander not upgraded to a Mounted Force Commander may take a Boarding Shield for free; any other Infantry Character may take one for +5 points.
- Infantry in Command Cadres and Grenadier Squads may take Boarding Shields for +25 points per unit.
- Infantry Squads may take Combat Shields for +20 points per unit.

## Wargear

Wargear defined in Liber Imperatus. Other wargear (Flak Armour, Carapace Armour, Militia Cavalry Mount, Militia Jet Pack and so on) uses its official rules; see the Rules Checklist.

### Auxiliary Crew Bay

A model with an Auxiliary Crew Bay gains the Transport Sub-type, Transport Capacity 14 (or has its capacity raised to 14), Access Points on both sides and the rear if it lacked them, and Infantry Transport.

### Boarding Shield

A Boarding Shield gives a 5+ Invulnerable Save. It does not stack with other Invulnerable Saves (choose one) but can be improved by rules such as Cyber-Familiar. The model cannot gain bonus attacks for two melee weapons or attack with Two-Handed weapons in the Assault phase.

### Combat Shield

A Combat Shield gives a 6+ Invulnerable Save. It does not stack with other Invulnerable Saves (choose one) but can be improved by rules such as Cyber-Familiar.

### Command Crew Bay

A model with a Command Crew Bay gains the Transport Sub-type, Transport Capacity 8 (or has its capacity raised to 8), Access Points on both sides if it lacked them, and Infantry Transport.

### Cortex Controller

Units including Cybernetica models ignore Programmed Behaviour while within 12" of a friendly model with a Cortex Controller.

### Exo-Driller Armour

Exo-Driller Armour gives a 2+ Armour Save and a 5+ Invulnerable Save.

### Helical Targeting Array

The Controlling Player may activate it at the start of any of their turns, or before the opponent's first turn if they are not going first. Until the start of their next turn:

- The model may not move, Run, or make Reactions other than Interceptor or Overwatch.
- Its Shooting Attacks may use Skyfire.
- Shrouded Damage Mitigation rolls may not be taken against its attacks.
- Its Interceptor Reactions cost no Reaction point; this may exceed three Reactions in a phase but not one per phase for the unit.
- A Vehicle with it may fire all its weapons, not only Defensive Weapons, when making Interceptor.

### Power Pack

Available through the Kinfolk Helots Provenance of War to any model with the Infantry Unit Type and Character Sub-type, at no cost. A model with a Power Pack may take up to two different weapons from: Circular Stone Saw (+5 points), Gem Extractor (+10 points), Kinfolk Arc Welder (+15 points), Stone Burner (+10 points). It has no other effect.

### Sawtooth Rockgrinder

A model with a Sawtooth Rockgrinder gains +1 Front Armour (maximum 14), suffers -2" Movement, and ignores Difficult and Dangerous Terrain. Its Ram attacks inflict +D3 Hits at +3 Strength, AP 3, with Shred and Sunder.

### Shredder Prow

A model with a Shredder Prow inflicts +D3 Hammer of Wrath Hits, and its unit's Hammer of Wrath attacks are AP 3.

### Shroud Bombs

Shooting Attacks against a unit with at least one model with Shroud Bombs count the range as 6" further than it is. Enemy models with Night Vision and Primarchs ignore this. Barrage weapons always scatter against the unit as if it were out of line of sight.

### Towing Cable

In its Movement phase, a model with a Towing Cable may tow a friendly Infantry model with Movement "-" that is in contact with its hull and not locked in combat.

- Both may move up to the towing model's remaining Movement, staying in contact the whole way. A move that would remove the towed model as a casualty may not be made.
- Other models in the towed model's unit with Movement 1 or more count as Embarked on the vehicle during the tow, and must disembark normally once it finishes moving.
