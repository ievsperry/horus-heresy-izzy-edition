# Traders Militant Horus Heresy 2.0 - Homebrew Conversion

Oct 26, 2026 · @Isaac

## Introduction

The Traders Militant are Rogue Traders and their entourages: nobles, exiles and rivals of the Emperor sent beyond the Imperium's borders under a Warrant of Trade, the only humans permitted to treat with xenos. When the Heresy came, some tore up their Warrants and turned reaver; others led their fleets back towards Terra.

This conversion adapts the Traders Militant section of Liber Imperatus v1.2 for Horus Heresy 2.0. It uses the Aegis Militant Force Organisation Chart below instead of the Crusade chart.

### Aegis Militant Detachments

- An Aegis Militant Detachment replaces the Crusade Force Organisation Chart and its Detachments, and counts as your Primary Detachment for all Special Rules, Limitations and the Allies Matrix.
- For the Allies Matrix, Traders Militant are Agents of the Emperor if Loyalist, or Agents of the Warmaster if Traitor. All models from this list have the Traders Militant Sub-faction.
- A Detachment with the Traders Militant Sub-faction may only be the Primary Detachment through this chart, and this chart may only be used when that Detachment is the Primary Detachment.
- The army may include one Allied Detachment for every 1,000 points (e.g. three at 3,000 points). Any Detachment that explicitly replaces an Allied Detachment may take one of those places.
- Allied Detachments must each be a different Faction or Sub-faction, and must match the army's Allegiance. Warrants of Trade can change both limits; Fragile Alliance changes the Levels of Alliance.
- If the Rogue Trader Militant is removed from play, use the lowest Level of Alliance for any Factions whose level depends on which Detachment is Primary.
- No Optional Detachments may be taken other than a single Lord of War Detachment. Its units must share a Faction or Sub-faction with one of the army's Primary or Allied Detachments.
- The Aegis Militant Detachment may only include units from this document. These units may only otherwise be taken in an Allied Detachment or a Zone Mortalis Engagement Force Organisation Chart; if so, the Rogue Trader Militant may not select a Warrant of Trade, and any Traders Militant unit that can be an Elites choice may also be taken as a Troops choice.
- An Aegis Militant Detachment may include one unit from the Knights Errant, Divisio Assassinorum or Cults Abominatio lists as a non-Compulsory Elites choice, even though these are normally HQ choices.

## Army-wide Special Rules

Every special rule unique to this army is defined here; datasheets list them by name. Core Horus Heresy 2.0 rules are listed in the Rules Checklist.

### Astra Telepathica

A model with this Special Rule and the Psyker Sub-type must select one Psychic Discipline: Divination, Telekinesis or Telepathy (Core Rulebook).

### Astropathic Communication

While a model with this Special Rule is on the battlefield (not in Reserves), the Controlling Player may re-roll all Reserve rolls.

### Dimensional Edge (X)

A weapon with this Special Rule only ever Wounds on the number in brackets. No Saves or Damage Mitigation rolls of any kind may be taken against Wounds it causes.

### Embrace of Death (X)

In a turn in which a model with a weapon with this Special Rule Charges or is Charged, it may make the number of attacks in brackets with that weapon at Initiative step 10, but may make no other attacks with it. This grants no extra Pile In move, may be used even after a Disordered Charge, and the weapon may not be used in later turns of the same combat.

### Faceless Mask

A model with this Special Rule may be included in a Detachment that is not a Saedathii Detachment, despite the normal restriction of the Rillietann Sub-type.

### Fragile Alliance

While a model with this Special Rule is the army's Warlord and has not been removed as a casualty, all models in its army count their Level of Alliance as Fellow Warriors, unless they are already Sworn Brothers.

### Guardian Retainers

A Retainers Militant unit may only be selected in a Detachment that includes at least one model with the Warrants of Trade Special Rule. It is a Retinue Squad, and that model is its Leader (if there are several, the Controlling Player picks one).

- The Retinue Squad does not use a Force Organisation slot and is part of the same unit as its Leader.
- It must be deployed with its Leader as part of the unit, and the Leader may not voluntarily leave it.
- A Retainers Militant unit may not be selected in an army without a Leader.

### Heralds of the Advance

A unit that includes any models with this Special Rule may not join or be joined by any model without it. The unit never counts as a Scoring or Denial unit, whatever other rules apply, but its models may re-roll failed Shrouded Damage Mitigation rolls.

### Honour-Bound, Honour-Defined

If possible, a unit that includes a model with this Special Rule must issue a Challenge when Engaged in combat, and a model with this Special Rule must accept any Challenge issued to its unit. In any Assault phase where this model begins the Fight sub-phase in a Challenge, or enters one, it and all friendly models in the same combat gain Fearless until the end of that Assault phase.

### Inexorable

A unit made up only of models with this Special Rule ignores Leadership modifiers on Morale checks and Pinning tests, except those from Fear (X) and the Corrupted and Anathema Sub-types. A unit with both Fearless and Inexorable uses Fearless.

### Makeshift Weapon

A model attacking with a weapon with this Special Rule makes only a single attack with it, at Initiative step 1, and may not gain bonus attacks for Charging or from any Special Rule.

### Navigis Astrologica

A model with this Special Rule and the Psyker Sub-type automatically knows the Thaumaturgy (Core Rulebook) and Navis Astrologis Psychic Disciplines.

### Null-Soul

A model with this Special Rule may not be in a unit containing any Psyker models. It does not apply the Anathema Sub-type's Leadership penalties to a unit it joins that does not have the Anathema Sub-type.

### Ponderous Aim

A weapon with this Special Rule cannot be fired as part of a Reaction or used to make Snap Shots.

### Psykana Militis

A model with this Special Rule and the Psyker Sub-type must select one Psychic Discipline: Anathemata, Biomancy, Divination, Pyromancy or Telekinesis (Core Rulebook).

### Representative of the Legion

A model with this Special Rule must have the same Legiones Astartes (X) variant as the Detachment that allowed it to be selected. It may select any Legion-specific options that variant permits, counting as having the Character Sub-type to do so, and is affected by those rules even though it is not in a unit made up entirely of models with that Special Rule.

### Severax Talon

Before a unit with this Special Rule is deployed (at the start of the battle or from Reserves), split it into two units as evenly as possible. Each is deployed separately, following all normal placement and coherency rules, and from then on counts as its own unit for Victory Points, Units Destroyed and similar rules.

### Skilled Rider

A model with this Special Rule may Run in the Movement phase and still fire one ranged weapon in the Shooting phase. These shots are made at BS2 and their To Hit rolls cannot be improved by any source. A unit that uses this Special Rule may not benefit from Firing Protocols (X) in the same Game Turn.

### Sonic Pulse

Place the Blast (3") marker touching the firing weapon's barrel. Instead of scattering it, move it in a straight line away from the firer, in any direction within the weapon's 45° forward arc, until it reaches maximum range or leaves the battlefield. Every model it passes over suffers one automatic Hit. Flyers cannot be hit. Against models with the Knight, Titan, Super-Heavy Vehicle, Building or Fortification Unit Type, the Hit is Strength 8.

### Sworn to Serve

A model with this Special Rule may only be selected as part of a Retainers Militant unit, by paying the cost shown in that unit's options.

### Vortex

Once an attack with this Special Rule is resolved, leave its Blast markers in place for the rest of the battle as Vortex markers. Each is Area Terrain with these effects:

- No line of sight may be drawn into, out of or through it.
- A model that enters it, or begins its Controlling Player's Movement phase inside it, suffers one Str 10, AP 1 Hit with Instant Death and Armourbane (Melee). A Vehicle suffering a Penetrating Hit from this adds +3 to its Vehicle Damage roll.
- It grants no Cover Save, and models inside it may not use Cover Saves from any other source.

### Warrants of Trade

During Army Selection, a model with this Special Rule must select one Warrant of Trade below. A model may never select more than one, and a model with this Special Rule in an Allied Detachment may not select a Warrant.

#### Warrant of Compliance

*The most common Warrant, issued in bulk to legitimise short, brutal campaigns of subjugation under the close watch of a patron.*

Effects

- A Loyalist army may include one Allied Detachment with the Agents of the Emperor Faction.
- A Traitor army may include one Allied Detachment with the Agents of the Warmaster Faction.

Limitations

- The army may not include any Detachments from a Faction that may select the Xenos Allegiance.
- No more than one Agents of the Emperor or Agents of the Warmaster Allied Detachment may be included.

#### Warrant of Conquest

*Granted to single-minded, bloody-handed Traders who would reclaim systems at any cost, even with xenos aid.*

Effects

- The army may include one Detachment from a Faction that may select the Xenos Allegiance.

Limitations

- No more than one Xenos Detachment may be included.
- The army may not include any Allied or Optional Detachments from the Agents of the Emperor or Agents of the Warmaster Factions.

#### Warrant of Exploration

*Carried by those who ranged ahead of the fleets and fraternised with human strays and xenos alike, to the Legions' disgust.*

Effects

- The army may include up to two Detachments from the Mechanicum Faction.

Limitations

- The army must include one Detachment from a Faction that may select the Xenos Allegiance, and no more than one.
- The army may include only one Detachment from a Faction containing models with the Legiones Astartes (X) Special Rule.
- No more than two Mechanicum Detachments may be included.
- The Detachment may not include any Allied or Optional Detachments from the Agents of the Emperor or Agents of the Warmaster Factions.

#### Warrant-Breaker

*Those who broke the terms of their Warrant found it voided, and were hunted by loyalist and traitor alike.*

Effects

- The same Faction or Sub-faction may be selected for more than one Allied or Optional Detachment (up to twice each), overriding any limit on taking more than one Faction or Sub-faction that may select the Xenos Allegiance.

Limitations

- The army may only include Allied or Optional Detachments from Factions that may select the Xenos Allegiance, or from the Imperial Army Faction.
- No Faction or Sub-faction may be selected more than twice.
- All enemy models gain Hatred (Traders Militant) when fighting this army.

### Weaponsmith's Ingenuity

Once per game turn, at the start of the Controlling Player's Shooting phase, the Controlling Player may roll a D6 on the table below. The result applies to all models in the unit with the Traders Militant Sub-faction until the start of the Controlling Player's next Shooting phase.

| D6 Result | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| 1 – Oops! | Weapons of all affected models gain Gets Hot; weapons that already have it trigger it on To Hit rolls of 1 or 2. |
| 2 – Aesthetic Modifications | No effect. |
| 3 – Rangefinder Calibration | All ranged weapons in the unit gain +12" Range. |
| 4 – Armour Repairs | Affected models may re-roll Armour Saves of 1. |
| 5 – Cooling Coils | Affected models' ranged weapons fire one extra shot (e.g. Assault 2 becomes Assault 3) and ignore Gets Hot. |
| 6 – Complete Overhaul | Choose two different results from this table and apply both. |

## Psychic Disciplines

### Psychic Discipline: Anathemata

A Loyalist Psyker with this Discipline gains Seal the Veil and Void Darts. A Traitor Psyker gains Breach the Veil and Void Darts. A Psyker of either Allegiance also gains the Aetheric Lightning Psychic Weapon (Core Rulebook), unless it already has it from another source.

#### Breach the Veil (Psychic Power)

*The Psyker calls to the horrors beyond the void and invites them into the materium.*

Instead of making a Shooting Attack, the Psyker may pick a point within 12" and at least 3" from any enemy model or Impassable Terrain, and place a Blast (3") marker there as the Warp Rift.

- The Controlling Player may make a Psychic check. If it is passed, the Warp Rift does not scatter.
- If it is failed or not taken, the point scatters. If it lands on Impassable Terrain, within 3" of an enemy model or off the battlefield, the Psyker suffers Perils of the Warp and the point is moved the minimum distance needed to be clear of all three, in a direction of the Controlling Player's choice.
- The Controlling Player may then bring up to one Ruinstorm Daemon unit in Reserves onto the battlefield from any point on the edge of the marker, as if from Reserves, treating the marker as their battlefield edge. The marker is then removed.
- That unit may be targeted by the Interceptor Reaction, and may shoot and declare a Charge in the turn it arrives.
- Using this power requires access to the Ruinstorm Daemons Army List.

#### Seal the Veil (Psychic Power)

*By will or by prayer, the Psyker compels reality to knit itself back together.*

Instead of making a Shooting Attack, the Psyker may target one enemy unit made up entirely of models with the Daemon Unit Type or Corrupted Sub-type, in line of sight and with at least one model within 18". All models in the target unit suffer -1 Strength and -1 Toughness (minimum 1) until the end of its Controlling Player's next turn. The Psyker's Controlling Player may then make a Psychic check: if passed, the target unit also suffers Perils of the Warp; if failed, both the target unit and the Psyker suffer Perils of the Warp.

#### Void Darts (Psychic Weapon)

*The Psyker hurls shards of unreality that wound the material and immaterial alike.*

| Void Darts | 18" | 5 | 4 | Assault 12, Deflagrate, Psychic Focus, Sanctic |

## Unit Sub-types

### Anathema

- A unit with at least one Anathema model may not be directly targeted by Psychic Powers, nor use Psychic Powers or Psychic Weapons. Powers that would affect it through area effects or scatter have no effect on it, but still affect other units.
- Wounds from Psychic Weapons (any weapon granted by a Psychic Discipline) automatically fail to Wound an Anathema model, and their special rules do not trigger. Force weapons gain no benefit from Force, Achean Force or Soul Tear against it. None of this protects a Transport carrying Anathema models unless the Transport is Anathema itself.
- Non-Anathema models in a unit with an Anathema model suffer -2 Leadership; Anathema models suffer -1. Any unit with a model within 6" of an Anathema model suffers -1 Leadership on its non-Anathema models, or -2 if it includes Corrupted, Psyker or Daemon models. Only the worst of these applies, but they stack with other modifiers such as Fear (X).
- Embarked units suffer no Anathema Leadership penalties. Stubborn does not ignore these penalties, but other rules that ignore Leadership penalties do.

### Asuryani

- Models with this Sub-type have Legacies of the Fall.
- Infantry and Cavalry models with this Sub-type have Battle Focus.

### Guardian

- Units with Guardian models may Embark on Transports, Buildings and Fortifications as if they were Infantry.
- They may be joined by friendly Characters and Independent Characters, and may then make Reactions even if their Unit Type normally could not.
- In a unit with Guardian and Character models, Wounds that would go to a Character (including from Precision Strikes (X) or Sniper) may be allocated to a Guardian model first.
- Unless joined by a friendly Character, Guardian models suffer -2 Movement, may not Run, and have Initiative 1.

### Rillietann

- Models with this Sub-type have Chosen Warriors, Fear (1), Furious Charge (1), Hit & Run and Move Through Cover.
- Models with this Sub-type may only be taken in a Saedathii Detachment (see Faceless Mask).

### Arkadia Pattern Grav-Attack

An Arkadia Pattern Grav-Attack has two Heavy Bolters. A model with one that Runs gains Shrouded (5+) until the start of its Controlling Player's next turn.

### Boarding Shield

A Boarding Shield gives a 5+ Invulnerable Save. It does not stack with other Invulnerable Saves (choose one) but can be improved by rules such as Cyber-Familiar. The model cannot gain bonus attacks for two melee weapons or attack with Two-Handed weapons in the Assault phase.

### Cortex Controller

Units including Cybernetica models ignore Programmed Behaviour while within 12" of a friendly model with a Cortex Controller.

### Explorator Augury Web

At the start of each of its Controlling Player's turns, choose a mode that lasts until their next turn:

- Disruption: enemy Reserves rolls suffer -1. This does not stack with other negative modifiers (use the highest), but positive modifiers can still offset it.
- Relay: the Controlling Player may re-roll Reserves rolls.

### Flip Belt

A unit made up entirely of models with Flip Belts may ignore terrain and intervening friendly units when moving, including Charges, Fall Backs, Advancing and Reactions. It ignores Difficult Terrain but still tests for Dangerous Terrain it begins or ends in.

### Heavy Mesh Armour

Heavy Mesh Armour gives a 4+ Armour Save.

### Indomitus Terminator Armour

Indomitus Terminator Armour gives a 2+ Armour Save and a 5+ Invulnerable Save.

### Jokaero Armaments

Whenever the model makes a Shooting Attack, choose one weapon to make it with: Disintegrator, Heavy Flamer, Lascannon, Multi-Melta or Volkite Culverin.

### Mechanicum Protectiva

A Mechanicum Protectiva gives a 4+ Invulnerable Save. It does not stack with other Invulnerable Saves (choose one) but can be improved by rules such as Cyber-Familiar.

### Plasma Grenades

A unit with at least one model with Plasma Grenades attacks at its normal Initiative after a successful Charge through Difficult or Dangerous Terrain, though Charge roll penalties still apply. Instead of attacking normally, a model in base contact with a Building, Fortification, Vehicle, Dreadnought or Automata may make one automatic Str 5, AP 4 Hit with Breaching (6+) at Initiative step 1; its unit then Piles In at Initiative 1, and that model may make no other attacks that phase (but may Sweeping Advance).

### Power Armour

Power Armour gives a 3+ Armour Save.

### Praesidium Shield

A Praesidium Shield adds +1 to the model's Invulnerable Save (to a maximum of 3+), or gives a 6+ Invulnerable Save if it has none. The model cannot gain bonus attacks for two melee weapons or attack with Two-Handed weapons in the Assault phase.

### Proteus Pattern Storm Shield

A Proteus Pattern Storm Shield gives a 4+ Invulnerable Save that does not stack with others (choose one) and cannot be modified. The model may never gain an extra Attack for two close combat weapons or attack with Two-Handed weapons.

### Rad Furnace

Melee Hits allocated to models in combat with a unit that includes a Rad Furnace Wound on one lower result than normal (minimum 2+); this is not cumulative. Models with a Rad Furnace are immune to Rad Grenades, Rad-Phage and enemy Rad Furnaces, and Rad-Phage Hits only Wound them on a 6+.

### Rad Grenades

In a turn in which a unit with at least one model with Rad Grenades Charges or is Charged, models in the enemy unit(s) suffer -1 Toughness (minimum 1) until the end of the following Fight sub-phase. This affects Instant Death thresholds.

### Suspensor Web

A Heavy weapon with a Suspensor Web may be fired as an Assault weapon at targets within half its maximum range (e.g. a 36" Heavy 4 Heavy Bolter can fire as 18" Assault 4). Range-based Special Rules such as Armourbane (Melta) still use the weapon's original range.

### Trader-Militant Banner

- A unit with at least one model with this banner adds +1 to its Wounds total when working out who won a Combat in the Assault phase.
- The unit may Fall Back D6" instead of 2D6", and stops 1" from the battlefield edge rather than leaving it.
- In an army whose Primary Detachment has the Traders Militant Sub-faction, the Controlling Player's units within 12" of the banner count as Leadership 9 (unless higher) for Morale checks and Pinning tests, but not Psychic checks.
