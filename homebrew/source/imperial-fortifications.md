# Imperial and Traitor Fortifications for The Horus Heresy 2.0

Four Fortifications for the Imperial and Traitor armies, added to the game system (`2022 - Horus Heresy.gst`) beside its standard Fortifications by `homebrew/add_imperial_fortifications.py`.

## Introduction

**Available Factions.** Legiones Astartes (all eighteen Legions, Blackshields and Shattered Legions), Solar Auxilia, Mechanicum (Taghmata Omnissiah, Cybernetica, Ordo Reductor and Dark Mechanicum), Imperialis Militia (including Cults and Renegade Militia), Legio Custodes, Sisters of Silence, Questoris Familia and Collegia Titanica, and the homebrew Legiones Hereticus, Legiones Inductii, Grey Knights, Adepta Sororitas, Astra Militarum and Traders Militant. The Divisio Assassinorum are taken as Allied Detachments and agents and take no Fortifications. Xenos armies, the Daemons of the Ruinstorm, Necrons, Leagues of Votann and Rillietann may not take these Fortifications.

## Army-wide Special Rules

**Imperial Fortification.** An Imperial Fortification is deployed during the Fortifications deployment step within your Deployment Zone. It cannot move, declare Charges, make Reactions (unless granted by a specific special rule), control or deny Objectives, or take Morale or Pinning tests. Any automated weapon on it fires at BS 3, or uses the Ballistic Skill of an embarked model firing it manually.

## Wargear

### Armoured Ceramite

Enemy weapons with the Melta special rule do not roll extra armour penetration dice against a Fortification with Armoured Ceramite.

### Auspex Array

An Orbital Uplink Bunker's auspex array gives it the Tactical Auspex Uplink special rule.

### Auxiliary Void Shield

The Fortification has 1 Void Shield (AV 12), as detailed in the Age of Darkness rulebook.

### Camo-Netting

A turret with camo-netting has Shrouded (6+).

### Cognis-Signum / Vox-Relay

A Fortification with a Cognis-Signum / Vox-Relay has the Vox-Relay special rule: friendly Infantry units with the Line sub-type within 6" of it may re-roll failed Morale and Pinning tests. In addition, friendly units within 3" of it (including units embarked inside it) count as being within range of an Objective Marker controlled by their player for the purpose of triggering special rules, Warlord Traits, and Rites of War (such as Heart of the Legion), though the Fortification does not award Victory Points at the end of the battle.

### Orbital Strike Coordinates

Legiones Astartes and Solar Auxilia only. Once per game (One Shot), during your Shooting phase, if a friendly HQ model is embarked inside this Fortification, they may call down an Orbital Bombardment instead of firing a weapon: Range Unlimited, Strength 9, AP 2, Ordnance 1, Massive Blast (7"), Barrage, Pinning, One Shot (it always scatters 2D6").

### Reinforced Armoured Plating

The Fortification's Front, Side and Rear Armour Values are 13.

### Void-Transponder Array

An Orbital Uplink Bunker's void-transponder array gives it the Drop-Beacon Relay special rule.
