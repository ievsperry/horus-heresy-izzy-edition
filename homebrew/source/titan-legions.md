# Titan Legions for The Horus Heresy 2.0 — Homebrew Expansion

Nov 13, 2026 · @Isaac

## Introduction

This expansion adds three Secutarii and Collegia Titanica units, three Rites of War, two Advanced Reactions and three Warlord Traits to the official Titan Legions army list, and expands the Titan Maniple Detachment. It is used alongside the official list, not instead of it.

## Special Rules

### Secutarii

A unit with this special rule belongs to the Titan Legions (Collegia Titanica and Secutarii) and may only be taken in a Titan Maniple Detachment.

### Titan Guard

Models with this special rule gain the Stubborn and Feel No Pain (6+) special rules while within 6" of a friendly model with the Titan Unit Type.

### Micro-Auspex Designator

In the Shooting phase, instead of firing its Galvanic Carbine, one model in this unit may use its Micro-Auspex Designator to target one enemy unit within 36" and line of sight (making a normal To Hit roll). If a hit is scored, place a Target-Lock Token beside that enemy unit until the end of the phase. Friendly models with the Titan Unit Type firing at a unit with a Target-Lock Token gain +1 to their Ballistic Skill Characteristic (to a maximum of BS 5) and Ignores Cover on all Shooting Attacks against that target.

### Void-Shield Capacitor Relay

During the Shooting phase, instead of making a Shooting Attack or using Battlesmith, a Titan Enginseer within 3" of a friendly model with the Titan Unit Type may attempt to reboot its collapsed Void Shields. Roll a D6, adding +1 for every Repair Servo-Automaton in the unit within 3" of the Titan: on a 4+, the Titan immediately restores 1 collapsed Void Shield (in addition to any normal end-of-turn Void Shield restoration rolls).

### Titan-Link Targeting

If a friendly model with the Titan Unit Type within 12" fired at an enemy unit in the same Shooting phase, this unit may re-roll failed To Hit rolls of 1 when targeting that same enemy unit.

## Titan Maniple Detachment

A Titan Maniple Detachment may only include units with The Titan Legions faction icon (including Secutarii and Collegia Titanica units). These units may not be selected as part of any other Force Organisation chart or Detachment besides a Lords of War Detachment in a Crusade Force Organisation chart or a Titan Maniple Detachment.

- **Standard Use (Optional Detachment):** May be included as an Optional Detachment in any army that uses the Crusade Force Organisation chart (this does not replace any other Optional Detachments in that Force Organisation chart).
- **Primary Detachment Upgrade:** A Titan Maniple Detachment may instead be selected as an army's Primary Detachment if the controlling player selects one of the Collegia Titanica and Secutarii Rites of War for that Detachment, provided the Detachment meets all listed Prerequisites.
- **Detachment Slots:** Compulsory: 1 Lords of War or 1 Troops. Optional: +2 HQ, +6 Troops, +3 Lords of War.
- **Expanded Maniple Support (Primary Detachment only):** When selected as a Primary Detachment using one of the Collegia Titanica and Secutarii Rites of War, the Detachment also unlocks +2 Elites, +2 Fast Attack and +2 Heavy Support, exclusively for units with The Titan Legions faction icon.
- **Warlord Selection:** When used as a Primary Detachment, the army's Warlord must be a Secutarii Axiarch (in an HQ slot) or a Titan (in a Lords of War slot, if eligible to serve as Warlord).
- **Lord of War Points Limit:** When a Titan Maniple Detachment is used as a Primary Detachment, the standard 25% points limit on Lords of War choices does not apply to models with the Titan Unit Type, though at least 25% of the army's total points must still be spent on non-Lords of War units (HQ, Troops, Elites, Fast Attack, or Heavy Support) unless a scenario or agreement states otherwise.

## Rites of War

When selecting an army with the Collegia Titanica or Secutarii faction trait, the controlling player may select a single Rite of War for their Primary Detachment, provided the Detachment meets all listed Prerequisites (including the presence of a Secutarii Axiarch or a Titan). Only one Rite of War may be selected per Detachment, and all Effects and Limitations apply for the duration of the battle.

### Iron Phalanx of the God-Engines

*Marching in tight, interlocking shield-walls around the towering ankles of striding Warhound and Reaver Titans, the Secutarii cohorts turn every advance into an impenetrable moving fortress, shielding their god-engines from anti-tank hunters while Sacristan repair teams mend damaged plating under fire.*

**Prerequisites**

- A Detachment using this Rite of War must include at least one Secutarii Axiarch or at least one model with the Titan Unit Type, and its Warlord must be either a Secutarii Axiarch or a Titan (if eligible to serve as Warlord).

**Effects**

- Titan Guard Battleline: Secutarii Peltasts and Secutarii Hoplites may be selected as Troops choices in a Detachment using this Rite of War, and gain the Line Unit Sub-type.
- Synchronized Shield-Wall: Friendly Secutarii Hoplites, Secutarii Peltasts, and Secutarii Ballistarii within 6" of a friendly model with the Titan Unit Type improve their Kyropatris Field Generator Invulnerable Save by +1 (to a maximum of 4+), and may re-roll failed Morale and Pinning tests.
- Sacristan Field-Support: Enginseer Field-Repair Teams in a Detachment using this Rite of War may be selected as non-Compulsory Troops choices in addition to Elites choices, and gain a 5+ Invulnerable Save while within 6" of a friendly Titan.
- No Step Back: Friendly units with the Secutarii special rule within 3" of an Objective marker or a friendly Titan gain the Heart of the Legion special rule (granting Feel No Pain (6+)).

**Limitations**

- A Detachment using this Rite of War may not include any Fast Attack choices (other than Moderati Recon Foot-Patrols), nor may it include any Fortification choices.
- All Compulsory Troops choices in a Detachment using this Rite of War must be Secutarii Peltasts or Secutarii Hoplites.

### Vanguard Reconnaissance Maniple

*Deployed far ahead of the main Titan Legion column, swift scout-packs of Warhound Titans, Moderati foot-patrols, and mobile Secutarii skirmishers flush out concealed enemy ambushes, laser-designate armoured columns, and strike from unexpected flanks.*

**Prerequisites**

- A Detachment using this Rite of War must include at least one Secutarii Axiarch or at least one Warhound Scout Titan, and its Warlord must be either a Secutarii Axiarch or a Warhound Scout Titan.

**Effects**

- Outriders of the Legion: Moderati Recon Foot-Patrols and Secutarii Peltasts may be selected as Compulsory Troops choices in a Detachment using this Rite of War, and gain the Line Unit Sub-type (removing Support Squad from Moderati Recon Foot-Patrols).
- Forward Target-Lock: Friendly Moderati Recon Foot-Patrols add +12" to the range of their Micro-Auspex Designators, and friendly Warhound Scout Titans may re-roll failed Outflank or Reserves rolls while at least one Moderati Recon Foot-Patrol is on the battlefield.
- Swift Stride: All friendly Secutarii Infantry units in a Detachment using this Rite of War gain the Scout and Move Through Cover special rules, and add +1" to their Run and Charge Distance rolls.
- Predatory Pack: If a Detachment using this Rite of War includes two or more Warhound Scout Titans, those Warhounds gain the Fleet (1) special rule and may re-roll To Hit rolls of 1 when targeting an enemy unit that was hit by a Micro-Auspex Designator in the same Shooting phase.

**Limitations**

- A Detachment using this Rite of War may not include any Reaver, Warbringer, or Warlord Titans, nor may it include any Heavy Support choices (other than Secutarii Ballistarii mounted in Dedicated Transports).
- A Detachment using this Rite of War may not select any Fortification choices.

### Ordo Destructor Siege Cohort

*Unleashed when the Collegia Titanica is tasked with shattering fortified hive-walls, void-shielded citadels, and entrenched Legion strongpoints, this formation pairs heavy artillery Titans with relentless Secutarii heavy weapon phalanxes and breaching teams.*

**Prerequisites**

- A Detachment using this Rite of War must include at least one Secutarii Axiarch or at least one model with the Titan Unit Type, and its Warlord must be either a Secutarii Axiarch or a Titan.

**Effects**

- Breaching Phalanx: Secutarii Ballistarii may be selected as non-Compulsory Troops choices in addition to Heavy Support choices in a Detachment using this Rite of War.
- Siege-Breaker Protocols: All Secutarii units and Titans in a Detachment using this Rite of War gain the Tank Hunters and Wrecker special rules when targeting enemy Vehicles, Dreadnoughts, or Buildings / Fortifications.
- Overcharged Capacitors: Once per battle, at the start of any of the controlling player's Shooting phases, the Warlord may activate Reactor Surge. Until the end of that Shooting phase, all Secutarii Ballistarii and Enginseer Field-Repair Teams gain +1 to their Ballistic Skill Characteristic (to a maximum of BS 5), and all friendly Titans add +1 to any rolls made to restore collapsed Void Shields at the end of that turn.
- Subterranean Assault: Any Secutarii Infantry unit in this Detachment numbering 10 models or fewer may select a Termite Assault Drill as a Dedicated Transport.

**Limitations**

- No unit in a Detachment using this Rite of War may make Run moves in the Shooting phase during the first game turn.
- A Detachment using this Rite of War may not include any Allied Detachment.
- All Compulsory Troops choices in a Detachment using this Rite of War must be Secutarii Peltasts or Secutarii Hoplites.

## Advanced Reactions

Units with The Titan Legions faction icon (including Secutarii and Collegia Titanica units) have access to all Core Reactions presented in the Warhammer: The Horus Heresy – Age of Darkness Rulebook, as well as the following two unique Advanced Reactions. Unless otherwise stated, declaring an Advanced Reaction costs 1 point of the controlling player's Reaction Allotment in the appropriate phase.

### Interlocking Phalanx (Assault Phase)

*Trained to lock their mag-inverter shields into an unbroken wall of crackling energy around the ankles of their god-engines, Secutarii cohorts meet enemy shock-assaults with disciplined volley-fire and electrified lances.*

- Trigger: This Advanced Reaction may be declared in the Assault phase, immediately after an enemy unit declares a Charge against a friendly unit composed entirely of models with the Secutarii special rule (or declares a Charge against a friendly model with the Titan Unit Type while at least one friendly unit with the Secutarii special rule is within 6" of that Titan).
- Effect: Every model in the reacting Secutarii unit gains the Counter-Attack (1) special rule until the end of the current Assault phase, and improves its Kyropatris Field Generator Invulnerable Save by +1 (to a maximum of 4+) during the ensuing Fight sub-phase. Furthermore, if the enemy unit declared a Charge against a friendly Titan, the reacting Secutarii unit may immediately make a free Charge move of up to 6" into base contact with the charging enemy unit before the enemy unit moves (provided the Secutarii unit is not locked in combat, Pinned, or Falling Back).

### Void-Shield Overload (Shooting Phase)

*Channeling auxiliary plasma reserves directly into the war-engine's void-projector banks, a Titan's Princeps can momentarily flare its defensive barriers to deflect catastrophic incoming salvos, at the risk of burning out the generators.*

- Trigger: This Advanced Reaction may be declared in the Shooting phase, immediately after an enemy unit selects a friendly model with the Titan Unit Type (or a friendly unit with the Secutarii special rule within 6" of a friendly Titan with at least one active Void Shield) as the target of a Shooting Attack, before any To Hit rolls are made.
- Effect: Until the end of that Shooting Attack, the reacting Titan's active Void Shields gain a 3+ Invulnerable Save (instead of the normal 4+ Void Shield save, or blocking hits on a 3+ before collapsing), and any friendly Secutarii unit within 6" of that Titan gains a 4+ Invulnerable Save against that Shooting Attack. At the end of that Shooting Attack, roll a D6: on a 1, the reacting Titan immediately loses 1 active Void Shield due to capacitor burnout.

## Warlord Traits

If an army's Warlord has The Titan Legions faction icon (such as a Secutarii Axiarch or a Titan), the controlling player may select one of the three Warlord Traits below instead of rolling on the Core Warlord Traits table.

### Master of the Titan Guard

*A veteran Axiarch or senior Princeps whose tactical telemetry links every Secutarii cohort and Sacristan repair crew into a single, seamless defensive web around the Legion's god-engines.*

Friendly units with the Secutarii special rule within 12" of a Warlord with this Trait gain the Stubborn and Relentless special rules, and may re-roll failed Battlesmith and Void-Shield Capacitor Relay rolls of 1. In addition, an army whose Warlord has this Trait may make one additional Reaction during the opposing player's Assault phase, as long as the Warlord has not been removed as a casualty.

### Princeps Senioris of the God-Engines

*An ancient, neuro-linked commander whose iron will drives the machine-spirit of his Titan to terrifying feats of destructive fury, overwhelming enemy armour and shattering morale across the battlefield.*

If the Warlord is a model with the Titan Unit Type (or while the Warlord is within 6" of a friendly model with the Titan Unit Type), that Titan adds +1 to all rolls made to restore collapsed Void Shields at the end of the turn, and gains the Preferred Enemy (Vehicles & Gargantuan Creatures) special rule on all Shooting Attacks. In addition, an army whose Warlord has this Trait may make one additional Reaction during the opposing player's Shooting phase, as long as the Warlord has not been removed as a casualty.

### Warden of the Outer Perimeter

*An expert in vanguard reconnaissance and defensive ambushes, positioning Moderati foot-patrols and forward Secutarii screens to intercept enemy infiltrators and outflanking raiders before they can threaten the Legion's flank.*

Up to three friendly units with the Secutarii special rule in the Warlord's Detachment gain the Scout and Infiltrate special rules (if they do not already possess them). Furthermore, friendly Moderati Recon Foot-Patrols within 18" of the Warlord may use their Micro-Auspex Designators even after making a Run move in the Shooting phase. In addition, an army whose Warlord has this Trait may make one additional Reaction during the opposing player's Movement phase, as long as the Warlord has not been removed as a casualty.
