# Drukhari for The Horus Heresy 2.0 — Homebrew Conversion

Oct 4, 2026 · @Isaac

## Introduction

This is a homebrew conversion of the 7th edition Codex: Dark Eldar (2014) range, plus the Drukhari units added in 8th to 10th edition 40k, for The Horus Heresy 2.0. 2.0 has no official Drukhari, so every unit has a datasheet here. It uses 2.0 mechanics only and follows the same conventions as the Ork, Tyranid and Astra Militarum conversions: the 2.0 Force Organisation chart, datasheet layouts, options tables with right-aligned costs, and a weapon-family armoury.

The design goals: Drukhari are fast, fragile raiders. They hit hard and first, with poisoned splinter weapons and lances that punch through any armour, then vanish before the enemy can answer. Their infantry is lightly armoured and their skimmers are made of paper. They grow stronger as the battle goes on and they feed on pain.

Drukhari vehicles are open-topped skimmers using the 2.0 Antigrav sub-type†. Their monsters, the Talos and Cronos, use a Monstrous Creature type† like the Tyranid conversion, built on the official 2.0 Monstrous sub-type.

Points start from 7th edition costs and are adjusted where 2.0 changes a unit's value; treat them as playtest values. Base sizes follow recent 40k kits and are approximate. Rules marked † lean on exact 2.0 wording; the checklist at the end lists them.

## Army-wide Special Rules

Every unit in this document has **Power from Pain**, **Night Vision** and **Obsession (X)** unless its entry says otherwise. Infantry and Cavalry also have **Fleet (1)**.

**Power from Pain.** Drukhari grow stronger as the battle goes on and the suffering builds. Units with this rule gain the benefits below, from the game turn shown onward:

| Game turn | Benefit |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Feel No Pain (6+), or improve an existing Feel No Pain by 1 |
| 2 | May re-roll one die when rolling charge distance |
| 3 | Furious Charge (1), or increase an existing Furious Charge (X) by 1 |
| 4 onward | Fearless |

**Combat Drugs.** Before deployment, roll once on this table for each unit with Combat Drugs (or choose, for +5 points per unit). The result applies to the unit for the whole game.

| D6 | Drug | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Adrenalight | +1 Attack |
| 2 | Grave Lotus | +1 Strength |
| 3 | Hypex | +2 to the Movement Characteristic |
| 4 | Painbringer | Improve Feel No Pain by 1 |
| 5 | Serpentin | +1 Weapon Skill |
| 6 | Splintermind | +1 Ballistic Skill and Initiative |

**Drukhari Skimmers.** Drukhari vehicles have the Antigrav, Fast and Open-topped sub-types† unless their entry says otherwise, and may move over other models and terrain as long as they do not end on top of them.

**Monstrous Creature.** A living war-beast with Toughness and Wounds instead of Armour Values. A Monstrous Creature is a model of the Infantry unit type with the official 2.0 Monstrous sub-type: its unit cannot be Pinned; it may fire all of its weapons in each Shooting Attack, including Reactions; it may fire Heavy and Ordnance weapons and count as Stationary even if it moved, and may still declare a charge after shooting; and no model that is not also Monstrous may join its unit. Monstrous Creatures also have Hammer of Wrath (1).

**Night Vision.** The unit ignores the effects of Night Fighting†.

**Stealth.** If the model has not moved this turn, it cannot be targeted by ranged attacks from more than 12" away.

## Advanced Reaction: Lightning Withdrawal

Drukhari use the 2.0 core reactions as normal, plus this advanced reaction. It costs one point of the Reaction Allotment like any other reaction†.

**Lightning Withdrawal** (Shooting phase). Trigger: a Drukhari Infantry, Cavalry or vehicle unit is selected as the target of a Shooting Attack. Before the attack is resolved, the unit moves up to D6 plus its Initiative Characteristic in inches (vehicles move up to 6"), and must end further from the attacking unit than it started. If it is then out of range or line of sight, the attack is lost.

## Obsessions

Every Drukhari force fights for a Kabal, a Wych Cult or a Haemonculus Coven, each with its own cruel obsession. Obsessions work like Ork Klans and Hive Fleets.

**Obsession (X).** When building an army, choose one obsession for each detachment. Every unit in that detachment gains Obsession (X), where X is the chosen Kabal, Cult or Coven. Unique characters may join any obsession, except Lelith Hesperax (Cult of Strife), Drazhar (any Kabal) and Urien Rakarth (Prophets of Flesh).

**Obsession Advanced Reactions.** Each obsession has its own Advanced Reaction, usable only by units with that Obsession (X). It is available in addition to Lightning Withdrawal and costs one point of the Reaction Allotment†.

### Cult of Strife

*Lelith Hesperax's Cult, the deadliest gladiators in the arenas of Commorragh.*

- **Blade Artists:** the unit's melee weapons gain Shred.
- **The Spectacle of Murder:** the unit may re-roll to-hit rolls of 1 in close combat.

**Advanced Reaction: Whirling Death** (Assault phase). Trigger: an enemy unit declares a charge against a Cult of Strife unit. Instead of firing Overwatch, the unit gains Counter-Charge (1) and Reach (1) until the end of the phase†.

**Warlord Trait: Precision Killer.** The Warlord's melee weapons gain Murderous Strike (5+)†.

### Cult of the Red Grief

*Speed-addicted Wyches who race into battle on jetbikes and skyboards.*

- **Blur of Motion:** the unit increases its Fleet (X) by 1.
- **Speed Kills:** Cavalry, Jump and vehicle units add 2 to their Movement Characteristic.

**Advanced Reaction: Too Fast to Catch** (Movement phase). Trigger: an enemy unit ends a move within 12" of a Red Grief unit. The unit moves up to D6 plus its Initiative Characteristic in inches (vehicles move up to 6") in any direction.

**Warlord Trait: Hyper-swift Reflexes.** The Warlord and any unit he joins add 1 to their Initiative and gain Outflank†.

### The Dark Creed

*A Coven of terror-mongers who harvest fear as other Covens harvest flesh.*

- **Fear Incarnate:** enemy units within 6" subtract 1 from their Leadership.
- **Masters of Torment:** enemy units that fail a Morale check within 12" of this unit lose one additional model.

**Advanced Reaction: Torment** (Assault phase). Trigger: an enemy unit declares a charge against a Dark Creed unit. The charging unit must take a Leadership test; if it fails, it gains no bonuses for charging in that combat.

**Warlord Trait: Dread Master.** Enemy units within 12" of the Warlord subtract a further 1 from their Leadership.

### Kabal of the Black Heart

*Asdrubael Vect's own Kabal, the most powerful in Commorragh, driven by an endless thirst for power.*

- **Thirst for Power:** the unit gains each Power from Pain benefit one game turn earlier.
- **Vect's Favour:** the unit may re-roll failed Morale checks.

**Advanced Reaction: Strike First** (Shooting phase). Trigger: a Black Heart unit is selected as the target of a Shooting Attack. The unit makes a Return Fire Shooting Attack before the enemy attack is resolved, instead of after it.

**Warlord Trait: Hatred Eternal.** The Warlord and any unit he joins have Hatred against every enemy†.

### Kabal of the Poisoned Tongue

*Masters of poison and treachery, who kill their rivals before the rivals know they are enemies.*

- **Serpent's Kiss:** enemy units engaged with this unit subtract 1 from their Leadership.
- **Toxin Crafters:** the unit's weapons with Poisoned (X) improve that value by 1, to a best of 2+†.

**Advanced Reaction: Venomous Retort** (Shooting phase). Trigger: a Poisoned Tongue unit is selected as the target of a Shooting Attack. After the attack is resolved, the unit makes a Return Fire Shooting Attack whose Poisoned weapons wound on 2+†.

**Warlord Trait: Soul Thirst.** Each time the Warlord slays an enemy model in close combat, he restores one lost Wound, up to his starting value.

### Prophets of Flesh

*Urien Rakarth's Coven, the oldest and most twisted of the flesh-sculptors.*

- **Experimental Creations:** Monstrous Creatures in the detachment add 1 to their Wounds Characteristic.
- **Masters of the Flesh:** improve the unit's Feel No Pain by 1, to a best of 4+.

**Advanced Reaction: Insensible to Pain** (Shooting phase). Trigger: a Prophets of Flesh unit is selected as the target of a Shooting Attack. Against that attack, the unit's Feel No Pain improves by a further 1.

**Warlord Trait: Diabolical Soothsayer.** The Warlord has a 4+ Invulnerable Save.

## Warlord Traits

A Drukhari Warlord chooses one Warlord Trait: one of the three generic traits below, or the Warlord Trait of his obsession.

### Ancient Evil

*A being so old and so cruel that lesser creatures quail in its presence.*

Enemy units within 12" of the Warlord subtract 1 from their Leadership. In addition, while the Warlord is on the battlefield, your army may make one additional reaction in your opponent's Movement phase, above its normal Reaction Allotment†.

### Labyrinthine Cunning

*A schemer whose plans have plans, and who is never where the enemy expects.*

You may re-roll one Reserves roll each turn. In addition, while the Warlord is on the battlefield, your army may make one additional reaction in your opponent's Shooting phase, above its normal Reaction Allotment†.

### Master of Pain

*A connoisseur of agony who drinks deep of every scream.*

The Warlord and any unit he joins improve their Feel No Pain by 1. In addition, while the Warlord is on the battlefield, your army may make one additional reaction in your opponent's Assault phase, above its normal Reaction Allotment†.

## Force Organisation and Rites of War

Use the 2.0 Crusade Force Organisation chart: HQ, Elites, Troops, Fast Attack, Heavy Support, Dedicated Transports and Lord of War. Each unit takes the slot of the section it appears under:

| 2.0 slot | Drukhari units |
| --- | --- |
| HQ | Archon, Succubus, Haemonculus, Asdrubael Vect, Lady Malys, Lelith Hesperax, Urien Rakarth |
| Elites | Incubi, Drazhar, Court of the Archon, Mandrakes, Grotesques, Kheradruakh the Decapitator |
| Troops | Kabalite Warriors, Wyches, Wracks |
| Dedicated Transports | Raider, Venom |
| Fast Attack | Reavers, Hellions, Scourges, Beastmasters, Razorwing Jetfighter |
| Heavy Support | Ravager, Talos, Cronos, Voidraven Bomber, Reaper, Tantalus |
| Lord of War | Asdrubael Vect on the Dais of Destruction |

A Drukhari army may take one Rite of War, chosen when the army is built. Each has a benefit and a limitation.

**Arena Spectacle.** Requires a Succubus or Lelith Hesperax as Warlord. Benefit: Reavers and Hellions may be taken as Troops and gain the Line sub-type. Limitation: no Haemonculus Coven units (Haemonculus, Wracks, Grotesques, Talos, Cronos).

**Coven Experiments.** Requires a Haemonculus or Urien Rakarth as Warlord. Benefit: Talos and Cronos may be taken as Troops. Limitation: the army may not include more Infantry units than it has Monstrous Creature units, and no Fast Attack choices.

**Realspace Raid.** Requires an Archon as Warlord. Benefit: units may disembark from a Raider or Venom after it has moved at full speed, and may charge in the same turn†. Limitation: every Infantry unit must start the game embarked on a transport.

**Webway Ambush.** Requires any Drukhari HQ as Warlord. Benefit: every Infantry unit gains Deep Strike, and units arriving by Deep Strike may do so from the first game turn†. Limitation: no Heavy Support choices, and no Lords of War.

## Wargear

### Armour

*From the light armour of a Kabalite to the heavy plate of an Incubus, all Drukhari armour is made to be light enough to stay fast.*

#### Ghostplate Armour

*Armour laced with resin and a defensive field, worn by Archons and Scourges.*

Ghostplate armour confers a 4+ Armour Save and a 6+ Invulnerable Save.

#### Incubus Warsuit

*The heavy, ritual armour of the Incubi.*

An Incubus warsuit confers a 3+ Armour Save.

#### Kabalite Armour

*Segmented armour plates worn by Kabalite Warriors.*

Kabalite armour confers a 5+ Armour Save.

#### Wychsuit

*Minimal, flexible armour that leaves a Wych free to dodge and dance.*

A wychsuit confers a 6+ Armour Save.

### Chain-snares and Cluster Caltrops

*Barbed chains and scattered blades trailed from a skimmer or jetbike.*

After moving, a vehicle with chain-snares or a Reaver with cluster caltrops may inflict D3 hits at Strength 4, AP 5 on one enemy unit it moved over this turn.

### Clone Field

*A field that projects shifting false images of the wearer.*

A model with a clone field has a 5+ Invulnerable Save.

### Flickerfield

*A field that makes a skimmer flicker in and out of sight.*

A vehicle with a flickerfield has a 5+ Invulnerable Save against Shooting Attacks.

### Grav-talon

*A gravitic projector that slams enemies into the ground as a jetbike passes.*

When a Reaver with a grav-talon makes a Bladevane Strike, the hits gain Pinning.

### Grisly Trophies

*The flayed remains of victims hung from a skimmer's hull.*

Friendly Drukhari units within 6" of a vehicle with grisly trophies may re-roll failed Morale checks.

### Night Shields

*Shadow-projectors that cloak a vehicle in darkness.*

A vehicle with night shields has Shrouded (6+).

### Phantasm Grenade Launcher

*A launcher that floods the area with hallucinogenic gas.*

Enemy units that charge or are charged by a unit with a phantasm grenade launcher subtract 1 from their Leadership and Initiative until the end of the phase.

### Plasma Grenades

*Compact grenades that burst into searing plasma.*

Plasma grenades count as frag grenades†.

### Shadow Field

*A personal field of impenetrable darkness, flawless until it fails.*

A model with a shadow field has a 2+ Invulnerable Save. The first time it fails this save, the shadow field is destroyed for the rest of the game.

### Shock Prow

*A reinforced prow for ramming enemy vehicles.*

A vehicle with a shock prow adds 2 to its Strength when Ramming.

### Spirit Probe and Spirit Vortex

*Devices that harvest and redirect the souls of the dying.*

A Cronos with a spirit probe extends its Pain Parasite range to 12". A Cronos with a spirit vortex gains the spirit vortex weapon (see the Armoury).

### Splinter Racks

*Racks of splinter guns that let passengers fire a hail of poisoned shards.*

Units embarked on a vehicle with splinter racks may re-roll to-hit rolls of 1 with splinter weapons when firing from it.
