# Drukhari for The Horus Heresy 2.0 — Homebrew Conversion

Oct 25, 2026 · @Isaac

## Introduction

This is the Drukhari part of Liber Aelphari (Liber Xenos) for The Horus Heresy 2.0, merged with the earlier Drukhari conversion. The shared Aeldari rules (civilization keywords, the Aeldari detachments and the psychic disciplines) and the Anhrathe Corsairs are in the Aeldari document. Where Liber Aelphari has a rule or datasheet, it replaces the earlier version; units it does not cover (Asdrubael Vect, Lady Malys, Urien Rakarth, Kheradruakh, Beastmasters, the Razorwing, Voidraven and Reaper, and the Dais of Destruction) keep their earlier entries.

*The Drukhari are the cruel and predatory civilization dwelling within the hidden nightmare realm of Commorragh. Known to humanity as the Dark Eldar, the Drukhari rejected the disciplined Paths of the Craftworlds and instead embraced violence, excess and suffering as the foundation of their society. Their armies strike from hidden Webway portals in lightning-fast raids designed to capture slaves, harvest pain and terrorize entire worlds before vanishing once more into the darkness. Drukhari warfare relies upon speed, psychological warfare and overwhelming cruelty delivered through hyper-lethal close-range assaults.*

The Drukhari is the raiding army: fast, fragile, and hitting hard and first with poisoned splinter weapons and lances that punch through any armour, then vanishing before the enemy can answer. Points are playtest values. Rules marked † lean on exact 2.0 wording; the checklist at the end lists them.

## Army-wide Special Rules

Every Drukhari unit has **Night Vision** unless its entry says otherwise.

**Aeldari (X).** The unit's civilization keyword and, after it, its Drukhari faction (Kabal, Wych Cult, Haemonculus Coven, Incubi or Hired Blades). Aeldari (Drukhari) units may only be selected in armies whose Civilization keyword is Drukhari unless specifically stated otherwise, and may not be selected within the same Primary Detachment as Asuryani units. The full civilization rules are in the Aeldari document.

**Drukhari Skimmers.** Drukhari Antigrav vehicles may move over other models and terrain as long as they do not end on top of them†.

**Night Vision.** The unit ignores the effects of Night Fighting†.

**Stealth.** If the model has not moved this turn, it cannot be targeted by ranged attacks from more than 12" away.

**Ancient Cruelty.** *The ancient nobility of Commorragh are masters of calculated violence, striking with cold precision to eliminate leaders, sow terror and collapse resistance.* A model with this Special Rule gains Fear (1), Precision Shots (5+) and Precision Strikes (5+). In addition, attacks made by character models with this Special Rule against Characters gain Murderous Strike (6+).

**Arena Killers.** *These warriors are consummate gladiators, masters of spectacle, precision and humiliation in close combat.* A model with this Special Rule gains Fleet (1), Precision Shots (6+) and Precision Strikes (6+). In addition, while involved in a Challenge, a model with this Special Rule gains Reach (1), Fearless and Battle-hardened (2).

**Blade Artists.** Models with this Special Rule gain Rending (6+) and Sudden Strike (1). If a character model already possesses either of these Special Rules, improve their value by +1 instead, to a maximum of 4+ for Rending and 4 for Sudden Strike.

**Combat Drugs (X).** *The Drukhari make extensive use of psycho-reactive stimulants, adrenal catalysts and hyper-aggressive chemical compounds designed to heighten combat performance beyond natural limits.* A unit with Combat Drugs (X) must roll X D6 before deployment and apply the corresponding effects from the table below for the duration of the battle. Unless otherwise stated, duplicate results have no additional effect. If a result would grant a Special Rule the unit already possesses, increase the value of that Special Rule by +1 instead, to a maximum of 4 unless otherwise specified.

| D6 | Combat Drug | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Hypex | The unit gains Fleet (2). |
| 2 | Grave Lotus | The unit gains Furious Charge (1). |
| 3 | Serpentin | The unit gains Sudden Strike (1). |
| 4 | Painbringer | The unit gains Feel No Pain (6+). Feel No Pain granted by this rule may not be improved beyond Feel No Pain (4+). |
| 5 | Adrenalight | The unit gains Rage (2). If the unit already has Rage, increase its value by +1 instead, to a maximum of 3. |
| 6 | Splintermind | The unit gains Counter-Attack (1) and Fear (1). Fear granted by this rule does not scale. |

**Kabalite Discipline.** *Kabalite Warriors prosecute raids with cold precision, coordinating overlapping fire and surgical elimination strikes against vulnerable prey.* A unit with this Special Rule gains Precision Shots (6+). In addition, when targeting Pinned, Falling Back or Disordered enemy units, ranged attacks made by this unit gain Breaching (6+).

**Lightning Blows (X).** *Some warriors fight with such speed and precision that they are capable of landing a series of blows like lightning, before their opponent can even react. There are few that can withstand such a barrage of attacks, and those warriors that have mastered this technique are rightfully feared on the battlefield.* After normal To Hit rolls for a weapon with this special rule have been made, count the number of Hits inflicted on a roll of equal to or more than the value listed in brackets as part of this rule. Immediately resolve a number of additional To Hit rolls on the same unit using the attacking models' WS equal to the number of Hits counted earlier – any Hits scored roll To Wound as normal using the profile of the weapon with this special rule or a weapon chosen from those available to the model with this special rule if the rule is not applied to a specific weapon. These additional attacks do not themselves benefit from the Lightning Blows (X) special rule, but do benefit from any other special rules possessed by the attacking model, such as Preferred Enemy (X) or Precision Strikes (X).

**Master of the Arena.** Wych Cult Troops units in an army that includes one or more Succubus models gain the Line Unit Sub-type.

**Master of Blades.** Incubi units in an army that includes one or more models with this special rule gain the Line Unit Sub-type.

**Master of the Coven.** Coven Troops units in an army that includes one or more Haemonculus models gain the Line Unit Sub-type.

**Master Fleshcrafter.** Friendly Wracks, Grotesques, Talos and Cronos units within 6" of one or more friendly models with this special rule gain +1 to Feel No Pain rolls, to a maximum of Feel No Pain (4+).

**Master of the Kabal.** Kabalite Warriors Troops units in an army that includes one or more Archon models gain the Line Unit Sub-type.

**Master of the Raid.** Master of the Raid is the Drukhari equivalent of the Master of the Legion special rule and represents supreme battlefield authority within a Drukhari raiding force. Individuals possessing this rule are among the highest-ranking Archons, Succubi, or Haemonculi of Commorragh, commanding vast realspace raids through terror, manipulation, and overwhelming tactical brutality. A Detachment whose Primary HQ choice possesses the Master of the Raid special rule may select a Drukhari Rite of Raid. Unless otherwise specified, only one Rite of Raid may be chosen for each Primary Detachment. In addition, a Detachment led by a model with Master of the Raid may include a single Retinue unit that does not use up a Force Organization slot. Such a unit must begin the game joined to the model with this special rule unless explicitly stated otherwise. A model with Master of the Raid gains the Fear (1) special rule if it does not already possess it. Friendly Drukhari units within 12" of one or more models with this special rule may use the controlling player's choice of Leadership characteristic when taking Pinning tests, Regroup tests, or Morale checks caused by Shooting attacks. A Primary Detachment may only include a single model with the Master of the Raid special rule unless explicitly stated otherwise by a Rite of Raid.

**Power From Pain.** *The Drukhari feed upon terror, suffering, and the collapse of both friend and foe alike, descending into ecstatic states of violence as battle intensifies.* At the start of each Phase, a unit with this Special Rule may select one effect, unless otherwise specified, from the table below whose listed condition is currently being met. Each unit selects its effect independently.

| Condition | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Enemy unit within 12" Falling Back, Pinned, or Disordered | Fleet (1) |
| Enemy unit within 12" has suffered one or more casualties this Phase | Fleet (1), Sudden Strike (1) |
| Enemy unit within 12" is Below Half Strength | Fleet (2), Sudden Strike (2) |
| A friendly Wych unit has been destroyed during the battle | Rampage (1) |
| A friendly Wych Independent Character has been removed as a casualty during the battle | Rage (2) |

| An enemy Lord of War has been destroyed during the battle | Battle-hardened (2), Rampage (2) |
| An enemy Primarch has been removed as a casualty during the battle | Feel No Pain (4+), Furious Charge (2), Fleet (2), Rage (2) |

**Reservoir of Pain.** Whenever a model equipped with spirit-leech tentacles inflicts one or more unsaved Wounds that result in an enemy model being removed as a casualty during the Assault phase, the controlling player may select either the attacking unit or a single friendly Drukhari Infantry unit within 6" of the attacking unit. After all attacks for that combat have been resolved, the selected unit immediately regains a single lost Wound previously suffered during the battle. If no model in the selected unit has lost any Wounds, but the unit has suffered casualties earlier in the battle, a single previously removed model may instead be returned to the unit with a remaining Wounds characteristic of 1. A model returned to play by Reservoir of Pain must be placed in unit coherency. If the unit is locked in combat, the returned model may be placed in base contact with an enemy model already engaged with that unit. Character models returned to play by Reservoir of Pain do not grant additional Victory Points if removed as casualties again later in the battle.

**Shadowborn.** *These creatures drift through darkness like living nightmares, emerging from impossible angles amidst shadow and fear before vanishing once more into the gloom.* A unit with this Special Rule gains Infiltrate, Shrouded and Fear (1). In addition, enemy units treat models with this Special Rule as being 6" further away when making ranged attacks against them. Finally, enemy units arriving via Deep Strike within 3" of one or more models with this Special Rule suffer a Disordered Deep Strike Assault on a roll of 1, 2, or 3. Enemy units arriving via Deep Strike within 6" of one or more models with this Special Rule suffer a Disordered Deep Strike Assault on a roll of 1 or 2.

**Webway Assault.** *The Drukhari strike from hidden dimensional corridors and concealed webway breaches, descending upon their prey with terrifying speed and precision before vanishing once more into the void between worlds.* If a unit with this Special Rule arrives from Reserves during the current Turn, it gains Fleet (2), Sudden Strike (2) and Shrouded (6+). If a unit with this Special Rule already possesses Fleet or Sudden Strike, increase their value by +1 instead, to a maximum of 4. If a unit already possesses Shrouded, improve its value by +1 instead, to a maximum of Shrouded (4+). A unit may only benefit from Webway Assault during the Turn in which it arrived from Reserves unless otherwise specified.

## Types

### Bio-Construct (type)

*The Bio-Construct Unit Type represents grotesque fleshcrafted war-engines, pain engines, and surgically augmented monstrosities created by the Haemonculus Covens. Though biologically derived, these entities function on the battlefield in a manner comparable to Dreadnoughts, combining unnatural durability, integrated heavy weapon systems, and catastrophic close combat lethality within massively reinforced fleshcraft bodies. Bio-Constructs are sustained through layered biological redundancy, pain suppression matrices, vat-grown organ reinforcement, and psycho-reactive regenerative systems. Their bodies are resistant to conventional toxins and biological attack vectors, while invasive neural conditioning renders them incapable of fear or hesitation. Many Bio-Constructs also contain volatile animus-reactors, pain engines, or soul-furnaces that detonate violently upon catastrophic destruction.*

Any rule, effect, weapon, or special rule that specifically affects models of the Dreadnought Unit Type also affects models with the Bio-Construct Unit Type unless explicitly stated otherwise. The following rules apply to all models with the Bio-Construct Unit Type and any Bio-Construct sub-types: Successful Wounds scored by attacks with the Poisoned or Fleshbane special rules must be re-rolled against models of the Bio-Construct Unit Type. All Bio-Construct models have the Fearless special rule. A model with the Bio-Construct Unit Type may fire all weapons they are equipped with in each Shooting Attack they make, including as part of a Reaction. A model of the Bio-Construct Unit Type may fire Heavy and Ordnance weapons and counts as Stationary even if it moved in the preceding Movement phase, and may declare Charges as normal regardless of any Shooting Attacks made in the same turn. No model that is not also of the Bio-Construct Unit Type may join a unit that includes a Bio-Construct model. Any model with the Bio-Construct Unit Type that suffers an unsaved Wound with the Instant Death special rule is not immediately removed as a casualty, but instead loses D3 Wounds instead of one for each unsaved Wound with the Instant Death special rule inflicted upon it. In addition, when a model with the Bio-Construct Unit Type loses its last Wound, but before it is removed as a casualty, all models, friendly and enemy, within D6" suffer an automatic Hit at Strength 8, AP -.

## Advanced Reaction: Lightning Withdrawal

Drukhari use the 2.0 core reactions as normal, plus this advanced reaction. It costs one point of the Reaction Allotment like any other reaction†.

**Lightning Withdrawal** (Shooting phase). Trigger: a Drukhari Infantry, Cavalry or vehicle unit is selected as the target of a Shooting Attack. Before the attack is resolved, the unit moves up to D6 plus its Initiative Characteristic in inches (vehicles move up to 6"), and must end further from the attacking unit than it started. If it is then out of range or line of sight, the attack is lost.

## Kabals, Wych Cults and Haemonculus Covens

**Sub-faction (X).** Every Drukhari detachment fights for one of the three great powers of Commorragh: the Kabals, the Wych Cults or the Haemonculus Covens. Choose one when building each detachment, then one of its Obsessions (below). The sub-faction gives that detachment its Advanced Reaction, Warlord Trait and Line units.

### Kabals

*The Kabals are the ruling military aristocracies of Commorragh, vast criminal war-hosts ruled by ancient Archons whose power is maintained through terror, assassination and overwhelming violence. Kabalite raids are conducted with ruthless precision, combining highly disciplined infantry formations, elite skimmer assaults and synchronized volleys of poisoned firepower to collapse enemy resistance before meaningful retaliation can be organized. Every raid is both a military operation and a political performance, with rival nobles constantly competing for prestige, slaves and influence through displays of calculated brutality.*

Kabals primarily make use of Ancient Cruelty, Kabalite Discipline, Poison Masters and Terror Tactics.

- **Kabalite Line:** Kabalite Warriors, Kabalite Trueborn (if selected as a Troops choice), and Raiders selected as Dedicated Transports for Line units gain the Line Sub-type.

**Advanced Reaction: Coordinated Extermination** (once per battle). *The Kabalites are masters of synchronized violence, annihilating exposed enemies beneath precise and overwhelming fusillades.* This Advanced Reaction may be made once per battle when an enemy unit declares a Charge, arrives via Deep Strike, or ends a Movement within 12" of a unit composed entirely of models with the Kabalite Discipline Special Rule. The Reacting unit may immediately make a Shooting Attack targeting that enemy unit. During this Shooting Attack, all ranged weapons gain Precision Shots (5+). In addition, ranged weapons gain Breaching (5+) when targeting Pinned, Falling Back or Disordered enemy units.

**Warlord Trait: Lord of the Black Raid.** The Kabalite overlord orchestrates battlefield violence with terrifying precision, ensuring every strike falls exactly where resistance is weakest. The Warlord gains Master of the Raid and Fear (1). In addition, friendly units within 12" of the Warlord gain Precision Shots (6+). If a unit already possesses Precision Shots, improve its value by +1 instead, to a maximum of Precision Shots (4+).

### Wych Cults

*The Wych Cults are gladiatorial death societies devoted to speed, spectacle and ritualized slaughter. Their warriors are performers as much as killers, descending upon battlefields in ecstatic waves of hyper-violent motion driven by combat stimulants, martial obsession and a desperate hunger for sensation. Wych raids are designed not simply to destroy the enemy, but to humiliate and terrorize them publicly through impossible acrobatics, arena duels and massacres performed before cheering crowds within the arenas of Commorragh.*

Wych Cults primarily make use of Arena Killers, Blade Artists, Combat Drugs (X), Power From Pain and Webway Assault.

- **Wych Cult Line:** Wyches and Bloodbrides (if selected as a Troops choice) gain the Line Sub-type.

**Advanced Reaction: Death in Motion** (once per battle). *The Wyches of the arenas move with impossible speed and grace, turning enemy aggression into spectacle and slaughter.* This Advanced Reaction may be made once per battle when an enemy unit declares a Charge targeting a unit composed entirely of models with the Arena Killers Special Rule. The Reacting unit immediately gains Fleet (2), Sudden Strike (2) and Reach (2). In addition, the enemy unit's Charge is considered Disordered.

**Warlord Trait: Queen of Knives.** The Warlord is a legendary arena champion whose impossible speed and theatrical violence drives nearby warriors into ecstatic bloodshed. The Warlord gains Master of the Raid, Combat Drugs (4) and Fear (1). In addition, friendly units within 12" gain Fleet (1). If a unit already possesses Fleet, improve its value by +1 instead, to a maximum of 4.

### Haemonculus Covens

*The Haemonculus Covens are the flesh-sculptors and nightmare alchemists of Commorragh, ancient beings who have transcended conventional mortality through grotesque experimentation and bio-engineered resurrection. Their armies advance as processions of mutilated horrors, regenerated monstrosities and pain-engines stitched together through forbidden sciences older than many civilizations. Unlike the lightning-fast assaults of the Kabals and Wych Cults, Coven warfare is methodical, terrifying and inexorable, grinding enemies beneath waves of regenerating flesh while spreading psychological collapse through sheer biological horror.*

Haemonculus Covens primarily make use of Fleshcrafted Horror, Poison Masters, Power From Pain and Terror Tactics.

- **Coven Line:** Wracks, Haemoxytes and Grotesques (if selected as a Troops choice) gain the Line Sub-type.

**Advanced Reaction: Flesh Rewoven** (once per battle). *The grotesque creations of the Coven absorb horrific punishment while malformed flesh rapidly stitches itself back together amidst battle.* This Advanced Reaction may be made once per battle when a unit composed entirely of models with the Fleshcrafted Horror Special Rule is targeted by a Shooting Attack or Charge. Until the end of the current Phase, the Reacting unit gains Feel No Pain (4+) and Battle-hardened (2). In addition, models in the unit with more than 1 Wound regain 1 lost Wound at the end of the current Phase on a roll of 5+.

**Warlord Trait: Master of Agonies.** The Haemonculus Warlord reshapes flesh and suffering into weapons, driving nearby creations beyond mortal limitations. The Warlord gains Master of the Raid, Fear (2) and It Will Not Die (5+). In addition, friendly units within 12" with the Fleshcrafted Horror Special Rule improve their Feel No Pain rolls by +1 to a maximum of Feel No Pain (4+).

## Obsessions

Every Kabal, Wych Cult and Haemonculus Coven has its own cruel obsession. Obsessions are subsets of the sub-factions: a detachment's Obsession must belong to its Kabals, Wych Cults or Haemonculus Covens choice.

**Obsession (X).** When building an army, choose one Obsession of the detachment's sub-faction for each detachment. Every unit in that detachment gains Obsession (X), where X is the chosen Obsession. Unique characters may join any Obsession of their sub-faction, except Lelith Hesperax (Cult of Strife), Asdrubael Vect (Kabal of the Black Heart) and Urien Rakarth (Prophets of Flesh).

**Obsession Advanced Reactions.** Each Obsession has its own Advanced Reaction, usable only by units with that Obsession (X). It is available in addition to Lightning Withdrawal and the sub-faction's Advanced Reaction, and costs one point of the Reaction Allotment†.

### Kabal of the Black Heart

**Sub-faction:** Kabals

*Asdrubael Vect's own Kabal, the most powerful in Commorragh, driven by an endless thirst for power.*

- **Thirst for Power:** the unit gains each Power from Pain benefit one game turn earlier.
- **Vect's Favour:** the unit may re-roll failed Morale checks.

**Advanced Reaction: Strike First** (Shooting phase). Trigger: a Black Heart unit is selected as the target of a Shooting Attack. The unit makes a Return Fire Shooting Attack before the enemy attack is resolved, instead of after it.

**Warlord Trait: Hatred Eternal.** The Warlord and any unit it joins have Hatred against every enemy†.

### Kabal of the Poisoned Tongue

**Sub-faction:** Kabals

*Masters of poison and treachery, who kill their rivals before the rivals know they are enemies.*

- **Serpent's Kiss:** enemy units engaged with this unit subtract 1 from their Leadership.
- **Toxin Crafters:** the unit's weapons with Poisoned (X) improve that value by 1, to a best of 2+†.

**Advanced Reaction: Venomous Retort** (Shooting phase). Trigger: a Poisoned Tongue unit is selected as the target of a Shooting Attack. After the attack is resolved, the unit makes a Return Fire Shooting Attack whose Poisoned weapons wound on 2+†.

**Warlord Trait: Soul Thirst.** Each time the Warlord slays an enemy model in close combat, it restores one lost Wound, up to its starting value.

### Cult of Strife

**Sub-faction:** Wych Cults

*Lelith Hesperax's Cult, the deadliest gladiators in the arenas of Commorragh.*

- **Blades of Strife:** the unit's melee weapons gain Shred.
- **The Spectacle of Murder:** the unit may re-roll to-hit rolls of 1 in close combat.

**Advanced Reaction: Whirling Death** (Assault phase). Trigger: an enemy unit declares a charge against a Cult of Strife unit. Instead of firing Overwatch, the unit gains Counter-Charge (1) and Reach (1) until the end of the phase†.

**Warlord Trait: Precision Killer.** The Warlord's melee weapons gain Murderous Strike (5+)†.

### Cult of the Red Grief

**Sub-faction:** Wych Cults

*Speed-addicted Wyches who race into battle on jetbikes and skyboards.*

- **Blur of Motion:** the unit increases its Fleet (X) by 1.
- **Speed Kills:** Cavalry, Jump and vehicle units add 2 to their Movement Characteristic.

**Advanced Reaction: Too Fast to Catch** (Movement phase). Trigger: an enemy unit ends a move within 12" of a Red Grief unit. The unit moves up to D6 plus its Initiative Characteristic in inches (vehicles move up to 6") in any direction.

**Warlord Trait: Hyper-swift Reflexes.** The Warlord and any unit it joins add 1 to their Initiative and gain Outflank†.

### The Dark Creed

**Sub-faction:** Haemonculus Covens

*A Coven of terror-mongers who harvest fear as other Covens harvest flesh.*

- **Fear Incarnate:** enemy units within 6" subtract 1 from their Leadership.
- **Masters of Torment:** enemy units that fail a Morale check within 12" of this unit lose one additional model.

**Advanced Reaction: Torment** (Assault phase). Trigger: an enemy unit declares a charge against a Dark Creed unit. The charging unit must take a Leadership test; if it fails, it gains no bonuses for charging in that combat.

**Warlord Trait: Dread Master.** Enemy units within 12" of the Warlord subtract a further 1 from their Leadership.

### Prophets of Flesh

**Sub-faction:** Haemonculus Covens

*Urien Rakarth's Coven, the oldest and most twisted of the flesh-sculptors.*

- **Experimental Creations:** Bio-Construct models in the detachment add 1 to their Wounds Characteristic.
- **Masters of the Flesh:** improve the unit's Feel No Pain by 1, to a best of 4+.

**Advanced Reaction: Insensible to Pain** (Shooting phase). Trigger: a Prophets of Flesh unit is selected as the target of a Shooting Attack. Against that attack, the unit's Feel No Pain improves by a further 1.

**Warlord Trait: Diabolical Soothsayer.** The Warlord has a 4+ Invulnerable Save.

## Warlord Traits

A Drukhari Warlord chooses one Warlord Trait: one of the three generic traits below, or the Warlord Trait of its detachment's sub-faction (Kabals, Wych Cults or Haemonculus Covens) or Obsession.

### Ancient Evil

*A being so old and so cruel that lesser creatures quail in its presence.*

Enemy units within 12" of the Warlord subtract 1 from their Leadership. In addition, while the Warlord is on the battlefield, your army may make one additional reaction in your opponent's Movement phase, above its normal Reaction Allotment†.

### Labyrinthine Cunning

*A schemer whose plans have plans, and who is never where the enemy expects.*

You may re-roll one Reserves roll each turn. In addition, while the Warlord is on the battlefield, your army may make one additional reaction in your opponent's Shooting phase, above its normal Reaction Allotment†.

### Master of Pain

*A connoisseur of agony who drinks deep of every scream.*

The Warlord and any unit it joins improve their Feel No Pain by 1. In addition, while the Warlord is on the battlefield, your army may make one additional reaction in your opponent's Assault phase, above its normal Reaction Allotment†.

**Arena Spectacle.** Requires a Succubus, Master Succubus or Lelith Hesperax as Warlord. Benefit: Reavers and Hellions may be taken as Troops and gain the Line sub-type. Limitation: no Haemonculus Coven units (Haemonculus, Master Haemonculus, Wracks, Haemoxytes, Grotesques, Talos Pain Engine, Cronos Parasite Engine).

**Coven Experiments.** Requires a Haemonculus, Master Haemonculus or Urien Rakarth as Warlord. Benefit: Talos Pain Engines and Cronos Parasite Engines may be taken as Troops. Limitation: the army may not include more Infantry units than it has Bio-Construct units, and no Fast Attack choices.

**Realspace Raid.** Requires an Archon or Master Archon as Warlord. Benefit: units may disembark from a Raider or Venom after it has moved at full speed, and may charge in the same turn†. Limitation: every Infantry unit must start the game embarked on a transport.

**Webway Ambush.** Requires any Drukhari HQ as Warlord. Benefit: every Infantry unit gains Deep Strike, and units arriving by Deep Strike may do so from the first game turn†. Limitation: no Heavy Support choices, and no Lords of War.

## Wargear

### Armour

*From the light armour of a Kabalite to the heavy plate of an Incubus, all Drukhari armour is made to be light enough to stay fast.*

#### Bride Armour

*Elite Wych Cult ceremonial combat armour reserved for the most accomplished gladiatorial duelists and ceremonial blood brides of the arenas, built from refined flex-plating, nerve-linked reactive mesh and pain-synchronized reinforcement fibers.*

A model equipped with bride armour gains a 5+ Invulnerable Save. In addition, this improves to a 4+ Invulnerable Save in the Fight phase.

#### Fleshcrafted Plates

*Dense, ossified bio-armour grafts grown through accelerated ossification cycles and reinforced with synthetic tendon lattices.*

A model equipped with fleshcrafted plates gains a 6+ Armour Save, Feel No Pain (5+), and It Will Not Die (6+). In addition, models equipped with fleshcrafted plates do not suffer Instant Death. Instead, when affected by an attack or effect that would cause Instant Death, the model instead suffers D3 Wounds.

#### Ghostplate Armour

*Phase-laced composite armour that partially desynchronizes from realspace during impact events.*

A model equipped with ghostplate armour gains a 4+ Armour Save and a 6+ Invulnerable Save. In addition, if the model already has an Invulnerable Save, it is improved by +1 to a maximum of 4+.

#### Haemonculus Armour

A model equipped with Haemonculus armour gains a 4+ Armour Save and the Feel No Pain (5+) special rule. In addition, attacks with the Poisoned special rule targeting a model equipped with Haemonculus armour suffer -1 Strength (to a minimum of 1) as the armour's internal toxin filtration systems rapidly neutralize hostile biochemical agents. A model equipped with Haemonculus armour may ignore the first failed Dangerous Terrain test it suffers each turn.

#### Haemoxyte Armour

*Living bio-armour grown from harvested flesh matrices, semi-autonomous and continuously adaptive.*

A model equipped with Haemoxyte armour gains a 5+ Armour Save and the Feel No Pain (5+) special rule.

#### Incubi Armour

*A heavily reinforced shrine-warplate of layered psycho-reactive plating, hardened ablative mesh and localized shadowfield emitters, engineered for close-quarters execution warfare.*

A model equipped with Incubi armour gains a 3+ Armour Save and a 5+ Invulnerable Save. This Invulnerable Save may not be improved beyond 4+ by any means unless explicitly stated otherwise. In addition, a model equipped with Incubi armour gains Battle-hardened (1) during any Fight phase in which it successfully Charged or was Charged.

#### Kabalite Armour

## Armoury

*The Drukhari do not wage war through attrition, discipline or sustained firepower. Their arsenals are designed around terror, precision and the rapid extraction of suffering from the battlefield. Every weapon employed by the Drukhari reflects the realities of Commorragh's cruel culture — war is not simply conquest, but spectacle, punishment and harvest. Rather than relying upon mass-reactive munitions or brute-force kinetics, Drukhari weapons are engineered to incapacitate, dismember or agonize their victims with terrifying efficiency. Speed, elegance and lethality are valued above durability or reliability, for the Drukhari expect battles to end before retaliation can be meaningfully organized.*

*At the core of Drukhari warfare lies an obsession with biologically destructive weaponry. Splinter and shard weapons saturate targets with hyper-toxic projectiles capable of overwhelming even heavily augmented physiology through sheer virulence and volume of fire. Alongside these toxins, the Drukhari employ exotic darklight technologies such as Dark Lances and Heat Lances, weapons that distort matter itself through concentrated dimensional and thermal collapse effects. The Drukhari do not seek fair engagements — they seek asymmetry, exploiting fear, confusion and mobility to isolate and destroy vulnerable targets before vanishing back into the void.*

*Unlike the militaries of younger races, the Drukhari place little value on battlefield endurance or logistical simplicity. Their weapons are often highly specialized instruments crafted for raiding, assassination and shock assault operations. Gladiatorial aesthetics and personal prestige heavily influence weapon design, resulting in armaments that are both horrifying and ostentatious.*

Weapons that already exist in 2.0 use their official profiles (power weapon). Weapon special rules use their 2.0 names†.

## Rules Checklist (†)

These entries rely on 2.0 wording that should be confirmed. Where your book differs, keep the intent and use the book's mechanic.

- [ ] Antigrav, Fast, Open-topped and Super-heavy sub-types on Drukhari skimmers, and moving over other models.
- [ ] Flyer sub-type and flyer Movement (Razorwing, Voidraven).
- [ ] Reaction Allotment, Return Fire and extra reactions from Warlord Traits.
- [ ] Disembarking from a transport after it moved at full speed, then charging (Realspace Raid).
- [ ] Deep Strike on the first turn, Outflank, Infiltrate and Hit & Run.
- [ ] Weapon special rules: Poisoned, Lance, Haywire, Sniper, Shred, Rending, Reach, Murderous Strike, Hatred.
- [ ] Night Fighting and Night Vision.
- [ ] Fighting from a transport (Vect on the Dais of Destruction).

## Open Questions

- Fleshcrafted Horror, Poison Masters and Terror Tactics are named by the Kabals and Covens, but no rule text was supplied and no datasheet has them. The Flesh Rewoven reaction and Master of Agonies trait need units with Fleshcrafted Horror.
- The Blades of Lelith list a 6" profile with no weapon type; it is set as Assault 1 here.
- Thirst for Power (Kabal of the Black Heart) still refers to the old turn-by-turn Power from Pain; it needs new wording for the new Power From Pain table.
- The Cult of Strife's "Blade Artists" bonus is renamed Blades of Strife so it is not confused with the new Blade Artists special rule.
