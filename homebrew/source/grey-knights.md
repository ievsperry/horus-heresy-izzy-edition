# Grey Knights for The Horus Heresy 2.0 — Homebrew Conversion

2026-10-05 · @Isaac

## Introduction

This is a homebrew conversion of the 7th edition Codex: Grey Knights (2014) range, plus the Grey Knights units added in 8th to 10th edition 40k, for The Horus Heresy 2.0. The Grey Knights were founded after the Heresy, so like the Chaos Knights they suit Scouring-era, late-Heresy or narrative games. It uses 2.0 mechanics only and follows the same conventions as the other conversions.

The design goals: every Grey Knight is a psyker, armed with a nemesis force weapon and trained from birth to hunt daemons. They are few in number and very expensive, better than Legionaries in almost every way, and their army-wide psychic might lets them shrug off enemy sorcery, teleport across the battlefield and smash daemons apart.

**Unit types:** Grey Knights in power armour, Aegis Terminator armour and Aegis Tartaros armour are Infantry. The Dreadknight and the Dreadlancer are Armigers. Interceptors use the official 2.0 Jump Pack rules for their personal teleporters.

**Liber Daemonicus.** The rules, psychic powers, wargear, weapons and datasheets of Liber Daemonicus are merged into this list. Where the two disagree, Liber Daemonicus is used; every unit keeps Brotherhood of Psykers.

### Units to take from the official 2.0 books

Use the official datasheet for these units, adding the Grey Knights rules and points below†:

| 40k unit | Official 2.0 equivalent | Grey Knights rules | Extra points |
| --- | --- | --- | --: |
| Dreadnought, Venerable Dreadnought | Castra Ferrum Dreadnought Talon and Contemptor Dreadnought Talon (Heavy Support) | Psyker sub-type, Brotherhood of Psykers, Legiones Astartes (Grey Knights), Brotherhood (X), The Aegis | +25 points per Dreadnought; a Teleport Harness (Deep Strike) costs +40 points per Dreadnought |
| Land Raider | Land Raider Proteus Carrier Squadron or Land Raider Phobos Squadron (Heavy Support) | Brotherhood (X), The Aegis, Legiones Astartes (Grey Knights) | +10 points per Land Raider |
| Rhino, Razorback | Rhino Transport (2.0 has no Razorback), as the Dedicated Transport of a unit that may take one | Brotherhood (X), The Aegis, Legiones Astartes (Grey Knights) | +5 points |

Points start from 7th edition costs and are scaled to official 2.0 Legion values; treat them as playtest values. Rules marked † lean on exact 2.0 wording; the checklist at the end lists them.

## Army-wide Special Rules

Every Grey Knights unit in this document has **Brotherhood (X)**, **The Aegis**, **Legiones Astartes (Grey Knights)** and **Brotherhood of Psykers**, unless its entry says otherwise. Where Liber Daemonicus and the earlier conversion disagree, Liber Daemonicus is used.

**Loyalist.** A Grey Knights army must take the Loyalist Allegiance; it may never be Traitor.

**The Aegis.** All models with the Legiones Astartes (Grey Knights) special rule gain Adamantium Will (5+) and Shrouded (6+). In addition, enemy units making Shooting Attacks against a model with the Legiones Astartes (Grey Knights) special rule treat that model as being 3" further away than it actually is when determining weapon range, the effects of Special Rules, and whether a model is within a specified distance. When making Psychic Checks, units composed entirely of models with the Legiones Astartes (Grey Knights) special rule may re-roll Psychic Check results of 1.

**Legiones Astartes (Grey Knights).** All models with the Legiones Astartes (Grey Knights) special rule gain the Preferred Enemy (Daemons) and Stubborn special rules.

**Brotherhood of Psykers.** Every Grey Knight is a psyker: a model with this special rule has the Psyker Unit Sub-type. Its psychic powers come only from its datasheet: the Brotherhood Psyker and Brotherhood Librarian special rules, a Psychic Discipline it names, and any Minor Psychic Power it selects or carries. Psychic checks are made against the unit's Leadership; a failed check causes Perils of the Warp as in the 2.0 rulebook†.

**Brotherhood Psyker.** This model must select one Minor Psychic Power and one Psychic Discipline (Dominus, Sanctic, Anathemata, Divination or Telepathy). A model that already carries a Minor Psychic Power from its datasheet's Wargear keeps it and selects a second, so it has two; this is an exception to the Minor Psychic Powers rule.

**Brotherhood Librarian.** This model must select one Minor Psychic Power and two Psychic Disciplines. Only one Psychic Discipline may be used per Combat Turn.

**Core Psychic Discipline List.** Any Psyker that selects a Psychic Discipline also gains the Aetheric Lightning Psychic Weapon. This includes the Dominus, Sanctic, Anathemata, Divination and Telepathy Disciplines.

*Aetheric lightning is the fury of the Warp itself, coalesced and given form by the will of the psyker and directed at their foes like a storm of eldritch power.*

| Weapon | Range | Str | AP | Type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Aetheric Lightning | 18" | 3 | 4 | Assault 4, Force |

Force: Any Psyker with a weapon or ability with this special rule may choose to make a Psychic check before making any attacks with that weapon or resolving the ability. If the test is successful then the Strength value of any attacks made is doubled. If the test is failed then a Perils of the Warp attack is resolved targeting the unit containing the model that failed its test. If the Psyker survives Perils of the Warp then it may attack as normal.

**Dreadknight Talon.** When deployed onto the battlefield (either at the start of the battle or when arriving from Reserves), all models with this special rule in a unit must be placed within unit coherency but afterwards operate independently and are not treated as a unit.

**Minor Psychic Powers.** Models with this special rule know one or more Minor Psychic Powers. All models with the Brotherhood Psyker or Brotherhood Librarian special rule must select one Minor Psychic Power from the Minor Psychic Powers below during army selection, before the beginning of the battle; a datasheet that lists a Minor Psychic Power in its Wargear gives that power to every model in the unit instead. If a unit includes more than one model that must select a Minor Psychic Power, all those models must select the same power. No model may have or use more than one Minor Psychic Power, unless another special rule specifically states that it can. Unless otherwise specified, a Minor Psychic Power may be activated once per Phase at the time indicated in its description. To activate a Minor Psychic Power, the controlling player must make a Psychic Check using the Leadership Characteristic of the Psyker activating the power. If the Psychic Check is successful, resolve the effects of the power immediately. If the Psychic Check is failed, the power has no effect. If the Psychic Check results in Perils of the Warp, resolve the effects as normal. A model or unit may only attempt to activate each Minor Psychic Power once per Phase.

**Nemesis Force.** The controlling player of any unit that includes one or more models with the Psyker Unit Sub-type and a weapon or ability with this special rule may choose to activate this special rule before making any attacks with that weapon or resolving the ability. To activate this special rule, the controlling player must make a single Psychic Check using the Leadership Characteristic of any model in the unit that does not have the Independent Character Special Rule (unless the unit is entirely composed of models with the Independent Character Special Rule, in which case the controlling player may select which model's Leadership Characteristic is used). If the Check is successful, the Strength value of all attacks made with weapons or abilities with this special rule is increased by +2. In addition, all attacks made with weapons or abilities with this special rule gain Rending (6+) and Murderous Strike (6+). When targeting models with the Daemon Unit Type, attacks made with weapons or abilities with this special rule instead gain Rending (4+) and Murderous Strike (4+). If a weapon already possesses Rending and/or Murderous Strike, improve the value of each by +1 to a maximum of Rending (4+) and Murderous Strike (4+), or Rending (3+) and Murderous Strike (3+) when targeting models with the Daemon Unit Type. These benefits last until the end of the current Phase. If the Check is failed, no benefit is gained, but the models in that unit may attack as normal. This can trigger Perils of the Warp.

**Purifying Flame (Assault Variant).** Instead of acting solely as a ranged template weapon, a melee Purifier squad's psychic fire erupts outward in close combat. Cleansing Aura: at the start of each Fight sub-phase, before any Initiative steps are resolved, every enemy unit locked in combat with one or more units with this special rule suffers D6 automatic hits per 5 models in the Purifier unit (resolved at Strength 4, AP 4, Ignores Cover, Soul Blaze). Models with the Daemon, Corrupted, Corrupted Engine, or Malefic Unit Type or Sub-type suffer 2D6 automatic hits instead.

**Soul Blaze.** Whenever an enemy unit suffers one or more unsaved Wounds from an attack with the Soul Blaze special rule, it is set ablaze; place a marker next to the unit as a reminder. A unit cannot have more than one Soul Blaze marker on it at a time, regardless of how many Soul Blaze attacks wound it. At the end of every turn (both yours and your opponent's), roll a D6 for each unit with a Soul Blaze marker. On a 1–3 the flames burn out and the marker is removed. On a 4+ the unit stays ablaze and immediately suffers D3 hits resolved at Strength 4, AP 5, with the Ignores Cover special rule. Any Wounds caused by Soul Blaze are Randomly Allocated among the models in the burning unit. Against models with the Daemon, Corrupted, Corrupted Engine or Malefic Unit Type or Sub-type, Soul Blaze hits gain Rending (6+).

**Teleport Strike.** Grey Knights in Aegis Terminator armour, Strike Squads and Interceptor Squads have Deep Strike, and do not scatter if any friendly Grey Knights unit is within 6" of the target point†.

**Apothecarion Detachment.** A Brother Apothecary unit is selected as any other unit, using up a single Force Organisation slot and bought in the same manner. However, before the first turn begins and any models are deployed, every model in it must be assigned to another unit from the same Detachment. A Brother Apothecary may only be assigned to a unit composed entirely of Infantry models with the same Brotherhood (X) special rule as the Apothecary; to be assigned to a Brotherhood Terminator Squad or a Paladin Squad, the Apothecary must wear Aegis Terminator armour. No Brother Apothecary may be assigned to a unit that includes one or more models with the Independent Character special rule or the Unique Sub-type (but such models may join a unit that includes a Brother Apothecary as normal during deployment or any following turn). No more than one Brother Apothecary may be assigned to any given unit. Once assigned, the Apothecary is part of that unit and may not leave it under any circumstances; if that unit is removed as a casualty, the Apothecary is removed as well. In battles using Victory points, no Victory points are ever scored for removing a Brother Apothecary as a casualty. When assigned to a unit, a Brother Apothecary gains all of the special rules (except those that specifically forbid it) and Unit Sub-types listed for that unit, but does not gain access to any additional Wargear options available to it.

**Techmarine Covenant.** A Brotherhood Techmarine unit is selected as any other unit, using up a single Force Organisation slot and bought in the same manner. However, before the first turn begins and any models are deployed, every model in it must be assigned to another unit from the same Detachment. A Brotherhood Techmarine may only be assigned to a unit composed entirely of models with the Infantry Unit Type and the same Brotherhood (X) special rule as the Techmarine, and may not join a unit with Terminator armour of any kind. No Brotherhood Techmarine may be assigned to a unit that includes one or more models with the Independent Character special rule or the Unique Sub-type (but such models may join a unit that includes a Brotherhood Techmarine as normal during deployment or any following turn). No more than one Brotherhood Techmarine may be assigned to any given unit. Once assigned, the Techmarine is part of that unit and may not leave it under any circumstances; if that unit is removed as a casualty, the Techmarine is removed as well. In battles using Victory points, no Victory points are ever scored for removing a Brotherhood Techmarine as a casualty. When assigned to a unit, a Brotherhood Techmarine gains all of the special rules (except those that specifically forbid it) and Unit Sub-types listed for that unit, but does not gain access to any additional Wargear options available to it.

## Psychic Discipline: Dominus

A Psyker with this Discipline gains all the listed Powers, weapons and other special rules.

*The Dominus Discipline represents the Grey Knights' mastery of telekinetic force, psychic projection and the direct manipulation of reality through sheer mental strength. Practitioners of this discipline do not rely upon subtlety or divination, instead unleashing focused bursts of psychic power capable of crushing armour, hurling enemies from their feet and enhancing the speed and ferocity of their allies. Among the Brotherhoods of Titan, the Dominus Discipline is often favoured by battlefield commanders and warrior-mystics who seek to turn psychic power into a direct weapon of war.*

### Warp Manipulation (Psychic Power)

*Masters of the Dominus Discipline wield raw psychic force as a weapon and a shield, bending reality itself to hinder their enemies and empower their allies.*

Instead of making a Shooting Attack, a Psyker with this Psychic Power may select another friendly unit within 12". That unit gains Fleet (2) and Sudden Strike (2) for the duration of the current player turn. When using this power, the controlling player may choose to have the Psyker take a Psychic check. If the Check is passed then the target unit instead gains Fleet (3) and Sudden Strike (3) for the duration of the current player turn. If the Check is failed then no additional benefit is gained and the Psyker suffers Perils of the Warp.

### Dominus Blast (Psychic Weapon)

*The practitioners of the Dominus Discipline channel concentrated force through their minds, unleashing devastating blasts of psychic energy capable of crushing armour and flesh alike.*

| Weapon | Range | Str | AP | Type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dominus Blast | 24" | 7 | 3 | Assault 2, Force, Psychic Focus, Nemesis Force |

Attacks made with this weapon gain Rending (3+) against units containing one or more models with the Daemon or Corrupted Sub-types.

## Psychic Discipline: Sanctic

A Psyker with this Discipline gains all the listed Powers, weapons and other special rules.

*The Sanctic Discipline embodies the sacred rites, wards and purifying flames that define the Grey Knights' eternal war against the servants of Chaos. Its practitioners surround themselves and their allies with protective wards while unleashing torrents of sanctified psychic fire that scour flesh, spirit and soul alike.*

### Sanctuary (Psychic Power)

*The sacred wards and hexagrammic rites of the Grey Knights form an impenetrable barrier against the creatures of the Warp.*

Instead of making a Shooting Attack, a Psyker with this Psychic Power may select a single friendly unit within 12". That unit gains a 6+ Invulnerable Save until the beginning of the controlling player's next turn. If the target unit already possesses an Invulnerable Save, improve that Invulnerable Save by 1, to a maximum of 4+. When using this power, the controlling player may choose to have the Psyker take a Psychic check. If the Check is passed then the target unit instead gains a 5+ Invulnerable Save or improves any existing Invulnerable Save by 1 to a maximum of 3+ until the beginning of the controlling player's next turn. If the Check is failed then no additional benefit is gained and the Psyker suffers Perils of the Warp.

### Purifying Flame (Psychic Weapon)

*The Sanctic Discipline allows its practitioners to unleash torrents of silver fire, reducing Daemons and the servants of Chaos to ash.*

| Weapon | Range | Str | AP | Type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Purifying Flame | Template | 5 | 4 | Assault 1, Deflagrate, Torrent (6"), Psychic Focus, Nemesis Force |

Attacks made with this weapon gain Rending (3+) against units containing one or more models with the Daemon or Corrupted Sub-types.

## Psychic Discipline: Anathemata

A Psyker with this Discipline and the Traitor Allegiance gains the Breach the Veil Psychic Power and the Void Darts and Aetheric Lightning Psychic Weapons. A Psyker with this Discipline and the Loyalist Allegiance gains the Seal the Veil Psychic Power and the Void Darts and Aetheric Lightning Psychic Weapons. Grey Knights are always Loyalist, so a Grey Knights Psyker with this Discipline gains Seal the Veil, Void Darts and Aetheric Lightning; Breach the Veil is not used.

### Void Darts (Psychic Weapon)

*By tearing a series of tiny apertures into reality itself, the Esoterist can unleash the fury of hell itself upon the foe, channelling the power of the Warp for his own purposes.*

| Weapon | Range | Str | AP | Type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Void darts | 18" | 5 | 4 | Assault 12, Sanctic, Deflagrate, Psychic Focus |

Sanctic: A weapon with this special rule always Wounds models with the Daemon Unit Type on a 2+ and any successful Invulnerable Saves made by models with the Daemon Unit Type against any Wounds it inflicts must be re-rolled.

### Seal the Veil (Psychic Power)

*Some Esoterists study the Warp with the sole intent of discovering the weakness of those creatures that dwell within. They master the sealing of aetheric energy and investigate any method of repelling the foul entities that spill from that realm, no matter how strange or ritualistic such arcane tricks might be. Though those battle-psykers that have received real training may scoff at their dubious rites, few can deny their effectiveness in battle.*

Instead of making a Shooting Attack, a Psyker with this Psychic Power may select a single enemy unit composed entirely of models with the Daemon Unit Type or Corrupted Unit Sub-type that is within line of sight and has at least one model within 18" of the Psyker. All models in the target unit must reduce their Strength and Toughness by 1 (to a minimum of Toughness or Strength 1) until the end of the target unit's controlling player's next turn – the controlling player of the Psyker using this Power may then choose to make a Psychic check for the Psyker. If the Check is passed the target unit also suffers Perils of the Warp, and if the Check is failed then both the target unit and the Psyker using this Psychic Power suffer Perils of the Warp.

## Psychic Discipline: Divination

A Psyker with this Discipline gains all the listed Powers, weapons and other special rules, as well as the Aetheric Lightning Psychic Weapon.

*Diviners can effortlessly predict the paths of bullets and swords. By focusing their warp-sight even more closely, they can guide their allies' aim, bringing a swift and merciless death to their foes.*

If the Check is failed then no additional benefit is gained and the Psyker suffers Perils of the Warp.

## Psychic Discipline: Telepathy

A Psyker with this Discipline gains all the listed Powers, weapon and other special rules, as well as the Aetheric Lightning Psychic Weapon.

### Telepathic Fugue (Psychic Power)

*Paranoia, confusion and panic are heightened to a debilitating degree as the telepath alters their foes' perceptions, denying them a chance to react to the reality around them.*

Once per turn, at the start of any Phase, the Psyker with this Psychic Power's controlling player may select a single enemy unit within 24" and line of sight of the Psyker and take a Psychic check. If that Check is passed then the target unit may not make any Reactions for the duration of that Phase. If the Check is failed then the Psyker suffers Perils of the Warp.

### Telepathic Hallucinations (Psychic Weapon)

*The armour of the foe cannot protect them from the telepath's cruel assault, infiltrating the minds of their victims and turning brother against brother.*

| Weapon | Range | Str | AP | Type |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Telepathic Hallucinations | 36" | - | - | Assault 6, Hallucinations, Psychic Focus |

Hallucinations: A unit that suffers one or more Hits from a Weapon with this special rule must make an immediate Pinning test, adding one to the result of the roll for each Hit scored by this attack before the result is decided. For example, if a Psyker attacks an enemy unit that has a Leadership of 8, scoring 3 Hits with Telepathic Hallucinations, then that unit must make an immediate Pinning test and add 3 to the result rolled before determining the result.

Psychic Focus: Before making any To Hit rolls with this weapon, the Psyker must make a Psychic check. If the Check is passed, then the Psyker may attack as normal using the profile shown for this weapon. If the Check is failed, then the Psyker suffers Perils of the Warp, and if the model is not removed as a casualty then it may attack as normal but may not use this weapon.

## Minor Psychic Powers

A model selects or carries one of these, as the Minor Psychic Powers special rule describes, in the way a Thousand Sons psyker selects a Minor Arcana.

### Astral Aim

Astral Aim may be activated at the start of any Shooting Phase. If the Psychic Check is successful, all ranged weapons in the unit gain Indirect Fire and Ignores Cover. In addition, all Psi-weapons carried by models in the unit gain +6" Range. Any Incinerator or Heavy Incinerator carried by models in the unit gains Torrent (6").

### Cleansing Flame

Cleansing Flame may be activated at the start of any Assault Phase. If the Psychic Check is successful, every enemy unit within 6" of one or more models from the affected unit immediately suffers D6 Strength 3 AP - hits with Rending (6+). Against models with the Daemon Unit Type, these attacks instead gain Rending (4+). These hits are resolved immediately.

### Gates of Infinity

A unit containing one or more models with this Psychic Power may use it during its Movement phase instead of moving normally. If successfully manifested, the unit may immediately make a move of up to 12". This movement ignores all terrain, intervening models and obstacles, but may not end within 1" of an enemy model. A unit that uses this Psychic Power may Shoot, Run and Charge normally during the same turn. A unit may not use Gates of Infinity while Falling Back or Pinned.

### Hammerhand

Hammerhand may be activated at the start of any Assault Phase. If the Psychic Check is successful, all models in the unit gain Furious Charge (2) and Rage (2) until the end of the current Phase.

### Holocaust

Holocaust may be activated at the start of any Assault Phase. If the Psychic Check is successful, all models in the unit gain Hammer of Wrath (3) until the end of the current Phase. Against models with the Daemon Unit Type, these Hammer of Wrath attacks are resolved at AP2.

### Warp Quake

Warp Quake may be activated at the start of any Movement Phase. If the Psychic Check is successful, the unit gains Fleet (2) and Sudden Strike (2) until the end of the current turn. In addition, the unit may declare a Charge during a turn in which it Ran.

### Warp Shroud

Warp Shroud may be activated at the start of any Movement Phase. If the Psychic Check is successful, the unit gains Shrouded (5+) until the start of its controlling player's next turn. In addition, enemy units treat the affected unit as being 6" further away than it actually is when determining weapon range, the effects of Special Rules, and whether a model is within a specified distance. Enemy units declaring a Charge against the affected unit subtract 2" from their Charge Roll.

## Advanced Reaction: Psychic Ward

Grey Knights use the 2.0 core reactions as normal, plus this advanced reaction. It costs one point of the Reaction Allotment like any other reaction†.

**Psychic Ward** (Shooting phase). Trigger: a Grey Knights unit is selected as the target of a Shooting Attack or a psychic weapon. Before the attack is resolved, the unit casts Sanctuary on itself (taking a Psychic check as normal).

## Brotherhoods

The Grey Knights are organised into eight Brotherhoods, plus the Purifiers. Brotherhoods work like Ork Klans and Sororitas Orders.

**Brotherhood (X).** When building an army, choose one Brotherhood for each detachment. Every unit in that detachment gains Brotherhood (X). Kaldor Draigo, Castellan Crowe and Supreme Grand Master Janus may join any Brotherhood; Brother-Captain Stern, Grand Master Voldus and Grand Master Iapto only the 3rd Brotherhood; Grand Master Koios only the 1st, Satre the 2nd, Yotun the 4th, Epimetheus the 5th and Ogen the 6th; Grand Master Khyron the 7th or the 8th. Each shows only in a Detachment of his Brotherhood. Each Brotherhood's Advanced Reaction is available in addition to Psychic Ward and costs one point of the Reaction Allotment†.

### 1st Brotherhood, the Swordbearers

*The Chapter's foremost blademasters, who lead the charge and are first into every breach.*

**Brotherhood Trait: Fury of the Vanguard.** Units with Brotherhood (1st) gain the following:

- **Unwavering:** All models with the Brotherhood (1st) special rule have a Leadership Characteristic of 10 (this may not be modified above 10).
- **Vanguard of the Chapter:** Models with the Brotherhood (1st) special rule gain a +1 bonus to their Attacks Characteristic in any turn in which they made a successful Charge.

**Advanced Reaction: Blades Drawn** (Assault phase). Trigger: This Advanced Reaction may be declared in the Assault phase, after an enemy unit declares a Charge against a friendly unit composed entirely of models with the Brotherhood (1st) special rule, before any Overwatch attacks are resolved. Effect: The reacting unit forfeits its ability to make an Overwatch reaction against the charging unit. Instead, every model in the reacting unit gains the Counter-Attack (1) special rule until the end of the current Assault phase.

**Warlord Trait: First Among Equals.** A Warlord with this Trait, and any unit he joins, gains the Rage (2) special rule. In addition, an army whose Warlord has this Trait may make one additional Reaction during the opposing player's Assault phase, as long as the Warlord has not been removed as a casualty.

### 2nd Brotherhood, the Blades of Victory

*Duellists without peer, whose speed and precision have won a hundred wars.*

**Brotherhood Trait: Peerless Duellists.** Units with Brotherhood (2nd) gain the following:

- **Blade Mastery:** Models with the Brotherhood (2nd) special rule re-roll To Hit rolls of 1 when fighting in the Assault phase.
- **Lightning Reflexes:** Models with the Brotherhood (2nd) special rule gain a +1 bonus to their Initiative Characteristic.

**Advanced Reaction: Riposte** (Assault phase). Trigger: This Advanced Reaction may be declared in the Assault phase, immediately after an enemy unit completes a successful Charge against a friendly unit composed entirely of models with the Brotherhood (2nd) special rule. Effect: During the ensuing Fight sub-phase, all models in the reacting unit fight at the Initiative 10 step, regardless of their actual Initiative Characteristic or any penalties from unwieldy weapons.

**Warlord Trait: Unstoppable Champion.** All Melee weapons wielded by a Warlord with this Trait gain the Rending (6+) special rule (or improve an existing Rending value by 1, to a maximum of 4+). In addition, an army whose Warlord has this Trait may make one additional Reaction during the opposing player's Assault phase, as long as the Warlord has not been removed as a casualty.

### 3rd Brotherhood, the Wardmakers

*The Chapter's most potent psykers, masters of warding and banishment.*

**Brotherhood Trait: Masters of the Aegis.** Units with Brotherhood (3rd) gain the following:

- **Psychic Discipline:** Once per phase, a unit composed entirely of models with the Brotherhood (3rd) special rule may re-roll a single failed Psychic check.
- **Searing Wards:** Any Dominus Blast or Purifying Flame psychic weapon used by a model with the Brotherhood (3rd) special rule gains the Shred special rule.

**Advanced Reaction: Ward of the Brotherhood** (Shooting phase). Trigger: This Advanced Reaction may be declared in the Shooting phase, after an enemy unit selects a friendly unit composed entirely of models with the Brotherhood (3rd) special rule as the target of a Shooting Attack, before any To Hit rolls are made. Effect: Until the end of that Shooting Attack, all models in the reacting unit gain a 5+ Invulnerable Save. If a model already possesses an Invulnerable Save, it improves that save by +1 instead (to a maximum of 3+).

**Warlord Trait: Master of Wards.** A Warlord with this Trait may select one additional Minor Psychic Power at the start of the battle. In addition, an army whose Warlord has this Trait may make one additional Reaction during the opposing player's Shooting phase, as long as the Warlord has not been removed as a casualty.

### 4th Brotherhood, the Rapiers

*Marksmen and tacticians who prefer to purge the enemy at range.*

**Brotherhood Trait: Precision Volley.** Units with Brotherhood (4th) gain the following:

- **Rapier Volleys:** All Storm Bolters (and Combi-bolters) fired by models with the Brotherhood (4th) special rule gain the Breaching (6+) special rule.
- **Steady Aim:** Models with the Brotherhood (4th) special rule re-roll To Hit rolls of 1 when making Shooting Attacks.

**Advanced Reaction: Covering Bolters** (Shooting phase). Trigger: This Advanced Reaction may be declared in the Shooting phase, after an enemy unit selects a friendly unit composed entirely of models with the Brotherhood (4th) special rule as the target of a Shooting Attack. Effect: Rather than the targeted unit reacting, a single other friendly unit composed entirely of models with the Legiones Astartes (Grey Knights) special rule within 6" of the targeted unit may immediately make a Return Fire Shooting Attack targeting the active enemy unit, following all standard Return Fire restrictions.

**Warlord Trait: Strategist.** While a Warlord with this Trait is alive (including while in Reserves), the controlling player may re-roll one Reserves roll per turn. In addition, an army whose Warlord has this Trait may make one additional Reaction during the opposing player's Movement phase, as long as the Warlord has not been removed as a casualty.

### 5th Brotherhood, the Preservers

*Stoic guardians who hold the line against any horror.*

**Brotherhood Trait: Bastion of Titan.** Units with Brotherhood (5th) gain the following:

- **Stoic Defenders:** Models with the Brotherhood (5th) special rule gain the Feel No Pain (6+) special rule.
- **Unbreakable:** Units composed entirely of models with the Brotherhood (5th) special rule automatically pass all Pinning tests.

**Advanced Reaction: Unyielding Anvil** (Assault phase). Trigger: This Advanced Reaction may be declared in the Assault phase, immediately after an enemy unit declares a Charge against a friendly unit composed entirely of models with the Brotherhood (5th) special rule. Effect: The charging enemy unit must subtract D3 from its Charge Distance roll (to a minimum of 0).

**Warlord Trait: Stalwart.** A Warlord with this Trait, and any unit he joins, gains the Feel No Pain (5+) special rule. In addition, an army whose Warlord has this Trait may make one additional Reaction during the opposing player's Assault phase, as long as the Warlord has not been removed as a casualty.

### 6th Brotherhood, the Exactors

*Relentless hunters of daemons and witches, who never let their quarry escape.*

**Brotherhood Trait: Inexorable Hunt.** Units with Brotherhood (6th) gain the following:

- **Relentless Pursuit:** Models with the Brotherhood (6th) special rule re-roll To Wound rolls of 1 against any unit containing at least one model with the Psyker Unit Sub-type, or the Daemon, Corrupted, Corrupted Engine, or Malefic Unit Type or Sub-type.
- **Sanctified Quarry:** Units composed entirely of models with the Brotherhood (6th) special rule add +1 to their Sweeping Advance rolls.

**Advanced Reaction: Swift Retribution** (Movement phase). Trigger: This Advanced Reaction may be declared in the Movement phase, after an enemy unit ends a move within 12" of, and within line of sight of, a friendly unit composed entirely of models with the Brotherhood (6th) special rule. Effect: The reacting unit may immediately make a Shooting Attack targeting the triggering enemy unit, following all normal rules for Shooting Attacks (this counts as an Interceptor reaction).

**Warlord Trait: Merciless Hunter.** A Warlord with this Trait gains the Preferred Enemy (Everything) special rule. In addition, an army whose Warlord has this Trait may make one additional Reaction during the opposing player's Movement phase, as long as the Warlord has not been removed as a casualty.

### 7th Brotherhood, the Prescient Brethren

*Seers who strike exactly where and when the Emperor wills it.*

**Brotherhood Trait: Sight Beyond Sight.** Units with Brotherhood (7th) gain the following:

- **Foreseen Arrival:** Add +1 to all Reserves rolls (including Deep Strike Assault rolls) made for units composed entirely of models with the Brotherhood (7th) special rule.
- **Perfect Teleport:** Any unit composed entirely of models with the Brotherhood (7th) special rule arriving via Deep Strike (or Teleport Strike) does not scatter when placed on the battlefield (it must still be placed more than 1" away from enemy models and impassable terrain).

**Advanced Reaction: Foresight** (Movement phase). Trigger: This Advanced Reaction may be declared in the Movement phase, after an enemy unit ends a move within 12" of a friendly unit composed entirely of models with the Brotherhood (7th) special rule. Effect: Every model in the reacting unit may immediately make a move of up to D6 + its Initiative Characteristic in inches. This move may not bring any model within 1" of an enemy model, and does not prevent the unit from shooting or charging in its own turn.

**Warlord Trait: Prescience.** Once per game, while the Warlord is on the battlefield, the controlling player may re-roll any single dice roll (including a single dice as part of a 2D6 or multi-dice roll) made for a friendly unit with the Legiones Astartes (Grey Knights) special rule. In addition, an army whose Warlord has this Trait may make one additional Reaction during the opposing player's Movement phase, as long as the Warlord has not been removed as a casualty.

### 8th Brotherhood, the Purifiers

## Wargear

Wargear marked with an asterisk on a datasheet (power armour, artificer armour, servo-arm, frag assault launchers, smoke launchers, hunter-killer missiles) uses its official 2.0 rules.

### Aegis Artificer Armour

A model equipped with Aegis Artificer armour gains a 2+ Armour Save. Models may also Run, even if they have the Heavy sub-type, but their Initiative is still calculated at -1.

### Aegis-Cataphractii Terminator Armour

*A rare, heavily modified variant of the Cataphractii pattern maintained exclusively by the armourers of Titan, incorporating sanctified silvered ceramite and psychically attuned shield capacitors.*

A model equipped with Aegis-Cataphractii Terminator Armour has a 2+ Armour Save and a 4+ Invulnerable Save, and gains the Adamantium Will (3+), Relentless and Shrouded (6+) special rules and the Heavy Unit Sub-type. A model equipped with Aegis-Cataphractii Terminator Armour may not make Run moves in the Shooting phase, nor may it make Sweeping Advances in the Assault phase.

### Aegis Dreadknight Armour

A model equipped with Aegis Dreadknight armour has a 2+ Armour Save and a 5+ Invulnerable Save. Models equipped with Aegis Dreadknight armour gain the Bulky (6) special rule. Models may also Run, even if they have the Heavy sub-type, but their Initiative is still calculated at -1. In addition, any Adamantium Will save it possesses is improved to Adamantium Will (4+).

### Aegis Power Armour

A model equipped with Aegis Power armour gains a 3+ Armour Save. Models may also Run, even if they have the Heavy sub-type, but their Initiative is still calculated at -1.

### Aegis Tartaros Armour

Aegis Tartaros armour provides a 2+ Armour Save and a 5+ Invulnerable Save. Models equipped with Aegis Tartaros armour gain the Bulky (2) and Shrouded (5+) special rules. Unlike models equipped with Aegis Terminator armour, models wearing Aegis Tartaros armour do not have the Heavy sub-type and may make Reactions, Sweeping Advances, Runs and Charges normally. A model equipped with Aegis Tartaros armour may not embark upon any Transport that prohibits models with the Bulky (2) special rule.

### Aegis Terminator Armour

*Terminator plate inscribed with wards and hexagrammic sigils, proof against both bolt and sorcery.*

A model equipped with Aegis Terminator armour has a 2+ Armour Save and a 5+ Invulnerable Save. Models equipped with Aegis Terminator armour gain the Bulky (2) special rule. Models may also Run, even if they have the Heavy sub-type, but their Initiative is still calculated at -1. In addition, any Adamantium Will save it possesses is improved to Adamantium Will (4+).

### Brotherhood Banner

*A sacred standard of the Brotherhood, carried into battle by its most honoured Paladin.*

As the bearer's datasheet states.

### Icon of Justice

A model equipped with an Icon of Justice gains a 5+ Invulnerable Save. If the model already has an Invulnerable Save, improve the value of its Invulnerable Save by 1, to a maximum of 4+.

### Narthecium

*The Apothecary's medical gauntlet.*

A unit that includes one or more models equipped with a Narthecium gains Feel No Pain (5+) and It Will Not Die (6+).

### Paladin Banner

A unit that includes one or more models equipped with a Paladin Banner gains the Line Unit Sub-type. Friendly units with the Legiones Astartes (Grey Knights) special rule within 12" of one or more models equipped with a Paladin Banner may re-roll failed Morale Checks and Pinning Checks. In addition, a unit that includes one or more models equipped with a Paladin Banner adds +1 to its Combat Resolution score.

### Personal Teleporter

*A short-ranged teleporter that lets an Interceptor blink across the battlefield.*

A personal teleporter counts as a jump pack, using the official 2.0 Jump Pack rules.

### Psybolts

Storm bolters carried by models equipped with Psybolts gain the Nemesis Force special rule.

### Psyk-out Grenades

*Grenades filled with dust ground from a null-crystal, agonising to psykers and daemons.*

Psyk-out grenades count as frag grenades. In addition, enemy psykers and Daemons charged by the bearer's unit, or charging it, do not gain the Initiative bonus for charging†.

### Purity Seals

A model equipped with Purity Seals may re-roll failed Adamantium Will rolls of 1.

### Rosarius

*An amulet that projects a protective conversion field.*

A model with a rosarius has a 4+ Invulnerable Save.

### Teleport Harness

*A heavy teleport array that lets a Dreadnought or Dreadknight strike from orbit.*

A model equipped with a Teleport Harness gains the Deep Strike special rule.

### Titan Shield

*The ancient storm shield carried by the Supreme Grand Master.*
