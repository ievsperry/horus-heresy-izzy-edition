# Legio Custodes Horus Heresy 2.0 - Homebrew Conversion

Oct 26, 2026 · @Isaac

## Introduction

This conversion adapts the Legio Custodes section of Liber Imperatus v1.2 for Horus Heresy 2.0. It expands the official Legio Custodes list with new HQ choices, Chambers of the Golden Host, Shield-Hosts, new units and extra wargear options for existing units. It is used alongside the official Legio Custodes army list, not instead of it.

The Custodians rarely massed in numbers during the Great Crusade, gathering only around the Emperor or on narrow missions such as watching the Primarchs. The Heresy changed that, and the Legio assembled its Shield-Hosts to break Horus' ascendancy.

## Army-wide Special Rules

Every special rule unique to this expansion is defined here; datasheets list them by name. Core and official Legio Custodes rules (Legio Custodes, Nemesis and so on) are listed in the Rules Checklist.

### Auramite Talon

When deployed onto the battlefield (either at the start of the battle or when arriving from Reserves), all models with this Special Rule in a unit must be placed within unit coherency, but afterwards operate independently and are not treated as a unit. Once a unit with this Special Rule has separated, each model counts as its own unit for the purposes of Victory Points, Units Destroyed, and similar rules.

### Auric Guardians

While a unit with at least one model with this Special Rule is within 3" of a friendly Infantry model with the Independent Character Special Rule and without the Legio Custodes Special Rule, any Wound allocated to that Independent Character (including from Precision Shots (X), Precision Strikes (X) or Sniper) must instead be allocated to a valid model in this unit.

### For the Future of All Mankind

A model with this Special Rule may only be selected in an army that includes an Allied Detachment. If it is selected as part of an Allied Detachment, it loses the Support Squad Special Rule.

### Gravitic Repulsion

Whenever a charge is declared against a model with this Special Rule, the Charging unit suffers a -2 penalty to their Charge roll.

### Master of the Shield-Hosts

A model with this Special Rule is required to select a single Shield-Host upgrade. However, no model may take more than one such upgrade, and no model in your army may select the same Shield-Host upgrade option as another. Each Shield-Host upgrade must be paid for at a cost in points, indicated as part of the army list entry for the unit. Shield-Host upgrades may provide Special Rules, Wargear, Unit Sub-types, or other effects; these are noted in their description, along with any other benefits or restrictions. In all cases, the Shield-Host's description will indicate all changes, benefits and restrictions. See Shield-Hosts of the Legio Custodes.

### Moiraides Strike

A model with this Special Rule, and any unit it joins during Deployment, gains Crusader and Deep Strike and must be assigned to a Deep Strike Assault.

### Shining Fleet

A Detachment that includes a model with this Special Rule must have more of its Infantry units starting the game Embarked on a Transport than not. Models with the Independent Character Sub-type are not counted.

### Skilled Rider

*A lifetime in the saddle allowed the most skilled to fire their arsenal at full effectiveness, even on the move.*

A model with this Special Rule may opt to Run in the Movement Phase and still fire a single ranged weapon in the Shooting Phase.

These shots are made at BS 2. The To-Hit rolls for these shots cannot be improved by any source.

Note that if a unit makes use of this Special Rule, they may not claim the benefits of the Firing Protocols (X) Special Rule in the same Game Turn.

### Slayer of Kings

A model with this Special Rule gains the Preferred Enemy (Independent Characters) Special Rule, but this Special Rule is not conferred to any model that does not have the Slayer of Kings Special Rule.

### Sudden Strike (X)

In a turn in which a model with this Special Rule Charges, it adds X to its Initiative until the end of the Assault phase. It gains no benefit after a Disordered Charge.

### Taloned Onslaught

A model with this Special Rule, and any unit it has joined, gains Fleet (1), Move Through Cover and Pathfinder while the model remains part of that unit.

### Vaults of Rython

A model with this Special Rule may select a Custodian Guard Squad as a Retinue Squad, with this model as its Leader.

- The Retinue Squad does not use a Force Organisation slot and is part of the same unit as its Leader.
- It must be deployed with its Leader, who may not voluntarily leave it.
- Its models must exchange their Guardian Spears for Tempest Spears at +10 points each.

### Walkers in Shadow

An army containing a model with this Special Rule may not include an Allied Detachment, and the model may not be selected in one.

## Chambers of the Golden Host

A Primary Detachment with the Legio Custodes Sub-faction may use one Chamber of the Golden Host, chosen during army selection. Optional and Allied Detachments may not. Unless a Chamber says otherwise, its Effects and Limitations apply to every model and unit in that Detachment.

### Chamber of the Golden Host: Hykanatoi

*The heavy infantry heart of the Legio, including the Sentinel Guard and the Hetaeron Companions: the Emperor's will made absolute.*

Effects

- Custodian Host-Masters are a 0-2 choice in this Detachment instead of 0-1.
- Custodian Guard, Hetaeron Guard and Sentinel Guard Squads in this Detachment gain Counter-Attack (1) and Crusader while holding or contesting an Objective Marker. No effect in missions without Objective Markers.

Limitations

- The Detachment must include at least one Custodian Host-Master.
- The Detachment may not include more Vehicle and Dreadnought units than units without those Unit Types.
- If you control fewer Objective Markers than your opponent at the start of your turn, Legio Custodes models in this Detachment may only use Nemesis against enemy units controlling or contesting an Objective Marker until the start of your next turn. No effect in missions without Objective Markers.

### Chamber of the Golden Host: Kataphraktoi

*The jetbike riders and jump-harnessed hunters of the Legio, the Emperor's heavy cavalry.*

Effects

- Custodian Venatari Squads may be taken as Troops and gain the Line Sub-type when they are.
- Agamatus Jetbike Squadrons and Dawneagle Jetbike Squadrons may be taken as non-Compulsory Troops.
- If the army's Warlord is from this Detachment, it must take the Kataphraktoi Charger Warlord Trait instead of Golden Exemplar, and no other.

Limitations

- The Detachment's Compulsory HQ must be a Custodian Agamatii, Venataris or Vertoii Shield Captain.
- The Detachment may not include any models in Allarus or Aquilon Terminator Armour.
- The Detachment may not include Heavy Support choices or Dedicated Transports without the Antigrav Sub-type.

### Chamber of the Golden Host: Moritoi

*The honoured dead, entombed in Dreadnought sarcophagi and woken when the Emperor's fury is needed.*

Effects

- Contemptor-Achillus and Contemptor-Galatus Dreadnoughts may be taken as Troops.
- Sagittarum Guard Squads may be taken as Troops and gain the Line Sub-type.
- One Contemptor-Achillus or Contemptor-Galatus Dreadnought may be the Compulsory HQ. It must be upgraded to an Honoured Ancient (+30 points), must be the army's Warlord, and must take the Ancient of War Warlord Trait and no other. An Honoured Ancient has an Iron Halo and the Eternal Warrior Special Rule.

Limitations

- Compulsory Troops must be Contemptor-Achillus and/or Contemptor-Galatus Dreadnoughts.
- Each Dreadnought in this Detachment is worth +1 Victory Point to the opponent when destroyed.
- The Detachment may not include more Fast Attack and Heavy Support choices combined than Troops choices.
- The Detachment may never Seize the Initiative.
- An army whose Primary Detachment uses this Chamber may not take Fortifications or a Lord of War Detachment.

### Chamber of the Golden Host: Tharanatoi

*Siege and boarding specialists clad almost entirely in Allarus and Aquilon Terminator Armour.*

Effects

- Allarus Terminator Squads and Aquilon Terminator Squads may be taken as Troops.
- Allarus and Aquilon Terminator Squads taken as Compulsory Troops with no more than 5 models may buy Deep Strike for +20 points per model.
- Allarii and Aquilus Shield Captains may buy Deep Strike for +30 points each.
- If the army's Warlord is from this Detachment, it must take the Wrath of the Tharanatoi Warlord Trait instead of Golden Exemplar, and no other.

Limitations

- The Detachment's Compulsory HQ must be a Custodian Allarii or Aquilus Shield Captain.
- Compulsory Troops must be Allarus and/or Aquilon Terminator Squads, and each is worth +1 Victory Point to the opponent when destroyed.
- The Detachment may not include any Cavalry models.
- The Detachment may include only one Fast Attack choice.

## Wargear

Wargear defined in Liber Imperatus. Other wargear uses its official rules; see the Rules Checklist.

### Allarus Terminator Armour

Allarus Terminator Armour gives a 2+ Armour Save and a 5+ Invulnerable Save.

### Auric Shackles

A model with Auric Shackles adds +2 to its Wounds total when working out who won a Challenge. If its unit slays the enemy Warlord with a Melee attack or Sweeping Advance, the Controlling Player gains +1 Victory Point.

### Custodes Dawneagle Jetbike

*Introduced to the Custodes armoury toward the latter half of the Great Crusade, these bikes sacrificed the raw speed of the Agamatus for both better performance over rough terrain, and ease of construction.*

A Custodes Dawneagle Jetbike has Two Lastrum Storm Bolters.

In addition, a model with a Custodes Dawneagle Jetbike that chooses to Run gains the Shrouded (5+) Special Rule until the start of the Controlling Player's next turn.

A model that is equipped with a Custodes Dawneagle Jetbike must change its Unit Type to Cavalry (Antigrav, Skirmish), if it does not already have this Unit Type and Sub-types, keeping any other Unit Sub-types it had.

Additionally, the model must change its Movement Characteristic to 14", and gains the Hammer of Wrath (1), Skilled Rider, Firing Protocols (2) and Deep Strike Special Rules.

### Helical Targeting Array

The Controlling Player may activate it at the start of any of their turns, or before the opponent's first turn if they are not going first. Until the start of their next turn:

- The model may not move, Run, or make Reactions other than Interceptor or Overwatch.
- Its Shooting Attacks may use Skyfire.
- Shrouded Damage Mitigation rolls may not be taken against its attacks.
- Its Interceptor Reactions cost no Reaction point; this may exceed three Reactions in a phase but not one per phase for the unit.
- A Vehicle with it may fire all its weapons, not only Defensive Weapons, when making Interceptor.

### Praesidium Shield

A Praesidium Shield adds +1 to the model's Invulnerable Save (to a maximum of 3+), or gives a 6+ Invulnerable Save if it has none. The model cannot gain bonus attacks for two melee weapons or attack with Two-Handed weapons in the Assault phase.

### Tarsus Buckler

Shooting Attacks against a unit in which most models have a Tarsus Buckler suffer -2 Strength (minimum 1).

### Tenebris Praetoria

Shooting Attacks against a unit with a Tenebris Praetoria count the range as 6" further than it is. Units with Night Vision, Template weapons and Primarchs ignore this. Barrage weapons always scatter against the unit as if it were out of line of sight.

### Vexillum Imperatus

- Friendly Legio Custodes units within 12" of the model count as Leadership 10 for Morale checks and Pinning tests.
- Once per game, a friendly Legio Custodes unit within 12" may re-roll a failed Leadership test.
- All models in the unit gain the Line Sub-type while the model with the Vexillum Imperatus remains part of it.
