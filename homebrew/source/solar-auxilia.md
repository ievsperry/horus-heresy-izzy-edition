# Solar Auxilia Horus Heresy 2.0 - Homebrew Conversion

Oct 27, 2026 · @Isaac

## Introduction

This conversion adapts the Solar Auxilia section of Liber Imperatus v1.2 for Horus Heresy 2.0. It adds Warlord Traits, Cohort Doctrines, Armoured Marshal upgrades, new Tercios, new units and extra wargear options for existing units. It is used alongside the official Solar Auxilia army list in Liber Imperium, not instead of it.

The Solar Auxilia were the elite of the Imperial Army: void-hardened cohorts drilled to a standard few mortal regiments matched, fighting in close order beside the Legions across the length of the Great Crusade and the Heresy that followed.

## Army-wide Special Rules

Every special rule unique to this expansion is defined here; datasheets list them by name. Core and official Solar Auxilia rules (Tercio, Cohort Doctrines, Field Officer and so on) are listed in the Rules Checklist.

### Advanced Weapons

A unit with this Special Rule adds +1 Strength (to a maximum of 4) to its Lasrifles, Laspistols and Rotor Cannons.

### Artillery Spotters

Instead of using a Cognis-Signum it has bought, a unit with this Special Rule may grant its benefit to one unit from the same Tercio with at least one model within 6" of this unit.

### Attack Wing

When a unit with this Special Rule is deployed (at the start of the battle or from Reserves), all its models must be placed in unit coherency. After that, each model operates independently and counts as its own unit for Victory Points, Units Destroyed and similar rules.

### Auxilia Rangers

A unit that includes any models with this Special Rule may not join or be joined by any model without it. It never counts as a Scoring or Denial unit, whatever other rules apply, but its models may re-roll failed Shrouded Damage Mitigation rolls.

### Battle-Automata

For every model in a Detachment with this Special Rule that also has a Cortex Controller, the army may include one Thallax Cohort or Castellax Battle-Automata Maniple (Liber Mechanicum) as an Elites choice. A Thallax Cohort taken this way may not take a Dedicated Transport.

### Battle Psyker

A model with this Special Rule must select one Core Psychic Discipline: Biomancy, Divination, Pyromancy, Telekinesis, Telepathy or Thaumaturgy.

### Blitz Barrage

Once per game, before any Shooting Attacks in one of its Controlling Player's Shooting phases, a unit with a model with this Special Rule may declare a Blitz Barrage. That phase, it may fire each of its Ordnance weapons twice. The second Shooting Attack may target a different unit but otherwise follows the normal rules.

### By His Sanction Alone

A model with this Special Rule may only be taken in a Detachment that also contains the army's Warlord, and only in games of 2,500 points or more.

### Concentrated Fire (Advanced Reaction)

Available to a unit with an Armoured Fist Marshal. It uses a point of the Reaction Allotment as normal.

- Trigger: an enemy unit makes a Shooting Attack targeting this unit.
- After all To Hit, Armour Penetration, Invulnerable Save and Damage Mitigation rolls, but before lost Hull Points or Vehicle Damage results are applied, the reacting unit makes a Shooting Attack at the triggering unit.
- Each model may fire all its Defensive Weapons and up to one Battle Weapon. Template weapons may only be used if the target is within 8", and must use Wall of Death.

### Countdown to Launch

A Deathstrike Missile may never be fired normally. Instead, in its Controlling Player's Shooting phase, roll a D6 and add the current turn number. On 7 or more it launches.

- The warhead has no Range: place its Blast marker anywhere on the battlefield, then scatter it, reducing the scatter by the firer's unmodified BS.
- Once fired, the missile counts as having suffered an irreparable Weapon Destroyed result for the rest of the game. It cannot otherwise be Weapon Destroyed, and cannot be fired by another player through any effect.

### Crew-Served Weapon

A model with this Special Rule may Embark on a Transport with Infantry Transport despite having Bulky (X), but its Bulky value still counts against Transport Capacity.

### Defender

A unit hit during an Overwatch Reaction by a weapon with this Special Rule suffers -1 To Hit on its attacks in the following Assault phase.

### Destructor

Each Wound or Hull Point allocated from a weapon with this Special Rule becomes D6 Wounds or Hull Points (2D6 against Knights, Titans, Gargantuans, Super-Heavy Vehicles, Buildings, Fortifications and Monstrous models), each with the same AP and rules and each saved separately. Damage beyond a model's remaining Wounds or Hull Points is lost.

### Emplaced Position

A unit that includes any models with this Special Rule may only make the Return Fire and Overwatch Reactions and the Interceptor Advanced Reaction.

### Exposed Plasma Conduits

Each time a model with this Special Rule suffers a Penetrating Hit, add +1 to all its rolls on the Vehicle Damage table.

### Eye of the Storm

Hits on models under the central 5" marker of an Apocalyptic Mega-Blast from a weapon with this Special Rule gain Destructor. Any unit hit by any part of the marker permanently suffers -1 Toughness for the rest of the game.

### Follow-Up Attack

Before the game, one Infantry unit without Bulky (X) from the same Detachment may be assigned to each Hades Drill as if Embarked on it, whatever its size.

- When the Drill arrives by Subterranean Assault, place a Blast (3") marker directly beneath it. The marker is Difficult Terrain for the rest of the game, and the Drill gains Shrouded (5+) for that turn.
- In any later Movement phase of its Controlling Player, the assigned unit may deploy as if disembarking, measuring from any point of the marker. It may move up to its Movement and may Run.
- It cannot be targeted by Interceptor in the first Movement phase it could deploy, and may still deploy if the Drill has been destroyed.
- If there is not enough room, it stays in Reserves and arrives next turn.

### Forward Defences

Before the game, the Controlling Player may buy one Defence Line for each Solar Auxilia Pioneer Section without a Dedicated Transport, at its normal cost. It uses no Force Organisation slot and may be upgraded normally. If taken, the Pioneer Section must deploy on the table and may not be held in Reserves.

### Furibundus Talon

When a unit with this Special Rule is deployed (at the start of the battle or from Reserves), all its models must be placed in unit coherency. After that, each model operates independently and counts as its own unit for Victory Points, Units Destroyed and similar rules.

### Gravitic Implosion

Instead of rolling To Wound, a non-Vehicle model hit by a weapon with this Special Rule must roll under its Strength on 2D6 (double 6 always fails) or suffer a Wound. Armour Saves and Damage Mitigation rolls may be taken, except Shrouded. Against Armour Values, roll 4D6 for Armour Penetration. If the weapon also has Blast, the marker's area stays as Difficult and Dangerous Terrain until the start of the attacker's next turn.

### Heavy Beam

Draw a 1" wide line from the barrel to the weapon's full range. The closest model in it must be an enemy model.

- Every model in the line (friend or foe, except the firer and Flyers) suffers one Hit; each unit takes one Hit per model in the line.
- A Terrain piece, Building, Vehicle or model with 6 or more Wounds blocks the beam and suffers 1+D3 Hits instead of 1.
- If a Transport suffers a Penetrating Hit from it, each unit Embarked suffers D6 Str 4, AP - Hits with Deflagrate, allocated by its controller.

### Heavy Walker

A model with this Special Rule may not Embark on any model without the Transport Bay Special Rule.

### Immobile

A model with this Special Rule may never move, Run, Charge, Fall Back or make Reactions that move it. If it would be forced to move, it is destroyed.

### Impact Flash

Any unit with line of sight to any part of the final Blast marker of an attack with this Special Rule must take a Blind test.

### Independent Fire Control

After a model with this Special Rule (or a unit made entirely of such models) resolves a Shooting Attack, any weapons that did not fire may make Secondary Shooting Attacks, all together or split between different targets. Each weapon fires only once, normal range and line of sight apply, and these attacks suffer -1 To Hit. Instead, in a turn it remained Stationary or moved at Combat Speed, it may make Shooting Attacks that Shooting phase as if it had the Super-Heavy Sub-type.

### Inexorable

A unit made up only of models with this Special Rule ignores Leadership modifiers on Morale checks and Pinning tests, except those from Fear (X) and the Corrupted and Anathema Sub-types. A unit with both Fearless and Inexorable uses Fearless.

### Launch System Failure

If a model with this Special Rule loses its last Hull Point before it has fired its Deathstrike Missile, it suffers Catastrophic Damage as if it were Super-Heavy and explodes, regardless of anything else. It cannot then also suffer an Explodes result.

### Light Command Tank

A model with this Special Rule is bought as a separate unit, but during deployment must be assigned to another unit from the same Tercio (the Controlling Player chooses if several qualify) rather than deploying alone. Once assigned, it is part of that unit and may never leave it.

### Plasma Burn

If a Vehicle loses one or more Hull Points to a weapon with this Special Rule, roll a D6. On a 4+ it loses D3 more Hull Points, with no Cover Saves or Damage Mitigation rolls allowed.

### Ponderous Aim

A weapon with this Special Rule cannot be fired as part of a Reaction or used to make Snap Shots.

### Seismic Shock

A unit that loses any Wounds or Hull Points to a weapon with this Special Rule halves its Movement and may not Run or make Reactions until the end of the attacker's next Shooting phase.

### Shieldbreaker

No Invulnerable Saves may be taken against damage from a weapon with this Special Rule, and it ignores Void Shields (X) and Flare Shields.

### Solar Auxilia Command Tank

A model with this Special Rule is bought as a separate unit, but during deployment must be assigned to a unit made entirely of Vehicles from the same Tercio (the Controlling Player chooses if several qualify). Once assigned, it is part of that unit and may never leave it. It may still shoot normally in a turn it uses its Cognis-Signum to give the rest of its unit +1 BS.

### Spotted!

A model with a weapon with this Special Rule gains Split Fire and ignores Night Fighting penalties when shooting, but enemy units ignore the 24" Night Fighting line of sight limit when shooting at its unit. Any unit hit by this weapon may be targeted by other units that Shooting phase ignoring the Night Fighting BS penalty and 24" limit.

### Techno-Arcana: Enginseer

While its unit contains any Servo-Automata, an Enginseer Adept's Battlesmith (X) improves to Battlesmith (4+).

### Thermal Runaway

Whenever a model with this Special Rule rolls to determine an explosion radius, add +3" to the result.

### Traitor

A model with this Special Rule may only be included in an army with the Traitor Allegiance.

### Tremor

A unit hit by a weapon with this Special Rule treats all terrain, even open ground, as Difficult and Dangerous Terrain until the end of its Controlling Player's next turn. For line of sight the weapon counts as having Barrage, but may still fire without line of sight in Zone Mortalis games.

### Unified Assault

During deployment, a unit may be split between the transports of a squadron with this Special Rule instead of one transport, up to their combined Transport Capacity.

- The squadron's models must stay in unit coherency (ignoring any rule that would separate them) until the passenger unit has disembarked from all of them.
- If any part of the passenger unit disembarks, all of it must disembark that phase, within coherency.
- If one transport suffers a Crash and Burn result, everyone inside it is destroyed; the rest of the unit must still disembark in coherency. No Leadership tests are caused by models removed this way.

### Volatile Plasma Containment

If a model with this Special Rule loses its last Hull Point to Gets Hot, it also suffers an Explodes result. Whenever it suffers an Explodes result, the Hits it causes are Strength 10.

### Vortex

Once an attack with this Special Rule is resolved, leave its Blast markers in place for the rest of the battle as Vortex markers. Each is Area Terrain with these effects:

- No line of sight may be drawn into, out of or through it.
- A model that enters it, or begins its Controlling Player's Movement phase inside it, suffers one Str 10, AP 1 Hit with Instant Death and Armourbane (Melee). A Vehicle suffering a Penetrating Hit from this adds +3 to its Vehicle Damage roll.
- It grants no Cover Save, and models inside it may not use Cover Saves from any other source.

## Cohort Doctrines

These are extra Cohort Doctrines, usable by any Detachment that includes a model with the Cohort Doctrines Special Rule (or can upgrade one to have it).

### Cohort Doctrine: Apostle Pattern Cohorts

*Traitor cohorts that gave themselves wholly to the Primordial Annihilator, trading discipline for mutation and possession.*

Effects

- Possessed Auxilia may be taken as Troops.
- Mutated Spawn may be taken as Fast Attack choices (see the Imperialis Militia conversion).
- Auxilia Battle Psykers may take the Anathemata Psychic Discipline instead of a Core Psychic Discipline.
- Any unit made up entirely of Infantry, Cavalry, Dreadnought or Mechanised models may gain the Corrupted Sub-type for +25 points per unit, losing Close-Order if it had it. Attached models such as Medicae are upgraded separately.
- Any Character may exchange a Power Weapon for a Tainted Axe, Tainted Blade or Tainted Maul for +5 points each.

Limitations

- The Detachment must have the Traitor Allegiance.
- It may include no more than one Auxilia Armoured Tercio and no more than one Auxilia Artillery Tercio.

### Cohort Doctrine: Echoes of Lost Glory

*Cohorts raised from compliant worlds that kept their own exotic arms, watched closely for any sign of wavering loyalty.*

Effects

- All units in the Detachment gain Advanced Weapons.
- Any Infantry unit without the Monstrous Sub-type may replace its Void Armour or Reinforced Void Armour with Heavy Void Armour for +30 points per unit.
- Any model may exchange a Blast Pistol for a Needle Pistol (free), or a Heavy Bolter for a Needle Cannon (+5 points each) or a Volkite Culverin (+10 points each).
- Any Infantry model may exchange a Volkite Charger for an Assault Needler (+2 points each), and any model may exchange a Rotor Cannon for a Needle Cannon (+10 points each). If taken, every model in the unit must make the same exchange.

Limitations

- The army's Warlord must have the Cohort Doctrines Special Rule.
- Every Tercio in the Detachment that can include a Command Section of any kind must do so.
- If the Warlord is removed as a casualty, every unit in the Detachment not locked in combat must take a Morale check. Units that cannot take or automatically pass Morale checks (Vehicles, Fearless and so on) ignore this.

### Cohort Doctrine: Legion-Sworn Cohorts

*Cohorts drawn from Legion homeworlds or bound to a Legion by long service in the same Expeditionary Fleet.*

Effects

- The Detachment counts as one Level of Alliance higher with one chosen Space Marine Legion Faction.
- Any model may exchange a Lasrifle for a Bolter for free, or a Laspistol for a Bolt Pistol for +2 points each.

Limitations

- Only an Allied Detachment may take this Cohort Doctrine.

### Cohort Doctrine: Linebreaker Pattern Cohorts

*Exosuit and Dreadnought spearheads sent to shatter the toughest lines of resistance.*

Effects

- Solar Auxilia Furibundus Dreadnought Talons and Solar Auxilia Extremis Boarding Rig Sections may be taken as Troops.
- Those taken as Compulsory Troops gain the Line Sub-type.

Limitations

- The Detachment may not include Auxilia Armoured Tercios, Solar Auxilia Macharius Heavy Tank Squadrons, Macharius Omega Heavy Tanks or Praetorian Heavy Tank Squadrons.
- The Detachment may not take Fortifications.
- An army whose Primary Detachment uses this Doctrine may not roll to Seize the Initiative.

### Cohort Doctrine: Pioneer Companies

*Pathfinders who ranged ahead of the cohort to seize forward positions and clear obstacles.*

Effects

- Solar Auxilia Pioneer Sections gain the Line Sub-type and Infiltrate.
- Auxilia Infantry Support Tercios made up only of Solar Auxilia Ancillary Command Sections and/or Pioneer Sections may be taken as Compulsory Troops.
- All units in an Auxilia Veletaris Tercio gain Scout.
- Aethon Heavy Sentinel Squadrons may be taken as non-Compulsory Troops.
- If the Primary Detachment uses this Doctrine, the player may re-roll the roll for first turn and the roll to Seize the Initiative.

Limitations

- The Detachment may not take Fortifications, except those bought in place of Dedicated Transports for Pioneer Sections.
- The Detachment may include no more than one Auxilia Armoured Tercio.

### Cohort Doctrine: Scourge Pattern Cohorts

*Cohorts trained and equipped to scour whole worlds clean of xenos taint.*

Effects

- Solar Auxilia Hellhound Flame Tank Squadrons may be taken as non-Compulsory Troops.
- Any model may exchange a Flamer or Heavy Flamer for a Toxiferran Flamer for free.
- Any Character may take Rad Grenades for +10 points.
- Any Auxilia Rapier Carrier with Quad Launchers may take Phosphex Canister Shot for +20 points each.
- Any Auxilia Malcador Infernus may replace its Centreline Mounted Inferno Cannon with a Centreline Mounted Chem Destroyer for +25 points each.

Limitations

- Only a Primary Detachment may take this Doctrine, and the army may not include an Allied Detachment.
- The Detachment may not take Fortifications.

### Cohort Doctrine: Steel Fury Pattern Cohorts

*Cohorts built around super-heavy tanks, every other element there to support the behemoths.*

Effects

- The Detachment must take a Lord of War in place of a Primarch on the Crusade Force Organisation Chart. It gains Cohort Doctrines, must be the army's Warlord regardless of any other rule, and must take the Super-Heavy Armoured Legate Warlord Trait.
- Solar Auxilia Macharius Heavy Tank Squadrons may be taken as Elites.

Limitations

- Every Lords of War Detachment in the army must have the Solar Auxilia Sub-faction.
- The Detachment must contain more Vehicle units than non-Vehicle units.

### Cohort Doctrine: Valkyria Pattern Cohorts

*Well-supplied cohorts that used gunships and assault carriers to insert whole Tercios and then stay on station as close air support.*

Effects

- Solar Auxilia Aello Gunship Squadrons may be taken as Heavy Support.
- Units that may take an Aurox Transport may take an Arvus Transport instead.
- Units that may take a Chimera Transport may take an Auxilia Aello or Auxilia Kelaino instead.
- Flyer Transport Vehicles from this Detachment in Reserves may automatically arrive at the start of their first turn without a Reserves roll. If they do not, they roll for Reserves normally from turn two.

Limitations

- Every unit made up entirely of Infantry must start the battle Embarked on a Transport or in Reserves.

## Armoured Marshals

An Armoured Marshal is an upgrade for a Vehicle with the Imperial Army (Solar Auxilia) Faction and Sub-faction. No model may take more than one, but several models may each take one, within each upgrade's own limits. An upgrade uses no Force Organisation slot. A Marshal bought for a Lord of War counts towards that slot's cost for the 25% Lords of War and Primarch limit. Show it with a commander model in a cupola or hatch.

*Commanders famed for the precision with which they wielded their vehicles' firepower.*

## Wargear

Wargear defined in Liber Imperatus. Other wargear uses its official rules; see the Rules Checklist.

### Ambulator Frame (Ireton MaSade)

An Ambulator Frame gives a 2+ Armour Save and It Will Not Die (5+).

### Auspex Surveyor

A unit with at least one model with an Auspex Surveyor makes Shooting Attacks with Ignores Cover, and:

- Enemy models may not Infiltrate within 24" of the model.
- The unit ignores the 24" line of sight limit and the -1 BS penalty from Night Fighting.
- When an enemy unit arrives from Reserves, the unit may make the Interceptor Advanced Reaction without spending a Reaction point. This may exceed three Reactions in a phase but not one per phase for the unit.

### Auxilia Tempestor Land Speeder

An Auxilia Tempestor Land Speeder has a Multi-Laser and a Havoc Launcher. A model with one that Runs gains Shrouded (5+) until the start of its Controlling Player's next turn.

### Battle Servitor Control

A model with Battle Servitor Control gains Sunder.

### Boarding Shield

A Boarding Shield gives a 5+ Invulnerable Save. It does not stack with other Invulnerable Saves (choose one) but can be improved by rules such as Cyber-Familiar. The model cannot gain bonus attacks for two melee weapons or attack with Two-Handed weapons in the Assault phase.

### Breacher Charges

In the Assault phase, a model with Breacher Charges that is Engaged with or in base contact with a Building or Fortification may, instead of attacking normally, make one automatic Str 10, AP 2 Hit against it at Initiative step 1. Any model doing so may not use any other weapon, rule or wargear to inflict Hits or Wounds that phase, but may still Sweeping Advance.

### Command Vox

- A friendly unit with a Command Vox may use the Leadership of any model in another friendly unit with a Command Vox for Pinning tests and Morale checks, even if that unit is in Reserves or Embarked.
- A Command Vox also benefits friendly units with a Vox Interlock.
- While at least one model with a Command Vox is on the battlefield and not Embarked, the Controlling Player may re-roll Scatter rolls (for attacks or deployment) if that model has line of sight to the target unit or point.

### Cortex Controller

Units including Cybernetica models ignore Programmed Behaviour while within 12" of a friendly model with a Cortex Controller.

### Extremis Warsuit (Ireton MaSade)

Ireton MaSade with an Extremis Warsuit uses his Mechanised profile, exchanges his Archaeotech Pistol for a Rotary Bolt Cannon, and gains Fleet (2), Inexorable, Move Through Cover and the Light and Mechanised Sub-types.

### Geo-Locator Beacon

While a model with a Geo-Locator Beacon is deployed on the battlefield, the Controlling Player may re-roll failed Reserves rolls.

### Helical Targeting Array

The Controlling Player may activate it at the start of any of their turns, or before the opponent's first turn if they are not going first. Until the start of their next turn:

- The model may not move, Run, or make Reactions other than Interceptor or Overwatch.
- Its Shooting Attacks may use Skyfire.
- Shrouded Damage Mitigation rolls may not be taken against its attacks.
- Its Interceptor Reactions cost no Reaction point; this may exceed three Reactions in a phase but not one per phase for the unit.
- A Vehicle with it may fire all its weapons, not only Defensive Weapons, when making Interceptor.

### Psi-Jammer

Enemy models within 18" and line of sight suffer -2 Leadership on Psychic checks (not cumulative; one Psi-Jammer at a time). The model and any unit it joins gain Adamantium Will (5+).

### Psychic Hood

Enemy models within 18" and line of sight of a model with a Psychic Hood suffer -2 Leadership on Psychic checks. This is not cumulative, and no model suffers penalties from more than one Psychic Hood.

### Rad Grenades

In a turn in which a unit with at least one model with Rad Grenades Charges or is Charged, models in the enemy unit(s) suffer -1 Toughness (minimum 1) until the end of the following Fight sub-phase. This affects Instant Death thresholds.

### Shroud Bombs

Shooting Attacks against a unit with at least one model with Shroud Bombs count the range as 6" further than it is. Enemy models with Night Vision and Primarchs ignore this. Barrage weapons always scatter against the unit as if it were out of line of sight.

### Siege Retrofit

A model with a Siege Retrofit loses the Fast Sub-type, has Front Armour 14, and has a Flare Shield at no cost (it may not buy one from any other source).

### Suspensor Web

A Heavy weapon with a Suspensor Web may be fired as an Assault weapon at targets within half its maximum range (e.g. a 36" Heavy 4 Heavy Bolter can fire as 18" Assault 4). Range-based Special Rules such as Armourbane (Melta) still use the weapon's original range.

### Verticalis Drop Harness

A model with a Verticalis Drop Harness gains Deep Strike, and may disembark from a Zooming Flyer, or a Flyer moving at Cruising Speed, regardless of other rules.

### Vox Disruptor Array

At the start of each Game Turn, declare whether each Vox Disruptor Array in your army is on or off. While any model on the battlefield (friend or foe) has one turned on, every Deep Strike Assault, Drop Pod Assault, Area Denial Drop and Subterranean Assault that turn is Disordered on a 1-3 instead of a 1.
