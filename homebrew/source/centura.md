# Liber Centura for The Horus Heresy 2.0 — Homebrew Conversion

Nov 1, 2026 · @Isaac

## Introduction

This doc restructures *Liber Centura: The Awakened Armoury* (v1.6, released 30 May 2023 by the Liber Panoptica Development Team) into the same layout as the other homebrew conversion docs. Liber Centura is already written for The Horus Heresy 2.0, so the rules are carried over as written rather than converted.

Liber Centura adds new units and options for all Legiones Astartes armies, plus Legion-specific units, characters, Rites of War and wargear. You still need the Horus Heresy: Age of Darkness rulebook, the Liber faction books, and the Warhammer Community PDFs to use it. Page references to other books are kept as the source gives them.

If Games Workshop releases official rules for any unit or item here, the official rules take priority unless both players agree otherwise. The Liber Panoptica team recommends using its FAQ, Errata and Balance Changes alongside these rules.

Liber Centura is unofficial and not for profit.

## Legiones Special Rules

These rules work alongside the special rules in *Liber Astartes*, *Liber Hereticus* and the Horus Heresy: Age of Darkness rulebook.

**Afterburner.** *Colossal secondary combustors give this flyer a surprising burst of speed.* Once per game, at the start of the Movement phase, a model with this special rule may fire its Afterburner. If it does, it may make one additional move after its normal move, up to its normal Movement. That Movement value cannot be modified in any way.

**Air Defence Screen.** *Batteries of interlinked anti-air weapons can deny whole areas of sky to the foe.* When deployed before the game, each model with this special rule is placed as a separate unit and is treated as a separate unit for the rest of the game. A model with this special rule must target the closest enemy Flyer in range, if there is one; otherwise it may target other units as normal.

**Airburst Munitions.** *These munitions fill the sky with scything shrapnel to bring down airborne targets.* A weapon with this special rule may only target models with the Flyer or Antigrav Sub-type, or models that activated a jump pack, jet pack or wings in the opponent's previous Game Turn, and can never hit any other kind of target. It may hit Flyers with its Blasts, even though this is normally prevented.

**Automata Attendants.** *Clustered around their charge, the Servo-Automata will give their synthetic lives to protect them.* A Legion Servo-Automata Maniple may only be taken in a Detachment that includes a model with the Techmarine Covenant special rule, and never in an army without one. It is a Retinue Squad, and that model is its Leader; if there are several, the controlling player picks one. The Retinue Squad does not use a Force Organisation slot, counts as part of its Leader's unit, must deploy with its Leader, and the Leader may not voluntarily leave it.

**Caestus Prow.** *The armoured fore of this craft is a masterwork of layered armour, shield generators and reinforced bulkheads.* A model with this special rule may make a Ramming attack as if it were a Tank, whatever its Unit Type. Declare it at the start of the Movement phase, after deciding whether to fire its Afterburner. The Ramming hit is resolved at Strength 10, AP3 and adds +1 to Vehicle Damage table rolls, or +2 if the model fired its Afterburner this turn. The model also has a 5+ Invulnerable Save against attacks on its Front Armour, including damage from ramming or being rammed. It is immune to Armourbane (Melta): no extra D6 may be rolled for armour penetration against it.

**Combat Transporter.** *The weight of its cargo makes it hard to deploy countermeasures.* While a Legion Storm Eagle Transporter carries a Vehicle or any Dreadnoughts in its Auxiliary Vehicle Bay, reduce its Countermeasures Save rolls by 1.

**Debilitating.** *These weapons leave painful, disfiguring wounds that make their targets easier prey.* A unit hit by a weapon with this special rule has its Strength reduced by 1 until the end of the controlling player's next Game Turn, to a minimum of 1. This does not stack with other Debilitating attacks, but does stack with other rules that reduce Strength.

**Dreadnought Magna-Grapples.** *Magnetic clamps let any vehicular cargo be carried.* Instead of a vehicle in its Auxiliary Vehicle Bay, a Legion Storm Eagle Transporter may carry up to two Legion Contemptor Dreadnoughts, Contemptor-Cortus Dreadnoughts, Castra Ferrum Dreadnoughts, Contemptor Centurions or Castra Ferrum Centurions, in any combination. They embark and disembark as normal for an Auxiliary Vehicle Bay, but do not gain Assault Vehicle when disembarking.

**Mindless Aggression.** *Mutated beyond recognition, these creatures keep one trait: the desire to kill.* A unit with this special rule must charge in the Assault phase if it can, choosing its target if several are available, and must always attempt a Sweeping Advance. Each time a model with this special rule attacks, roll a D6 to find how many attacks it makes.

**Misericordia.** *Inertial suppression clamps lock transported troops in place.* A model with this special rule can carry models in any type of Legion Terminator armour, including Legion-specific Terminator units, that have Bulky (2) as if they did not have Bulky (2).

**Neverborn.** *The soul of these creatures is more daemon than man.* A unit with this special rule may be taken in a Legiones Astartes (X) Detachment with the Traitor allegiance, even though it does not have Legiones Astartes (X).

**Open-Topped (X).** *Open hulls let occupants fire at the cost of protection.* A unit embarked on a Transport with this special rule may fire up to X ranged weapons, measuring from the Transport. If it does, it counts as having moved, which may stop some weapon types firing. Rules that let a unit count as stationary for shooting (such as Relentless or Legiones Astartes (Death Guard)) do not apply while it is embarked. Rolls on the Vehicle Damage table against a model with this special rule add +1.

**Paired Weapons.** *Some weapons must be used together to be fully effective.* A model armed with a weapon with this special rule gains +1 Attack.

**Pater Consularis.** *For the greatest Legion leaders there is no rest; even in death they are expected to serve.* A model with this special rule must select exactly one Pater Consularis upgrade. If it is slain, your opponent gains one additional Victory Point. Your army may include only one model with this special rule.

**Reactor Overcharge.** *When a little more power could mean life or death, some are willing to take the risk.* Once per game, in the controlling player's turn, a model with this special rule may overcharge its reactor. It first suffers D3 Wounds with no saves or Damage Mitigation rolls allowed. If it survives, until the start of the controlling player's next turn it adds 2" to its maximum Move and Charge distances, adds +1 Attacks and +1 Initiative, and its atomantic repulsor's Invulnerable Save becomes 5+. If it is removed as a casualty while overcharged, its atomantic repulsor's automatic hits are resolved at 6".

**Relic (X).** *The loss of an ancient artefact, legendary icon or bearer of a Legion's legacy is a catastrophic blow to morale and a triumph for the foe.* If a unit with this special rule is destroyed by any means, the opposing player immediately scores X additional Victory Points. These are in addition to any Victory Points normally scored for destroying the unit (such as Price of Failure, Slay the Warlord or mission objectives), and are scored separately for each such unit destroyed.

**Saturnine Terminator Armour.** A model in Saturnine Terminator armour has a 2+ Armour Save and a 4+ Invulnerable Save, and adds 1 to its Toughness (already included in the profiles). It gains the Heavy Sub-type and the Bulky (3), Firing Protocols (2) and Slow and Purposeful special rules, and is immune to the Concussive special rule.

**Synthetica Mindlock.** *With no will of their own, these automatons revert to basic programming when unaccompanied.* A unit with this special rule may make Reactions, ignoring the Automata Sub-type's restrictions, as long as it includes a Legion Techmarine.

**Tank Desant.** *Troops ride on this vehicle's armoured hull, guarding it as they go.* A model embarked on a model with this special rule may fire its ranged weapons as though it were not embarked, measuring range from the Transport's hull.

**Transport Skiff.** *Little more than an engine strapped to a grav-plate, skiffs have a useful place in the Legions' armouries.* A model with this special rule gains Infantry Transport and Assault Vehicle; for those rules and the Transport Sub-type, a Legion Whispercutter counts as a Vehicle. When it loses its last Wound, every embarked model suffers a Strength 6, AP - hit, allocated by the controlling player. Survivors are placed where the model was, or as close as possible, in coherency and at least 1" from enemy models; any that cannot be placed are removed as casualties. The unit must then take a Pinning test.

**Tremor.** *These weapons burrow into the earth instead of arcing through the sky.* A unit hit by a weapon with this special rule treats all terrain, including open terrain, as Difficult and Dangerous Terrain until the end of its controlling player's next Game Turn. For line of sight, the weapon counts as having Barrage, and it may fire without line of sight even in Zone Mortalis games.

**Unstable Internment.** *Madness from low-quality cybernetics is slow to manifest, but irreversible once it does.* A model with this special rule must always attempt to charge or make a Sweeping Advance if it can, and can never count as Scoring.

**Volatile Ordnance.** *Prototype weaponry often malfunctions, but the field data is worth the risk.* All Heavy weapons on a model with this special rule have Gets Hot. While the squad includes at least one Havoc Master, it may ignore the first Gets Hot result it suffers each phase.

**Vox Override.** *These arrays blast data across many channels to break through the static.* While a model with this special rule is deployed on the battlefield, you may re-roll failed rolls to see whether a Reserve is Disordered.

**Warlord: Fist of the Legion.** *Legion Land Raider Dionysus only.* If chosen as the Warlord, it has this Warlord Trait and no other. Friendly units within 24" with line of sight to it may use Leadership 9 for Morale and Pinning tests. The army gains one additional Reaction in the opponent's Movement phase while it has not been removed as a casualty.

**Whispercutter.** *Projectors cast false images and scatter-shrouds to hide the vehicle's true position.* A model with this special rule that moves at least half its Movement gains Shrouded (6+) until the start of its controlling player's next Movement phase.

## Rites of War

### Additional Unaligned Rites of War

Any Detachment with Legiones Astartes (X) that includes a model with Master of the Legion may use one of these instead of a Rite of War from *Liber Astartes* or *Liber Hereticus*. Legion-specific Rites of War are in each Legion's section.

#### Rite of War: Breacher Spearhead

*Void combat and hive warfare are brutal, claustrophobic affairs, where shield-walls of heavily armoured troops stride the darkened walkways.*

**Effects**

- A model with a boarding shield in base contact with another friendly model from its unit gains +1 to the boarding shield's Invulnerable Save, to a maximum of 4+.
- Legion Tactical Support Squads and Legion Veteran Squads may take boarding shields for +5 points per model. The whole squad must take them, and gains the Heavy Sub-type.
- Units with the Infantry (Heavy) Sub-type gain Relentless.
- Units with the Infantry (Heavy) Sub-type equipped with boarding shields gain Heart of the Legion.

**Limitations**

- Units made up entirely of Infantry (Heavy) with boarding shields must fill the Compulsory Troops choices. This does not override Support Squad.
- The Warlord must be an Independent Character equipped with a boarding shield.
- If the Primary Detachment uses this Rite of War, the army may not include any unit with a jump pack, jet pack, bike or jetbike.
- The Detachment must contain more Infantry units with the Heavy Sub-type than without it.
- An Allied Detachment may not use this Rite of War, and the Detachment may not take Fortifications.

#### Rite of War: Destroyer Company

*Shunned by their fellow Legionaries, Destroyer Companies carried forbidden weapons designed to scour worlds of life.*

**Effects**

- Legion Destroyer Assault Squads and Legion Mortalis Destroyer Squads may be taken as Troops.
- Any unit made up entirely of models with Bitter Duty, Cult of Morkai or Shadow of Death (excluding Independent Characters) may be taken as Troops.
- Any model with the Character Sub-type not wearing Terminator armour may take rad grenades for +10 points per model.
- Any model with a missile launcher or gravis missile launcher may also take rad missiles for +5 points per model.
- Independent Characters may buy Bitter Duty for +15 points per model.
- Each enemy Scoring or Denial unit destroyed by this Detachment while within control range of an objective gives the controlling player +1 Victory Point.

**Limitations**

- The Compulsory HQ choice must be a model with the Siege Breaker or Moritat Legiones Consularis upgrade (it need not be the Warlord). This may prevent some other characters being taken.
- If the Primary Detachment uses this Rite of War, the army may not include an Allied Detachment.
- An Allied Detachment may not use this Rite of War, and the Detachment may not take Fortifications.

#### Rite of War: Storm-Lance Tumen

*Favoured by the White Scars but known to almost every Legion, massed waves of bikes crash into the enemy like a cavalry charge of the old steppe clans.*

**Effects**

- Legion Outrider Squadrons and Legion Spatha Attack Bike Squadrons may be taken as Troops. Outrider Squadrons taken as Troops gain the Line Sub-type.
- Any model with a Legion Spatha attack bike gains Hammer of Wrath (2).
- Legion Hussar Squadrons gain Scout.

**Limitations**

- The Warlord must be equipped with a Legion Spatha combat bike.
- Vehicles must have the Flyer, Fast or Skimmer Sub-type.
- All units made up entirely of Infantry must begin the battle embarked on a model with both the Flyer and Transport Sub-types.
- The Detachment may not include Dreadnoughts or Automata.
- An Allied Detachment may not use this Rite of War, and the Detachment may not take Fortifications.

#### Rite of War: Tactica Obliqua

*Roboute Guilliman's late innovation, the forerunner of the Codex Astartes battle company, adopted by few beyond the Legions loyal to Terra.*

**Effects**

- The Detachment gains the Shifting Tides special rule.
- Legion Veteran Squads and Legion Veteran Assault Squads may benefit from any Shifting Tides doctrine. From Tactical Advance they gain Heart of the Legion for that turn, but not the range increase.

**Shifting Tides.** At the start of your turn you may activate one doctrine below; it lasts until the start of your next turn. Only one doctrine may be active each turn, and each may be used only once per game.

- **Ranged Devastation:** Legion Tactical Support Squads and Legion Heavy Support Squads gain +1 Ballistic Skill and Split Fire.
- **Tactical Advance:** Legion Tactical Squads increase the range of Heart of the Legion from 6" to 12" and gain Relentless.
- **Vicious Assault:** Legion Assault Squads, Assault Support Squads and Despoiler Squads gain +1 Weapon Skill and Furious Charge (1).

**Limitations**

- The Compulsory Troops choices must be filled by at least two Legion Tactical Squads at maximum size.
- The Detachment must include at least one Legion Assault Squad or Legion Despoiler Squad, and at least one Legion Heavy Support Squad.
- Only a Primary Detachment with the Loyalist allegiance may use this Rite of War.

### Modifications to Existing Unaligned Rites of War

These effects are added to the Rites of War in *Liber Astartes* and *Liber Hereticus*.

- **Angel's Wrath:** any unit that may take a Legion Land Raider Proteus Carrier as a Dedicated Transport may take a Legion Caestus Assault Ram instead.
- **Fury of the Ancients:** Legion Castra Ferrum Dreadnought Talons may be taken as Troops, including as Compulsory Troops, and gain the Line Sub-type when taken as Troops. Legion Contemptor-Cortus Talons may be taken as Troops. The Warlord may be any Dreadnought with the Character Sub-type, instead of a Legion Contemptor Dreadnought with the Venerable Ancient upgrade.
- **Pride of the Legion:** Legion Assault Veteran Squads may be taken as Troops, and gain the Line Sub-type when taken as Compulsory Troops. Saturnine Terminator Squads may be taken as Heavy Support.
- **Sky-Hunter Phalanx:** Legion Sky-Seeker Squadrons lose Support Squad.

## Legiones Consularis and Pater Consularis

### Legiones Consularis

A Legion Centurion, Cataphractii Centurion, Tartaros Centurion or Indomitus Centurion may take one of these instead of a Legiones Consularis upgrade from elsewhere. No Centurion may take more than one. Each upgrade grants special rules or wargear and may change the model's Sub-types or Characteristics. Legion-specific Consularis upgrades are in each Legion's section.

#### Legiones Consularis: Lodge Master (+30 points)

*The Warrior Lodges, born of Davinite custom, became the vehicle by which rebellion spread through the Legions.*

Only a Centurion with the Traitor allegiance may take this upgrade. The Lodge Master gains Preferred Enemy (Loyalists), Exemplar of Treason and I Can't Say.

**Exemplar of Treason.** The Lodge Master's Leadership is 10, and the Lodge Master and any unit it joins automatically pass Leadership tests.

**I Can't Say.** Before the game, its Detachment secretly notes one objective below, revealed only after the game. If the objective is not achieved by the end of the game, your opponent gains 1 Victory Point.

- **Hand of the Warmaster:** when totalling Victory Points at the end of the game, the Lodge Master counts as having the Line Sub-type for controlling objectives. If it holds an objective outside your deployment zone at the end of the game, you gain 1 Victory Point.
- **Killer of Gods and Kings:** if an enemy Primarch is slain by a model with the same Legiones Astartes (X) as the Lodge Master, you gain 1 Victory Point.
- **Mentor to All:** select a friendly Independent Character, other than the Lodge Master, from the same Detachment. If it survives the game, you gain 1 Victory Point.

#### Legiones Consularis: Terran-Born Crusader (+30 points)

*The last of those who fought beside the Emperor to retake Terra, the Imperial Truth etched into their souls.*

Only a Centurion with the Loyalist allegiance may take this upgrade. The Terran-Born Crusader gains Blood of a Thousand Worlds and In the Light of the Emperor, and may give one of its weapons Master-Crafted at no cost.

**Blood of a Thousand Worlds.** The Terran-Born Crusader and any unit it joins gain Preferred Enemy (Traitor) and Stubborn.

**In the Light of the Emperor.** The Terran-Born Crusader gains Adamantium Will (5+). Units with the Corrupted or Daemon Sub-type within 12" of it suffer -1 Leadership for Morale tests. When working out the turn-based bonuses or penalties of a Daemon unit, any such unit within 12" of it counts the Game Turn as one higher than it is.

### Pater Consularis

Legion Contemptor Centurions and Legion Castra Ferrum Centurions have the Pater Consularis special rule, so must take exactly one upgrade below. Each upgrade's cost is on the datasheet. Each grants special rules or wargear and may change the model's Sub-types or Characteristics.

**Pater Consularis: Armistos.** *Gunnery masters who still perfect their weapons from within an armoured chassis.* All of the model's ranged weapons gain Master-Crafted. It gains an augury scanner and a signum-primus at no cost.

**Pater Consularis: Chaplain.** *The burning hatred and conviction of the first Chaplains sustains them even in un-life.* The model gains Hatred (Everything!) and its Leadership becomes 10. At the start of your turn it may give one friendly unit within 3" Hatred (Everything!) and Stubborn until the start of your next turn. If it has a power weapon, it may give that weapon Master-Crafted at no cost.

**Pater Consularis: Forge Lord.** *Martian-trained warriors who embrace their new power over technology.* The model gains Battlesmith (4+), Master of Automata, Firing Protocols (2) and Legiones Thallaxes, and a machinator array at no cost. It may take a cortex controller for +15 points and/or a cyber-familiar for +15 points.

**Pater Consularis: Herald.** *Reborn standard-bearers, figures of dread to their enemies.* The model gains Fear (2) and a Legion standard at no cost. At the start of your turn it may give one friendly unit within 3" the Legion standard's bonuses until the start of your next turn.

**Pater Consularis: Librarian.** *Battle-psykers whose powers became a potent part of their new arsenal.* The model gains the Psyker Sub-type and one Core Psychic Discipline: Biomancy, Divination, Pyromancy, Telekinesis, Telepathy or Thaumaturgy, or a Legion-specific Discipline if it has the matching Legiones Astartes (X). It may replace a gravis power fist with a gravis force weapon at no cost, and may take a psychic hood for +15 points.

**Pater Consularis: Master of Signals.** *Signallers whose new bodies can run complex sensoria and comms equipment.* The model gains Strategic Comms and must replace one gravis power fist with a full-spectra comms array at no cost.

**Pater Consularis: Siege Breaker.** *Masters of destruction who can deal damage on a scale they could only dream of in life.* The model gains Sunder and Art of Destruction, and a nuncio-vox and a phosphex discharger at no cost. Any model whose upgrade is unlocked by a Legiones Consularis: Siege Breaker may also unlock it from a Pater Consularis Siege Breaker in the same Detachment. At the start of your turn it may let one friendly unit within 3" ignore the Night Fighting -1 Leadership penalty until the start of your next turn.

## Legiones Wargear

These items work alongside the wargear in *Liber Astartes* and *Liber Hereticus*. Liber Centura's own Legion Saturnine Terminator Armour is replaced by the Saturnine Terminator Armour special rule from the *Saturnine Terminators and Dreadnought* conversion doc.

**Aspectum-Pattern Occularis Array.** The bearer has Night Vision. Enemy models targeted by its Shooting Attacks suffer -1 to Cover Saves against those attacks.

**Atomantic Dispersion Shield.** The bearer has a 5+ Invulnerable Save, which can never be improved by wargear, special rules or psychic powers. When it loses its last Hull Point, its explosion radius increases by 3".

**Atomantic Repulsor.** The bearer has a 6+ Invulnerable Save. A bearer with a Wounds Characteristic that suffers an unsaved Instant Death wound loses D3 Wounds instead of being removed. When it loses its last Wound or Hull Point, before it is removed or replaced with a Wreck, every model within D3+3", friendly or enemy, suffers an automatic hit at Strength 8, AP -.

**Battle Servitor Control.** The model gains Sunder.

**Deredeo Atomantic Pavaise.** The bearer has a 4+ Invulnerable Save against Shooting Attacks; this does not replace the effects of an atomantic deflector. Friendly Infantry wholly within 4" of the Deredeo's base gain a 6+ Invulnerable Save against Shooting Attacks, or +1 to an existing Invulnerable Save, to a maximum of 4+. This has no effect in close combat and does not stack with other effects that improve an Invulnerable Save, including other pavaises.

**Frag Launchers.** A charge against a unit with frag launchers counts as a Disordered Charge.

**Full-Spectra Comms Array.** Counts as an augury scanner, nuncio-vox and vox disruptor array, and gives the bearer Night Vision. In the Shooting phase, the bearer may give one friendly unit within 3" +1 Ballistic Skill for that phase; this does not stack with a cognis-signum. At the start of your turn, a Pater Consularis Master of Signals may let one friendly unit within 3" ignore the Night Fighting -1 Leadership penalty until the start of your next turn.

**Hyperios Command Platform.** While it is on the table, the bearer gives Split Fire to all Hyperios air-defence launchers in its unit.

**Legion Estoc Jetbike.** Has a twin-linked bolter and an oracle array. A model with it that Runs gains Shrouded (5+) until the start of the controlling player's next turn. An Infantry model that takes one becomes Cavalry (Antigrav), keeping its other Sub-types; its Movement becomes 16 and it gains Firing Protocols (2), Battle-Hardened (1) and Hammer of Wrath (1).

**Oracle Array.** Counts as an augury scanner. At the start of the controlling player's Shooting phase, a unit with an oracle array may designate one enemy unit within 12" and line of sight. Until the start of the controlling player's next turn, friendly units with the same Legiones Astartes (X) add +1 to To Hit rolls against it in the Shooting phase. This does not stack, and designating counts as a Shooting Attack for the whole unit.

**Signum-Primus.** In the Shooting phase, the bearer may give one friendly unit with the same Legiones Astartes (X) within 3" +1 Ballistic Skill for that phase. This does not stack with a cognis-signum.

**Skytracker Augury.** The bearer may always shoot while Claimed, even when unmanned, and ignores the 24" line of sight limit and the Night Fighting Ballistic Skill penalty. When an enemy unit arrives from Reserves, a unit with a skytracker augury may make the Interceptor Advanced Reaction (rulebook, page 309) without spending a Reaction point; it still may not react more than once per phase, but may exceed the three-Reaction limit for that phase.

**Tarantula Deployment.**

- **Concealed Positions:** the unit has Shrouded (4+) until it first fires any of its weapons.
- **Drop Capsules:** the unit has Deep Strike.
- **Forward Deployment:** the unit has Scout, and cannot be placed into Reserves.

**Teleportation Transponders.** The unit counts as having Deep Strike.

### Legion-Specific Wargear

These items may only be used by models with the matching Legiones Astartes (X).

**Angelus Pattern Jump Pack (Blood Angels).** An Independent Character with Legiones Astartes (Blood Angels) that could take a Legion Warhawk Jump Pack may take this instead for +25 points. At the start of the controlling player's Movement phase, or when the unit arrives by Deep Strike Assault, the model may activate it: its Movement becomes 14 for that turn, with every benefit of that value, including the Charge bonus. While activated it may move over Impassable Terrain, vertical terrain and other models, but may not end within Impassable Terrain or within 1" of models from another unit; it treats Difficult Terrain as Dangerous and must take a Dangerous Terrain test if it starts or ends in Dangerous Terrain. It may still Run after activating, adding its Initiative to 14 and ignoring terrain and models, but then may not shoot or charge that turn. It may not be activated during a Reaction. The bearer gains Bulky (3), Deep Strike and Hammer of Wrath (2), and counts as having a Legion Warhawk Jump Pack for rules that affect that wargear.

**Fleshmetal Plating (Volk-Sa'ra'ram, Iron Warriors).** Gives a 2+ Armour Save and a 4+ Invulnerable Save. An unsaved Instant Death wound makes him lose 2 Wounds instead of being removed.

**Morphogenic Weaponry (Volk-Sa'ra'ram, Iron Warriors).** At the start of each of the controlling player's turns, choose two different ranged weapons (Shrapnel Cannon, Heavy Flamer, Volkite Culverin, Autocannon, Plasma Cannon, Multi-Melta, Lascannon) and one melee weapon (Chainfist, Graviton Maul, Two Lightning Claws, Power Lance). He may not choose the same selections two turns in a row.

**The Basilisk's Raiment (Omegon, Alpha Legion).** Gives a 2+ Armour Save and a 4+ Invulnerable Save, and adds +1 to Omegon's Cover Saves.

**The Chirurgeon (Fabius Bile, Emperor's Children).** Gives Feel No Pain (4+) and It Will Not Die (4+). Fabius makes 3 additional attacks in the Assault phase with The Chirurgeon's profile.

**The Death Mask (Barabas Dantioch, Iron Warriors).** Gives Fear (2) against units containing models with Legiones Astartes (Iron Warriors).

**The Shackles (Shiban Khan, White Scars).** Gives Feel No Pain (4+), and models attacking Shiban Khan treat his Weapon Skill as 4.
