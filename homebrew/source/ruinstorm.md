# Daemons of the Ruinstorm for The Horus Heresy 2.0 — Homebrew Expansion

Nov 13, 2026 · @Isaac

## Introduction

This expansion adds new units, Daemonic Assassins, Ætheric Blessings and Rites of War to the official Daemons of the Ruinstorm army list. It is used alongside the official list, not instead of it.

Daemons of the Ruinstorm are always part of the Traitor Alliance. Every unit, Daemonic Assassin, Ætheric Blessing and Rite of War here that names an Ætheric Dominion may only be taken in an army whose Ætheric Dominion is that Dominion (or, where its entry says so, any one under Formless Distortion).

## Special Rules

### Daemons of the Ruinstorm

A unit with this special rule belongs to the Daemons of the Ruinstorm, is part of the Traitor Alliance and has the Traitor special rule. It may only be taken in a Detachment of the Daemons of the Ruinstorm.

### Daemonic (X)

A model with this special rule has an Invulnerable Save of X.

### Master of the Ruinstorm

A Ruinstorm Daemon Sovereign or Ruinstorm Daemon Harbinger is treated as having the Master of the Legion special rule in every way: it may be the army's Warlord, it lets its Detachment select a Rite of War, and it counts toward the number of models with Master of the Legion an army may include.

### Harbinger of Execution

A model with this special rule occupies an HQ Force Organisation slot, may not be selected as the army's Warlord, and does not count toward the minimum number of Compulsory HQ choices required in a Detachment. A Detachment may only include a Daemonic Assassin whose Ætheric Dominion matches the Detachment's chosen Dominion (or any single Daemonic Assassin if the Detachment is aligned to Formless Distortion).

### Hunter of Champions

When resolving close combat attacks or Shooting Attacks against a unit containing at least one model with the Character or Unique Unit Sub-type, a model with this special rule re-rolls all failed To Wound rolls and gains +1 to its Combat Resolution score if it slays an enemy Character in a Challenge.

### Soul Blaze

At the end of each turn, roll a D6 for each enemy unit that suffered one or more unsaved wounds from a weapon with Soul Blaze that turn: on a 4+, the unit suffers D3 Strength 4, AP 5 hits with the Ignores Cover special rule.

### Melta

A weapon with this special rule rolls an additional D6 for Armour Penetration when the target is within half its maximum range.

### Paired Weapon

A model armed with this weapon gains +1 Attack, as if it were armed with two close combat weapons.

## Ætheric Blessings of the Ruinstorm

*As the Warp saturates realspace, daemonic entities draw sustenance and twisted power from the specific emotional and metaphysical currents of their patron Ætheric Dominion. By manifesting an Ætheric Blessing, a daemonic pack or behemoth attunes itself to the deeper resonances of the Empyrean.*

Any unit with the Daemon Unit Type (or Corrupted Engine Unit Sub-type) may purchase one Ætheric Blessing matching the Ætheric Dominion of its Detachment (or any single Ætheric Blessing if the Detachment has the Formless Distortion Dominion). Unless a unit's datasheet states otherwise, a Blessing costs +5 points per model for units of 1-Wound models, +15 points per model for units of 2–4 Wound models, and +30 points per model for 5+ Wound models and Walkers.

### Heedless Slaughter

Models in a unit with this Ætheric Blessing gain the Rage (2) and Furious Charge (1) special rules, and may re-roll failed Charge Distance rolls.

### Putrid Corruption

Models in a unit with this Ætheric Blessing gain the Feel No Pain (5+) special rule (or improve an existing Feel No Pain roll by +1, to a maximum of 4+), and enemy units locked in close combat with them suffer a -1 penalty to their Toughness Characteristic (to a minimum of T 1) when resolving To Wound rolls in the Assault phase.

### Infernal Tempest

Models in a unit with this Ætheric Blessing improve their Daemonic Invulnerable Save by +1 (to a maximum of 4+), and all ranged weapons and Psychic Weapons used by models in the unit gain the Rending (6+) special rule (or improve an existing Rending value by +1, to a maximum of 4+).

### Rapturous Sensation

Models in a unit with this Ætheric Blessing gain a +1 bonus to their Initiative Characteristic, gain the Fleet (2) special rule, and inflict Rending (5+) on all close combat attacks (or improve an existing close combat Rending value by +1, to a maximum of 4+).

### Encroaching Ruin

All Melee weapons and ranged weapons used by models in a unit with this Ætheric Blessing gain the Armourbane (Melee) or Sunder special rule (as appropriate to the weapon type), and enemy units within 6" of one or more units with this Blessing reduce their Cover Saves by -1 (to a minimum of 6+).

### Malevolent Artifice

Models in a unit with this Ætheric Blessing gain a +1 bonus to their Armour Save (or gain a 4+ Armour Save if they do not possess one) and gain the It Will Not Die (5+) special rule.

### Ravenous Dissolution

Models in a unit with this Ætheric Blessing gain the Hatred (Everything) special rule, and their Melee weapons gain the Murderous Strike (6+) special rule (or improve an existing Murderous Strike value by +1, to a maximum of 4+).

### Formless Distortion

At the start of each of the controlling player's turns, before any units are moved, select one of the following mutations for each unit with this Ætheric Blessing until the start of the controlling player's next turn. Razor-Limbs: models in the unit gain +1 Attack in the Assault phase. Chitinous Carapace: models in the unit gain +1 Toughness (to a maximum of T 8). Distorted Silhouette: models in the unit gain the Shrouded (5+) special rule.

## Rites of War

When selecting an army with the Daemons of the Ruinstorm faction trait, the controlling player may select a single Rite of War for their Primary Detachment, provided the Detachment's Warlord meets all listed Prerequisites. Only one Rite of War may be selected per Detachment, and all Effects and Limitations apply for the duration of the battle. "Lesser Daemon Swarms" means both Ruinstorm Lesser Daemons and Ruinstorm Daemon Swarms.

### Tide of the Empyrean

*Where the veil between realspace and the Immaterium is torn widest, reality drowns beneath an endless, surging ocean of warp-spawn. Driven forward by the chanting of Daemonic Heralds and towering Sovereigns, these chittering, clawing multitudes overwhelm mortal battlelines through sheer, suffocating numbers.*

**Prerequisites**

- A Detachment using this Rite of War must include a Ruinstorm Daemon Sovereign or a Ruinstorm Harbinger as the army's Warlord.

**Effects**

- Endless Multitude: Ruinstorm Lesser Daemon Swarms and Ruinstorm Warp-Spawn Packs in a Detachment using this Rite of War may increase their maximum unit size to up to 20 models (at their standard points cost per additional model).
- Surging Tide: Ruinstorm Warp-Spawn Packs and Ruinstorm Daemon Beasts in a Detachment using this Rite of War do not have the Support Squad special rule (if applicable) and may be selected as Troops choices, gaining the Line Unit Sub-type if selected as Compulsory Troops.
- Drawn Through the Breach: Whenever a Ruinstorm Lesser Daemon Swarm, Ruinstorm Warp-Spawn Pack, or Ruinstorm Daemon Beast unit is completely destroyed, the controlling player may place an identical replacement unit into Deep Strike Reserves on a D6 roll of 4+ (this replacement unit may not carry Ætheric Blessings or character upgrades, and each unit may only be replaced once per game).
- Chorus of Ruin: Friendly units with the Daemon Unit Type within 12" of a Ruinstorm Herald Conclave or Ruinstorm Harbinger gain the Stubborn special rule and add +1" to their Run and Charge Distance rolls.

**Limitations**

- A Detachment using this Rite of War may not include any Lords of War choices (such as Ruinstorm Arch-Daemons), nor may it include more than one Heavy Support choice (such as Ruinstorm Daemon Behemoths, Cataclysm Brute Packs, or Soul-Forge Engines).
- A Detachment using this Rite of War must include at least three Troops choices.
- All Compulsory Troops choices in a Detachment using this Rite of War must be Ruinstorm Lesser Daemon Swarms or Ruinstorm Warp-Spawn Packs.

### Phalanx of Abominations

*Where the fabric of reality fractures under immense psychic strain, towering legions of hulking Daemon Brutes and mutated abominations stride forth from the breach. Shrugging off heavy bolter fire and cannon shells with unnatural resilience, these lumbering walls of warp-forged muscle and bone crush everything in their path.*

**Prerequisites**

- A Detachment using this Rite of War must include a Ruinstorm Daemon Sovereign or a Ruinstorm Harbinger as the army's Warlord.

**Effects**

- Vanguard of Monstrosities: Ruinstorm Daemon Brutes may be selected as Troops choices in a Detachment using this Rite of War. Any unit selected as a Compulsory Troops choice in this manner gains the Line Unit Sub-type.
- Cataclysmic Fire Support: Ruinstorm Cataclysm Brute Packs may be selected as Elites choices in addition to Heavy Support choices in a Detachment using this Rite of War.
- Unstoppable Juggernauts: Every unit composed entirely of models with the Daemon Unit Type and a base Wounds Characteristic of 3 or 4 (including Ruinstorm Daemon Brutes, Cataclysm Brute Packs, and Flesh-Reaper Cavalry) in a Detachment using this Rite of War gains the Stubborn and Heart of the Legion special rules (granting Feel No Pain (6+)—or improving an existing Feel No Pain roll by +1, to a maximum of 4+—while within 3" of an Objective marker).
- Crushing Advance: Models in a Ruinstorm Daemon Brute or Cataclysm Brute Pack unit gain the Hammer of Wrath (1) special rule (resolved at their unmodified Strength Characteristic and AP 3) in any turn in which they make a successful Charge.

**Limitations**

- A Detachment using this Rite of War may not include any models with a base Wounds Characteristic of 1 (such as Ruinstorm Lesser Daemon Swarms or Ruinstorm Warp-Spawn Packs).
- A Detachment using this Rite of War may not select any Fortification choices, and may not include an Allied Detachment.
- All Compulsory Troops choices in a Detachment using this Rite of War must be Ruinstorm Daemon Brutes.

### Screaming Host of the Skies

*Descending from boiling warp-storms high in the upper atmosphere, winged horrors, manta-like sky-rays, and soaring greater daemons darken the heavens. Striking with blinding aerial speed, they tear apart gunships, flank armoured columns, and slaughter isolated infantry before vanishing back into the clouds.*

**Prerequisites**

- A Detachment using this Rite of War must include a Ruinstorm Daemon Sovereign (equipped with Wings / Flyer or Jump mobility) or a Ruinstorm Harbinger (equipped with Wings / Jump mobility) as the army's Warlord.

**Effects**

- Terrors of the Upper Air: Ruinstorm Sky-Ray Packs and winged Ruinstorm Daemon Beasts (such as Furies or Screamers) may be selected as Troops choices in a Detachment using this Rite of War. Any unit selected as a Compulsory Troops choice in this manner gains the Line Unit Sub-type.
- Swooping Predators: Every model in a Detachment using this Rite of War with the Flyer Unit Type, the Jump Unit Sub-type, or the Sky-Hunter special rule gains the Hit & Run and Fleet (2) special rules (or improves an existing Fleet value by +1).
- Aerial Ambush: The controlling player may add +1 to all Reserves rolls made for units arriving via Deep Strike or Outflank in a Detachment using this Rite of War, and units arriving via Deep Strike may re-roll their scatter distance dice.
- Raking Talons: Models with the Flyer Unit Type or Jump Unit Sub-type in this Detachment add +1 to their Hammer of Wrath and Slashing Dive attack rolls.

**Limitations**

- Every unit in a Detachment using this Rite of War must possess the Flyer Unit Type, the Jump Unit Sub-type, the Sky-Hunter special rule, or be joined by a character with one of those rules. Ground-bound units that cannot fly or skim (such as Ruinstorm Daemon Brutes, Cataclysm Brute Packs, Flesh-Reaper Cavalry, and Soul-Forge Engines) may not be included in the Detachment.
- A Detachment using this Rite of War may not select any Fortification choices.
