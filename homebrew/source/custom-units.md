# Liber Izonian for The Horus Heresy 2.0 — Custom Units

Nov 12, 2026 · @Isaac

## Introduction

Liber Izonian collects the custom units made for this project. Each unit is written into the official catalogues by `homebrew/add_custom_units.py`, with its source kept as `homebrew/source/custom-units.md`. A unit uses the official datasheet it is based on — the same models, characteristics, wargear, options and Dedicated Transports — unless its entry says otherwise.

## World Eaters

### The Red Fangs (Heavy Support) — 130 pts

*Based on the Legion Heavy Support Squad: the same models, characteristics, wargear, options and Dedicated Transports unless noted below.*

Every model has a Wounds Characteristic of 2.

**Unit Composition**

- 1 Legion Support Sergeant
- 4 Legionaries

The unit is always five models; it may not include additional Legionaries.

**Wargear**

- Heavy bolter (Wrathful Souls), on every Legionary

**Special Rules**

- Legiones Astartes (World Eaters): the unit may only be taken in a World Eaters army, of either Allegiance
- Bitter Duty
- Slow and Purposeful
- Wrathful Souls
- Dying Gift

**Options**

| Option | Cost |
| --- | --: |
| **The entire squad may exchange its heavy bolters for:** |  |
| – Autocannons | +5 points per model |

The Heavy Support Squad's other heavy weapons are not available.

**Wrathful Souls.** A unit with this special rule armed with heavy bolters gives them Rupture (6+); when the unit makes Shooting Attacks as part of an Overwatch Reaction, this becomes Rupture (4+). Against a unit with the Legiones Astartes (World Eaters) special rule of a different Allegiance, the Rupture (X) becomes Breaching (X) of the same value instead. In addition, its autocannons fire one additional shot (Heavy 3 instead of Heavy 2).

**Dying Gift.** Once per battle, at the start of the controlling player's Shooting phase, a unit with this special rule armed with heavy bolters or autocannons may elect to give those weapons the Gets Hot special rule for the phase and to improve their Rupture (X) or Rending (X) to Rupture (4+) or Rending (4+). A Wound suffered from Gets Hot while this is in effect is resolved at the improved AP: AP 3 for a weapon with Rupture and AP 2 for one with Rending.

| Autocannon (Wrathful Souls) | 48" | 7 | 4 | Heavy 3, Rending (6+) |
