# Liber Panoptica for The Horus Heresy 2.0 — Homebrew Conversion

Nov 2, 2026 · @Isaac

## Introduction

This doc restructures *Liber Panoptica: Operation Retrograde* (v5.3, released 24 December 2024 by the Liber Panoptica Developer Team) into the same layout as the other homebrew conversion docs. Liber Panoptica is already written for The Horus Heresy 2.0, so its rules are carried over as written rather than converted.

Unlike the faction supplements, Liber Panoptica is mostly a toolkit for the whole game:

- **Additional Missions:** Standard, Apocalypse and Zone Mortalis missions.
- **Additional Game Modes:** Decurion, Centurion and Praetorian, each with its own army construction, Reactions and missions.
- **Community FAQs, Errata and Balance Changes:** for the core rulebook, campaign books, the Liber faction books, Legacies of the Age of Darkness and Exemplary Battles.
- **Armoury of the Age of Darkness:** additional special rules, weapon profiles and Extrinsica melee weapons used across the Panoptica supplements.

You still need the Horus Heresy: Age of Darkness rulebook, the Liber faction books and the Warhammer Community PDFs to use it. If Games Workshop releases official rules that cover anything here, the official rules take priority unless both players agree otherwise.

Liber Panoptica is unofficial and not for profit.

## Additional Special Rules

The FAQs, Errata and Balance Changes in Liber Panoptica add these special rules to certain units. They work alongside the special rules in the official Games Workshop publications.

**Afterburner.** *Colossal secondary combustors give this flyer a surprising burst of speed.* Once per game, at the start of the Movement phase, the model may fire its Afterburner and make one additional move after its normal move, up to its normal Movement. That Movement value cannot be modified in any way.

**Armoured Superstructure.** *Approaching a Super-Heavy in bulk, some behemoths withstand blows that would destroy a lesser target.* Subtract 1 from any roll on the Vehicle Damage table against this model.

**Artillery Spotters.** *Spotters call targets for those under their command.* A unit with this special rule may give the benefit of a cognis-signum it has bought to any unit from the same Tercio with at least one model within 6" of it, instead of using the benefit itself.

**Augmetics (X).** *Extensive augmetics let some warriors survive grievous wounds.* Each time the model suffers an unsaved Wound, roll a D6; on X or more, the Wound is discounted. This is not a Saving Throw, so it can be used against attacks that allow no saves. Augmetics without a value counts as Augmetics (6+). This is a Damage Mitigation roll: a model may make only one Damage Mitigation roll of any type per Wound.

**Auto-Servo Tracking.** *Independent fire control lets this weapon track and fire without its operator.* A weapon with this special rule may fire at a different target from the model's other weapons.

**Born of Steel.** *More metal than flesh, this warrior walks among automata as one of their own.* The model may be given Patris Cybernetica at no cost. Decide at the start of the battle, before deployment; it cannot be changed.

**Brittle.** *The sharpened edge slices almost anything – unless it bites at a bad angle.* If the model hits with every attack made with this weapon in a single phase, the edge is blunted: at the end of that phase, the weapon's AP becomes 4 and it loses any Rending (X) for the rest of the battle.

**Broken Soul.** *For those who have given themselves to the darkness, inviting the evil within is a small step.* The model may be given the Corrupted Sub-type at no cost. Decide at the start of the battle, before deployment; it cannot be changed.

**Caestus Prow.** *The armoured fore of this craft is a masterwork of layered armour, shield generators and reinforced bulkheads.* The model may make a Ramming attack as if it were a Tank, whatever its Unit Type, declared at the start of the Movement phase after deciding whether to fire its Afterburner. The Ramming hit is resolved at Strength 10, AP3 and adds +1 to Vehicle Damage table rolls, or +2 if it fired its Afterburner this turn. The model has a 5+ Invulnerable Save against attacks on its Front Armour, including damage from ramming or being rammed, and is immune to Armourbane (Melta).

**Close-Quarters Boarding Protocol.** *The mortal factions of the Imperialis Militia often fought in the confines of a Zone Mortalis.* The unit may be chosen for a Detachment using the Zone Mortalis Engagement Force Organisation Chart despite having more than 15 models, but cannot be expanded above its starting size.

**Consul (X).** *Centurions often kept all the privileges of their former rank.* The model counts as having the named Legiones Consularis upgrade for all purposes, including Rites of War, special rules and wargear limits that require or forbid one in a Detachment. It gains none of that upgrade's benefits itself.

**Countermeasures.** *Speed, thermic flares or stranger means confound incoming fire.* The model has a 5+ Invulnerable Save against Shooting Attacks with Skyfire. If it does not make a Zoom move in the controlling player's Movement phase, it loses this benefit for the rest of that turn and the opponent's next turn.

**Crew-Served Weapon.** *Some heavy weapons had to be partially dismantled and carried by several troops.* The model may embark on models with the Transport Sub-type and Infantry Transport despite the Bulky (X) restriction, but its size still counts for Bulky (X).

**Daemonic Hordes.** *Unending multitudes of biting, gnawing creatures keep coming despite losing dozens of their number.* Each unsaved Wound from a Blast (any size) or Template weapon becomes two unsaved Wounds. The model cannot suffer Instant Death from an attack with Strength double its Toughness, though such attacks may not benefit from Feel No Pain (X) or Corrupted Resilience. Attacks with the Instant Death special rule work normally.

**Dimensional Edge (X).** *Exotic matter, caught in the right phase, passes straight through anything it touches.* A weapon with this special rule always wounds on X, and no Saves or Damage Mitigation rolls may be taken against its Wounds.

**Ejector Blowout.** *Runaway reactions and failed safety mechanisms can be explosively fatal.* A weapon with this special rule counts all Gets Hot results as AP2.

**Emperor's Chosen.** *A long and storied path awaits one marked by the Emperor himself.* When the model suffers an unsaved wound with Instant Death, it is not removed but loses D3 Wounds for each such wound instead.

**Exposed Compartment.** *Open crew bays make easy prey for weapons that exploit them.* Rolls on the Vehicle Damage table against this model add +1.

**Feeding Frenzy.** *Flailing tendrils snatch up the dead to be devoured, regenerating the creature's wounds.* At the end of an Initiative step in which an enemy model is slain in close combat by this model's attacks, roll a D6 for each model slain. On a 6, a model with this special rule in the attacking unit regains 1 Wound, up to its starting Wounds.

**Felling Blow.** *Trading speed for killing power.* When attacking in melee with this weapon, the model may halve its number of attacks; if it does, those attacks gain Murderous Strike (5+).

**Field Officer.** *Solar Auxilia officers are never far from the front line.* When the model joins a unit with the Close-Order Sub-type, it gains Close-Order while it remains in that unit.

**Gyrocoil Charge.** *Internal moving weights make for heavy blows at the cost of speed.* Attacks with this weapon suffer -2 Initiative.

**Hardwired Defences.** *Automated weapons follow their directives until destroyed.* A Fortification with this special rule may not be claimed by a player who did not select it as part of their army.

**Immobile.** *Fixed in place, these units must stand their ground or perish.* The model may not move for any reason, including Runs, Charges, Falling Back or Reactions that would move it. If forced to move, it is destroyed.

**Inertia Suppression Clamps.** *Inertial clamps lock transported troops in place, protecting them from impact.* The model may carry units made up entirely of Infantry as if their Bulky (X) were 2 lower, to a minimum of 1, for its own Transport Capacity only.

**Legiones Astartes Command Tank.** *Some Legionaries showed a proficiency in the cupola that none could approach.* The model is selected as a separate unit, but may join a unit made up entirely of Vehicles during deployment or the game as if it had the Character Sub-type and Independent Character, without gaining any other benefit from them. It may not join a unit containing a model with 6 or more Hull Points.

**Lords of the Ruinstorm.** *The greatest denizens of the warp are rare, and jealous in their command.* Only one model with this special rule may be included per 1,500 points in your army.

**Makeshift Weapon.** *Not every tool of the 31st millennium was meant for combat, but in a pinch it will serve.* A model attacking with this weapon may make only a single attack with it, at Initiative step 1, and may not gain bonus Attacks for charging or from any special rule.

**No Witnesses.** *There can be no witnesses if there are no survivors.* The model may never issue or accept challenges, and ignores effects that would force it to. It may never be chosen as the model that declines an opponent's challenge.

**Paired Weapons.** *Some weapons must be used together to be fully effective.* A model armed with a weapon with this special rule gains +1 Attack.

**Predator's Gaze.** *Survivors of an assassin's hunt speak of something unnatural in its gaze.* When the model issues a challenge, it chooses which eligible enemy character accepts.

**Psychic Mastery (X).** *This psyker pushes past limits that would doom others.* The model may manifest up to X Psychic Powers per phase, despite the normal limits. A power used as a Shooting Attack counts as one power, and the same power may not be used more than once per phase.

**Reinforced Shell.** *Built to survive the most horrendous damage.* When the model loses its last Hull Point, before resolving its Super-Heavy Explosion, roll a D6. On a 4+ it does not explode; its shell is treated as a ruined building instead of a wreck, with its two doorways counting as open access points.

**Riposte (X).** *The blade seems to be everywhere at once, always where it is needed.* Models attacking a model wielding a weapon with this special rule subtract X from their Weapon Skill.

**Rupture (X).** *Lighter targets are torn apart even by blows that cannot pierce the thickest armour.* Each To Wound roll of X or higher from a model or weapon with this special rule is resolved at AP3 instead of the weapon's normal AP. A weapon with both Rupture (X) and Gets Hot uses its base AP for Gets Hot wounds.

**Sensor Flare.** *Harsh light renders even advanced sensors useless.* A model hit by a weapon with this special rule must take an immediate Blind test.

**Shattered Legionary.** *Many Astartes were separated from their Legions and forced to band together.* This model may be included in a Shattered Legions Detachment that includes models of the same Legion as its Legiones Astartes (X). If it is, replace its Legiones Astartes (X) with Legiones Astartes (Shattered Legions); it must still represent that same Legion.

**Skilled Rider.** *A lifetime in the saddle lets the best fire their arsenal on the move.* The model may Run in the Movement phase and still fire one ranged weapon in the Shooting phase, at BS2; these To Hit rolls can never be improved. A unit that does this may not use Firing Protocols (X) that Game Turn.

**Soul-Death.** *This weapon inflicts the most terrible harm on those connected to the immaterium.* Against models with the Daemon, Psyker or Corrupted Unit Type or Sub-type, the weapon gains Fleshbane, Instant Death and Breaching (3+).

**Tank Desant (X).** *Troops ride on the vehicle's armoured hull, defending it directly.* A unit embarked on a Transport with this special rule may fire up to X ranged weapons, measuring from the Transport. If it does, it counts as having moved, which may stop some weapon types firing. Rules that let a unit count as stationary for shooting (such as Relentless or Legiones Astartes (Death Guard)) do not apply while it is embarked.

**The Black Shield.** *Escaping Istvaan III, Crysos Morturg harassed his Traitor brethren as Captain of the 108th Independent Company.* Crysos Morturg may be included in a Blackshields Detachment, replacing his Legiones Astartes (Death Guard) with Legiones Astartes (Blackshields). If he is the Warlord of a Blackshields Detachment, he gains Master of the Legion, and Death Guard Mortus Poisoner Squads may be included in that Detachment, also replacing their Legiones Astartes (Death Guard) with Legiones Astartes (Blackshields).

**Warp Resplendent.** *Some denizens of the immaterium reach an immense size, and their powers make others pale.* The model's Template weapons count as Hellstorm, and its Blast (3") weapons count as Large Blast (5"). Its other weapons double their Range.

## Additional Missions

### Standard Missions

These missions can be used alongside or instead of the rulebook missions. To pick one at random, choose six that appeal to both players and roll a D6.

#### Standard Mission Setup

Unless a mission says otherwise, every Standard Mission uses these steps:

- **The Armies:** each player builds an army with the standard rules and any one Force Organisation chart, to an agreed points limit.
- **Setting Up:** set up all scenery except Fortifications included in armies. Do not roll for a Deployment Map; use the one the mission names.
- **Deployment:** roll off; the winner chooses to deploy first or second. The first player picks a Deployment Zone and deploys everything except Reserves, including Fortifications, then the second player does the same.
- **First Turn:** the player who deployed first goes first, unless the opponent Seizes the Initiative.
- **Game Length:** after five turns, roll a D6. On 4+, a sixth and final turn is played.
- **Victory Conditions:** the player with the most Victory Points wins; if tied, the game is a draw.
- **Mission Special Rules:** Reserves and Night Fighting, plus any extra rules the mission lists.

#### Common Secondary Objectives

Missions list which of these they use. Where a mission gives a different reward, it says so.

- **Attrition:** the army that destroyed the most enemy units gains +1 Victory Point.
- **Big Game Hunter:** before the game, each player declares their single highest-cost unit. If it is destroyed, the opponent gains +1 Victory Point.
- **First Strike:** each unit completely destroyed during a player's first turn is worth +1 Victory Point to that player at the end of the game.
- **Ground Control:** at the end of the game, divide the board into quarters. Each quarter is worth +1 Victory Point to the player with more Scoring units in it (not counting units Falling Back); a tied quarter gives nothing.
- **Linebreaker:** each unit wholly within the opponent's Deployment Zone at the end of the game is worth +1 Victory Point.
- **Slay the Warlord:** a side that destroyed the enemy Warlord gains +1 Victory Point, or +2 if that Warlord was a Primarch.

#### Mission 1: Foothold

*'And so they clashed like thunderheads... but neither among them could gain a foothold.'*

- **Deployment Map:** Dawn of War.
- **Primary – Secure the Foothold:** divide the battlefield into four equal quarters. The player with the most Scoring units in a quarter controls it; a unit split between quarters counts for the one holding most of it, and can only control or deny one quarter at a time. If a unit is split evenly, its controller chooses at the end of their Movement phase. At the end of the game, each controlled quarter is worth +2 Victory Points, and each contested quarter (same number of units) +1 to each player.
- **Secondary:** Slay the Warlord, First Strike, Big Game Hunter.

#### Mission 2: Resource Collection

*'An army, no matter how strong, will fail without ammunition.'*

- **Deployment Map:** Clash of the Line.
- **Primary – Take the Resources:** after deployment, Infiltrators and Scout redeployments, each player places one Objective Marker in their own Deployment Zone, and one is placed at the centre. At the end of the game, controlling the marker in your own zone is worth +1 Victory Point, the centre +2, and the opponent's zone +3.
- **Secondary:** Ground Control, Linebreaker, Attrition.

#### Mission 3: Scorch and Burn

*'If you cannot have it for yourself, it is better to deny the same to your enemy.'*

- **Deployment Map:** Search and Destroy.
- **Primary – Deny the Enemy:** after deployment, the player who deployed first rolls D3+2; players take turns placing that many Objective Markers, the second-deploying player first. No more than one may be in either Deployment Zone. At the end of each player turn, each controlled marker is worth +1 Victory Point, kept even if the marker is later lost.
- **Secondary:** Slay the Warlord, First Strike, Attrition (+3 Victory Points in this mission), and **Burn the Witch:** the army that destroyed the most enemy Psykers gains +1 Victory Point.

#### Mission 4: Point Break

*'Often, before the siege itself comes, first come the barrages.'*

- **Deployment Map:** Ambush. Add extra walls, defence lines and similar around the Defender's Deployment Zone to show a dug-in position.
- **Deployment:** the player who deploys first is the Defender. Neither player may modify or re-roll Seize the Initiative.
- **Primary – Hidden Resources:** after deployment, players take turns placing five Objective Markers, the second-deploying player first. One is secretly marked so neither player knows which. At the end of the game, reveal it: its controller gains +3 Victory Points; if contested, the Defender counts as controlling it.
- **Secondary:** Big Game Hunter, First Strike, and **Cache Secured:** each other Objective Marker controlled at the end of the game is worth +1 Victory Point.

#### Mission 5: Anvil Strike

*'And so it was that here, the hammer blow fell the hardest.'*

- **Deployment Map:** Hammer and Anvil. The player who deploys first is the Attacker.
- **Primary – Extraction:** the encircled Attacker must break out. A unit with most of its surviving models within 2" of the Defender's board edge, and not locked in combat, is Extracted: it is removed at the end of the turn and gives the opponent no Victory Points. Units arriving from Reserves (including Deep Strike) may not be placed within 24" of the Defender's board edge, and no unit may be Extracted the turn it arrives. At the end of the game, the Attacker scores 1 Victory Point per Extracted unit, and the Defender scores 1 per Attacker unit still on the board or destroyed.
- **Secondary:** Slay the Warlord, First Strike.
- **Extra Mission Special Rule – Sudden Death:** if at the end of any Game Turn a player has no models on the battlefield, the battle ends immediately. Reserves do not count as on the battlefield; units Falling Back or embarked in a Transport, Building or Fortification do. The winner is still decided on Victory Points, but the player with models left on the battlefield scores +1.

#### Mission 6: Strategic Outreach

*'The winning move is often simply having a good plan.'*

- **Deployment Map:** Dawn of War. The player who deploys first is the Attacker.
- **Primary – Hold at All Costs:** after deployment, each player places one Objective Marker in their own Deployment Zone and one outside any Deployment Zone. At the end of the game, controlling the marker in the opponent's Deployment Zone is worth +4 Victory Points, and each marker outside the Deployment Zones +3.
- **Secondary:** Slay the Warlord, First Strike.

#### Mission 7: Killing Blow

*"Watch and learn how hounds bring down a bear."*

- **Deployment Map:** Ambush.
- **Primary – Hunt or Be Hunted:** after deployment, each player nominates three enemy units. At the end of the game, destroying one is worth +1 Victory Point, two +2, and all three +4.
- **Secondary:** Slay the Warlord, First Strike, Attrition (+2 Victory Points in this mission), Big Game Hunter (+2 Victory Points in this mission).

#### Mission 8: Sabotage

*'To crush your enemies, see them driven before you, and to hear the lamentations of their women.'*

- **Deployment Map:** Vanguard Strike. The player who deploys first is the Attacker.
- **Primary – Booby-Trapped!:** after deployment, the Defender places five Objective Markers, none within 6" of a board edge: at most two in its own Deployment Zone, and the rest at least 6" outside it.
  - **Defender:** a Defender unit controlling a marker may give up its Shooting Attacks in the Shooting phase to try to sabotage it; on a D6 roll of 4+ it is sabotaged and the Defender scores +1 Victory Point. Each marker can be sabotaged once, with one attempt per turn.
  - **Attacker:** when an Attacker unit takes control of an unsabotaged marker, the Attacker scores +1 Victory Point. If the marker was sabotaged, roll a D6: on 4+ the Attacker scores +1 Victory Point; on 1-3 it explodes, every unit within 3" suffers a Strength 8, AP - hit per model in range and takes a Pinning test, and the marker is removed.
  - **End of game:** the Attacker gains +1 Victory Point per marker it controls, and the Defender +2 per sabotaged marker that exploded.
- **Secondary:** Slay the Warlord, First Strike, Ground Control.

#### Mission 9: Control Point

*"The measure of true glory is... to spit defiance in midnight's eye."*

- **Deployment Map:** Search and Destroy. The player who deploys first is the Attacker. The Defender also deploys two Barracks buildings.
- **Primary – Shield Barrier:** the 18"-diameter circle at the centre is shielded. No Infiltrate, Scout or other pre-game moves into it, and any Deep Strike Assault into it counts as a Deep Strike Mishap. After deployment, each player places one Objective Marker anywhere, and one is placed at the centre. At the end of the game, each controlled marker is worth +3 Victory Points; a contested one gives +1 to each player.
- **Secondary:** Slay the Warlord, Attrition, First Strike, Big Game Hunter.
- **Extra Mission Special Rule – Barracks:** the Barracks are Impassable Terrain with Armour Value 14 on all sides, 4 Hull Points and an Access Point on each side, set up anywhere more than 6" outside the Attacker's Deployment Zone (a Bastion or similar building). Once per turn, the Defender may bring one unit in from Reserves as if disembarking from a Barracks instead of from its board edge. A destroyed Barracks can no longer be used.

#### Mission 10: Stronghold

*'A fortress is built with blood and toil. Only by blood and toil may it be taken.'*

- **Deployment Map:** Ambush. The player who deploys first is the Attacker. In the source's diagram, the Defender's 18"-deep Deployment Zone sits in the middle of the 72" x 48" table.
- **Primary – The Fortress, Fallen:** after deployment, the Defender places four Objective Markers at the locations shown on the source's diagram. They are Secret Objectives. At the end of each player turn, each controlled marker is worth +1 Victory Point, kept even if later lost, and each Secret Objective a player has revealed is worth +1 at the end of the game. At the end of the game, divide the board into quarters, treating the Defender's Deployment Zone as a separate area; each quarter controlled is worth +1. If the Defender controls its own Deployment Zone it gains +5; if the Attacker controls it, the Attacker gains +3.
- **Secondary:** Slay the Warlord, First Strike, Big Game Hunter.
- **Extra Mission Special Rule – Secret Objectives:** the first time a player controls a Secret Objective, roll a D6: **1 Bomb!** (each model in the controlling unit suffers a Strength 5, AP4 hit); **2 or 5 Nothing**; **3 Extra Ammo** (the unit doubles its shots in its next Shooting Attack); **4 Reserve Beacon** (+1 to the player's Reserve rolls next turn); **6 Melta Bomb** (the unit gains one single-use Melta Bomb, used by one model).

#### Mission 11: Refinery

*"If you remember nothing else from this, brother – remember how this loss stung you."*

- **Deployment Map:** Clash of the Line.
- **Primary – Take the Tankers:** after choosing Deployment Zones but before deploying, place eight Filtration Tankers as Objective Markers: five by the player who deploys second, three by the other. At the end of the game, each Tanker a player controls is worth +3 Victory Points, and each one they destroyed +1.
- **Secondary:** Ground Control, Big Game Hunter, First Strike.
- **Extra Mission Special Rule – Filtration Tankers:** each Tanker is Impassable Terrain about 2" across and 4" tall, with Armour Value 11 on all sides and 3 Hull Points. It counts as a Vehicle and adds +1 to Penetrating Hit results on the Vehicle Damage table (cumulative with AP1, AP2, Wrecker and similar). Shooting Attacks at a Tanker from more than 12" away must be Snap Shots. Tankers count as Objective Markers for control.

#### Mission 12: Heavy Ordnance

*"Nothing in the world truly beats the thundering of guns."*

- **Deployment Map:** Ransack (a custom map shown in the source, with 36"-deep Attacker and 24"-deep Defender Deployment Zones on a 72" x 48" table). The player who deploys first is the Attacker.
- **Primary – Break their Lines:** after deployment, the Defender places one Objective Marker in its own Deployment Zone and one in no man's land, and the Attacker places one anywhere outside the Defender's Deployment Zone; none within 6" of a board edge. At the end of the game, the Attacker gains +4 Victory Points for each Defender-placed marker it controls. The Defender gains +3 if it holds the Attacker's marker, and +1 for each Attacker unit it destroys.
- **Secondary:** Slay the Warlord, First Strike, Ground Control.

### Apocalypse Missions

These missions are for battles on a truly terrifying scale. The recommended size is 4,000 points or more on a table of 8ft x 6ft or larger. On a larger table, scale Deployment Zone measurements up to match; for example, Dawn of War uses 18" Deployment Zones instead of 12" on a table 50% larger. To pick a mission at random, roll a D3.

Apocalypse missions use the Standard Mission Setup, except that after four turns you roll a D6, and on 4+ a fifth and final turn is played.

#### Apocalypse Mission 1: Cold Storage

*'Among the burned fields, across the cities torn to rubble, and in view of the stars set ablaze – these all will be the battlefields of this war.'*

- **Deployment Map:** Dawn of War. Place the Filtration Tankers when setting up the scenery, before sides are decided.
- **Primary – Take the Tankers:** after choosing Deployment Zones but before deploying, place eight Filtration Tankers as Objective Markers: five by the player who deploys second, three by the other. At the end of the game, each Tanker a player controls is worth +3 Victory Points, and each one they destroyed +2.
- **Primary – Drowned in Blood:** at the end of the game, each enemy unit destroyed or Falling Back is worth Victory Points by type: Infantry and Daemons +1; Dreadnoughts, Automata, Cavalry and Armigers +2; Vehicles without the Super-Heavy, Titan or Lumbering Sub-type +2; Vehicles with one of those Sub-types +4; Primarchs +6.
- **Secondary:** Slay the Warlord, First Strike, Big Game Hunter.
- **Extra Mission Special Rule – Filtration Tankers:** as in the Standard mission Refinery, but each Tanker has 4 Hull Points, adds +2 to Penetrating Hit results on the Vehicle Damage table, and must be Snap Shot from more than 18" away.

#### Apocalypse Mission 2: Bloodbath

*'Until the ground itself pools and flows crimson. Until the soil is stained evermore with the blood of the enemy.'*

- **Setting Up:** determine the Deployment Map, set up scenery, then place the Objectives as described in Bloodbath Objectives.
- **Primary – Bloodbath Objectives:** at the start of each player's turn, that player gains +1 Victory Point per Objective Marker they control, kept even if later lost. At the end of the game, roll a D6 for each Objective each player controls: 1-2 no Victory Points, 3-4 +1, 5-6 +3.
- **Primary – Tide of Carnage:** at the end of the game, a side claims a sector if it has a Scoring unit there and the enemy has none. Its own Deployment Zone is worth +3 Victory Points, no man's land +5, and the opponent's Deployment Zone +7.
- **Primary – Death Toll:** at the end of the game, each enemy unit destroyed or Falling Back is worth +1 Victory Point.
- **Secondary:** Slay the Warlord, First Strike.
- **Extra Mission Special Rule – Bloodbath Objectives:** five Objective Markers, placed alternately outside both Deployment Zones before deployment is decided, the second-deploying player first, none within 6" of each other or a board edge. If both players agree, use three pieces of Objective Terrain instead: distinct pieces 6" to 12" on each side, fully outside both Deployment Zones and able to give Infantry cover (ruins, craters, redoubts, derelict buildings, wrecks). A Scoring or Denial unit needs a model within 6" of the centre of the terrain's ground level to claim or deny it, and no unit may claim or deny more than one.
- **Extra Mission Special Rule – Heavy Armour:** all Vehicles that are not Flyers also count as Scoring units.

#### Apocalypse Mission 3: Kingmaker

*'Face me, coward! Face me and die with honour, or stay in your hole and die with none!'*

- **Deployment Map:** Search and Destroy. Each player sets aside its Warlord and either its Retinue or one unit it could normally join, to deploy by the Kingmaker rule.
- **Primary – The Spoils of War:** after choosing Deployment Zones but before deploying, players alternately place D6+3 Relic Markers, the second-deploying player first, none within 12" of each other, in either Deployment Zone, or within 9" of a table edge.
- **Primary – Priority Target:** at the end of the game, a player whose Warlord is alive and in play gains +5 Victory Points. When a Warlord is slain and removed, place a marker where it was; it counts as a Relic Marker, and recovering it is worth +3 Victory Points on top of the normal Relic reward.
- **Secondary – Hammerfall:** each enemy Heavy Support unit destroyed is worth +1 Victory Point.
- **Secondary – A Light in the Dark:** each Independent Character carrying a Relic Marker at the end of the game is worth +2 Victory Points, or +4 if it is the Warlord.
- **Extra Mission Special Rule – Ceasefire:** during the first Game Turn, a unit containing a Warlord may only be shot at or charged by a unit containing the enemy Warlord. If a unit containing a Warlord shoots or charges any unit that does not contain the enemy Warlord, this rule ends.
- **Extra Mission Special Rule – Kingmaker:** each Warlord deploys first, outside the normal Deployment Zones in the central circle of the Search and Destroy map, with its Retinue or the one unit set aside for it.
- **Extra Mission Special Rule – Relics of a Bygone Age:** at the end of a player's Movement phase, an Infantry model within 1" of a Relic Marker may pick it up and carries it from then on; a unit may only have one model carrying a Relic. At the end of any of its Movement phases, the carrier may drop it within 1". If the carrier is slain, place the Relic as close as possible to where it was. At the end of the game, each Relic carried is worth +3 Victory Points, and a player carrying at least twice as many as the opponent gains +2 more.

### Zone Mortalis Missions

These missions can be used alongside or instead of the Zone Mortalis missions in *Campaigns in the Age of Darkness: Siege of Cthonia*. To pick one at random, roll a D6.

#### Zone Mortalis Mission Setup

Unless a mission says otherwise, every Zone Mortalis mission uses these steps:

- **Setting Up:** set up the battlefield using the Zone Mortalis rules in *Siege of Cthonia*.
- **Strategic Advantage:** determine Strategic Advantage before setting up; that player chooses who is Attacker and who is Defender.
- **Reinforcement Points:** each player starts with the Reinforcement Points the mission gives, uses them to place units in its own Deployment Zone, keeps any unspent, and gains more at the end of each Game Turn (as the mission says, plus any from mission special rules). Units not placed go into Reinforcements.
- **Quadrant Deployment:** the battlefield is split into four equal quarters. The Defender picks one as its Deployment Zone and deploys up to its starting Reinforcement Points, not within 6" of the centre. The Attacker then rolls a D6: on 3 or less, its Deployment Zone is the opposite quarter; on 4+, it picks any of the other three. It deploys the same way.
- **Objective Markers:** placed as the mission says, following the rulebook's restrictions (page 306) plus the mission's own.
- **First Turn:** the player with Strategic Advantage goes first, unless the opponent rolls for First Strike successfully.
- **Game Length:** after five turns, roll a D6. On 4+, a sixth and final turn is played.
- **Victory Conditions:** the player with the most Victory Points wins. If tied, the player with more Victory Points from Secondary Objectives wins; if still tied, the game is a draw.
- **Counter Assault:** when deploying from Reinforcements, a player may spend extra Reinforcement Points to bring a unit in from another edge, at the costs in the mission's table. A player's board edges are the two edges of its Deployment Quadrant; Flank Edges are the edges of quarters neither player deployed in.

Zone Mortalis missions use these Secondary Objectives, with the rewards each mission gives:

- **Attrition:** the army that destroyed the most enemy units gains the listed Victory Points.
- **First Strike:** each unit completely destroyed during a player's first turn is worth +1 Victory Point.
- **Last Man Standing:** the side with the most surviving units gains the listed Victory Points.
- **Slay the Warlord:** +1 Victory Point for destroying the enemy Warlord, or +2 if it was a Primarch.

#### Zone Mortalis Mission 1: Priority Cargo

*Cargoes of special importance became the focus of fierce fighting, as factions tried to snatch them from each other for any advantage.*

- **Reinforcement Points:** the Attacker starts with 8; both players gain 2 per Game Turn. The Defender always has enough to deploy its whole force in its quarter.
- **Deployment:** the Defender deploys as normal. The Attacker deploys in either quarter next to the Defender's, not within 6" of the centre.
- **Objective Markers:** after deployment, players alternately place six numbered Objective Markers (1 to 6), the second-deploying player first, not within 6" of each other or 3" of any Deployment Zone.
- **Primary – Secure the Cargo:** before the game, roll a dice secretly under a cup. Objective Markers are worth nothing for being held. Instead, in its Shooting phase a Scoring unit may have a model secure a marker before shooting. That model carries it and may not make Shooting Attacks; its unit may not Advance or use jump packs, jet packs or wings. The carrier may drop it in a later Shooting phase, and drops it where it falls if slain. A marker is retrieved when carried back into its controller's Deployment Zone. At the end of the game, reveal the dice: whoever retrieved the matching marker gains +5 Victory Points.
- **Secondary:** Slay the Warlord, Attrition (+D3), Last Man Standing (+D3).
- **Counter Assault costs (Attacker / Defender):** own edge 0 / 0; Flank Edge +1 / +1; opponent's edge +3 / +3.

#### Zone Mortalis Mission 2: Lights Out

*Players use lighting and door-seals to confound each other and steer their foes into the teeth of their weapons.*

- **Reinforcement Points:** both players start with 7 and gain 2 per Game Turn. Use Quadrant Deployment.
- **Objective Markers:** after deployment, the Defender places one Objective Marker in each quarter, touching a Column or Wall, not within 6" of the centre or each other.
- **Primary – Control Panel Override:** at the start of each turn, the active player gains +1 Victory Point per Activated Objective it controls, kept even if later lost.
- **Secondary:** Slay the Warlord, Attrition (+1).
- **Mission Special Rule – Lights Out!:** all Objectives start Activated. At the start of its Shooting phase, instead of shooting, an Infantry unit within 1" of an Objective may use its control panel, once per Objective per player turn:
  - **This Objective:** switch it between Activated and Deactivated on a 3+; on a 1-2 it becomes Deactivated.
  - **Another Objective:** switch it on a 4+; on a 1-3 the panel's own Objective becomes Deactivated.
  - **Deactivated:** its quarter falls under Night Fighting with a 12" range limit instead of 24", every door there closes and Locks, and the Objective gives no Victory Points. A model with any part of its base on a door's threshold as it closes takes a Crushed hit (*Siege of Cthonia*, page 173).
  - **Activated:** Night Fighting ends in its quarter, and its doors unlock but stay closed; destroyed doors are unaffected.
  - No player may perform a System Override.
- **Counter Assault costs (Attacker / Defender):** own edge 0 / 0; Flank Edge +1 / +1; opponent's edge +3 / +3.

#### Zone Mortalis Mission 3: Dead Void

*Fighting aboard a ship cracking apart under enemy fire, scrabbling to recover key intel before the void claims you.*

- **Setting Up:** the battlefield always has a ceiling, and uses 3 Doors per 12" x 12" grid section.
- **Reinforcement Points:** both players start with 6 and gain 2 per Game Turn. Use Quadrant Deployment.
- **Objective Markers:** after deployment, the Defender places one in each quarter, not within 9" of the centre or each other.
- **Primary – Claim the Intel:** at the start of its turn, a player with a Line unit within 1" of an Objective may try to claim it. That unit may not move, shoot or charge this turn, or the attempt fails. If it succeeds, at the end of the turn the player scores +3 Victory Points and the marker is removed.
- **Secondary:** Slay the Warlord, Attrition (+1), Last Man Standing (+1).
- **Mission Special Rule – Unstable Ground:** the area within 3" of each Objective Marker is Difficult, Dangerous and Hazardous Terrain, even after the marker is removed. No unit may make pre-game redeployments.
- **Mission Special Rule – The Hungering Void:** when a door to a section no unit has entered yet is opened or destroyed, roll a D6. On a 1-2, every unit with a model within 12" and line of sight of the door takes an Initiative test; units made entirely of Dreadnoughts, Monstrous or Automata models, or Heavy models, add +1 to their Initiative for it. A unit that fails is sucked into the void and destroyed, and gives Victory Points as normal. The door then closes and Locks for the rest of the battle, staying in place even if destroyed. No player may perform a System Override.
- **Counter Assault costs (Attacker / Defender):** own edge 0 / +1; Flank Edge +1 / +1; opponent's edge unavailable / +3.

#### Zone Mortalis Mission 4: Ingress

*A desperate scramble to keep the attackers out, while boarding torpedoes and assault drills keep coming regardless.*

- **Setting Up:** the battlefield always has a ceiling and must be at least 48" x 24", and at least 12" longer than it is wide. Access Points may not be placed on the Attacker's board edge.
- **Reinforcement Points:** both players start with 8 and gain 3 per Game Turn. The Defender deploys within 9" of its long board edge. The Attacker deploys nothing and uses Zone Breach instead.
- **Objective Markers:** after deployment, each player places two, not within 6" of each other or of any Access Point.
- **Game Length:** after four turns, roll a D6; on 4+, one more turn is played (the source says "a sixth and final turn").
- **Primary – Take Control:** at the start of each Defender Shooting phase, the Defender gains +1 Victory Point per Objective it controls. At the start of each Attacker Shooting phase, an Attacker unit controlling an Objective may either gain +1 Victory Point or destroy it for +2; a unit that destroys one may not shoot or do anything in place of shooting that phase. All these Victory Points are kept. A destroyed Objective gives no more Victory Points.
- **Secondary:** First Strike, Slay the Warlord, Attrition (+D3).
- **Mission Special Rule – Zone Breach:** the Attacker deploys nothing and may not use Access Points. Instead, it assigns its units to Boarding Markers (boarding torpedoes or assault drills), each with a Transport Capacity of 16 that several units may share; a unit may only be assigned to one. Each Boarding Marker costs 3 Reinforcement Points whatever is in it. At the start of each Attacker turn, it may buy as many Boarding Markers as it can afford. For each, declare which marker is arriving, place it on the Attacker's board edge and scatter it the full distance along that edge; a scatter arrow not clearly pointing left or right counts as a hit. If it would stop where its models cannot disembark, shorten the scatter as little as possible. The models then enter play from it as if from an Access Point, counting as disembarking from an Assault Vehicle, and the marker is removed. The Defender may not use Boarding Markers. The source recommends using Liber Panoptica's Transport Capacity changes for Automata, Dreadnoughts and similar.
- **Counter Assault costs (Attacker / Defender):** own edge unavailable / 0; Flank Edge unavailable / +1; opponent's edge unavailable / unavailable. The Defender's edge is the long edge of its Deployment Zone, and the Flank Edges are the short edges.

#### Zone Mortalis Mission 5: Lockdown

*Fighting for a key point, the defender desperately scrabbling to lock the attacker out or funnel them into their guns.*

- **Setting Up:** place a Control Bunker at or near the centre: four Columns, one Standard Door and seven Walls forming a small square with a single entrance. No other Columns or Walls may touch it.
- **Reinforcement Points:** the Defender starts with 9 and gains 2 per Game Turn; the Attacker starts with 6 and gains 4.
- **Deployment:** the Defender's Deployment Zone is inside the Control Bunker and within 9" of its outer walls. The Attacker rolls a D6: on 3+ it picks two diagonally opposite quarters as its Deployment Zone; on a 1-2 the Defender picks them. The Attacker may not deploy within 9" of the centre or 12" of any Defender unit.
- **Objective Markers:** after deployment, the Defender places one Objective Marker inside the Control Bunker: the Control Bunker Objective.
- **Primary – Take the Control Bunker!:** controlling the Control Bunker Objective at the end of the game is worth +5 Victory Points. If Victory Points are tied, the player controlling it wins; if neither does, the game is a draw.
- **Secondary:** Slay the Warlord, Attrition (+D3), Last Man Standing (+D3).
- **Mission Special Rule – Control Node Bunker:** at the start of its Shooting phase, instead of shooting, an Infantry unit within 1" of the Control Bunker Objective may use its control panel, once per player turn, to close and Lock any two doors, open and unlock any two doors, close all doors, or open and unlock all doors (non-destroyed doors only). It works on a 2+. A unit split by a closing door must try to regroup by the closest route, breaking the door down if needed, and a model on the threshold takes a Crushed hit (*Siege of Cthonia*, page 173). No player may perform a System Override.
- **Counter Assault costs (Attacker / Defender):** own edge +1 / unavailable; Flank Edge +1 / +1; opponent's edge unavailable / +2. The Defender has no board edge.

#### Zone Mortalis Mission 6: VIP Extraction

*Dignitaries, remembrancers or negotiators: mortal cargo that could decide control of whole sectors.*

- **Reinforcement Points:** both players start with 6 and gain 2 per Game Turn. Use Quadrant Deployment.
- **Objective Marker:** after deployment, the Attacker places the Extraction Point in the quarter diagonally opposite the Defender's, at least 6" from any board edge.
- **Primary – Evacuate the VIP:** if the Defender's VIP is in base contact with the Extraction Point at the end of any Attacker turn, the game ends and the Defender wins. If the VIP is slain at any point, the game ends and the Attacker wins. If the VIP is alive but has not reached the Extraction Point by the end of the final turn, the game is a draw.
- **Mission Special Rule – The VIP:** the Defender has a VIP model with this profile, Unit Type Infantry (Independent Character), a Laspistol and a Refractor Field:

| Model | M | WS | BS | S | T | W | I | A | Ld | Sv |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| VIP | 7 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 1 | 3 | 1 | 6 | 4+ |

- **Protectorate:** the VIP may not be targeted by Precision Shots (X), Precision Strikes (X) or Sniper attacks, and Wounds may not be allocated to it, unless there are no other eligible targets in its unit.
- **High Value Target:** the VIP has Hit & Run, but does not give it to a unit it joins; it may use it to leave a combat while its unit stays engaged.
- **Counter Assault costs (Attacker / Defender):** own edge 0 / 0; Flank Edge +1 / +2; opponent's edge +4 / +2.

## Additional Game Modes

### Game Mode: Decurion

*Small skirmishes between covert strike teams and lone survivors banded together, fighting for survival.*

Decurion uses small numbers of models for quick games. The recommended size is 250 to 750 points, on a table between 2ft x 2ft and 3ft x 3ft.

#### Decurion Army Construction

- Build your force with the Decurion Warband Force Organisation Chart.
- No models with the Unique Sub-type may be selected.
- All models are Individuals unless they are in unit coherency at the end of the Movement phase, when they form an Ad-Hoc Unit. Ad-Hoc Units may also be formed during deployment.
- An Ad-Hoc Unit may only contain models that are Sworn Brothers (per the Allies Matrix), share the same Legiones Astartes (X), or come from the same Faction and are meant to be the same Regiment, Cult, Taghmata or similar. Models in an Ad-Hoc Unit are a single unit for all rules, including Leadership tests, Movement and Shooting Attacks against them.
- For allies, special rules and anything else that checks the Allies Matrix, your Warband counts as your Hero's Faction.
- Only models in base-to-base contact during their Initiative step may fight in the Assault phase.
- All Infantry in a Warband count as having the Line Sub-type, unless their Faction is Distrusted Allies with your Hero's Faction.
- No model may issue a challenge, and you may not select a model with a Legion Standard.
- No model with Master of the Legion, Feudal Hierarchy, Cohort Doctrines, Tribune of the Golden Legion or Path of Command may be selected, and no Custodian Shield Captain.
- No model with the Assassin, Abomination or Mechanised Sub-type may be selected.
- Imperialis Militia Heavy Ordnance Batteries may not be selected. Imperialis Militia Field Gun Batteries gain Support Squad.
- A model with Aspect Shrine (X) may only join or be joined by models with the same Aspect Shrine (X). The selection limits on Aspect Shrine units are ignored.
- Models with Chosen Warriors count as having the Character Sub-type for being selected as a Leader or Hero, but not for any of their own options.

**Warlords.** Your Hero must be your Warlord. In games of 500 points or less, it has no Warlord Trait. In games of 501 points or more, it has one Warlord Trait chosen from the Core Warlord Traits (rulebook, page 285) or another list available to its Allegiance or army list.

**Squad-wide upgrades.** Wargear or special rules bought for a whole squad at a fixed cost (such as Melta Bombs for a Legion Destroyer Assault Squad at +25 points) are instead bought for individual models at +5 points per model.

#### Decurion Warband Construction

A Decurion Warband buys individual models instead of HQ, Troops and other slots.

**Heroes and Leaders**

- Only Infantry may be selected.
- A Hero must have the Character Sub-type and may have Independent Character. A Leader must have the Character Sub-type but may not have Independent Character.
- A Hero or Leader costs its normal cost if it can normally be bought as a single model, or the cost of adding a model to its normal unit plus 10 points (for example, a Legion Tactical Sergeant costs 20 points).
- In a unit with no per-model cost, divide the unit's cost by its number of models, round down to the nearest 5, and add 10 points.

**Troopers**

- Only Infantry may be selected, and Troopers may not have the Character Sub-type or Independent Character.
- A Trooper costs the same as adding a model to its normal unit. In a unit with no per-model cost, divide the unit's cost by its number of models and round down to the nearest 5.
- An upgrade normally limited to one per X models still needs that many models in the Warband (for example, five Legion Veterans for one flamer). An upgrade normally limited to one per squad is limited to one per Warband.
- A Trooper with a 2+ Armour Save has a 3+ Armour Save instead in this game mode.

**Artillery**

- **Legion Warband:** Rapier Carriers may be selected, with two Legion Gunners in the same slot. Models with Legion Artillerists may only join or be joined by other models with that rule, and Legion Gunners may only be selected as part of a Rapier Carrier.
- **Solar Auxilia Warband:** as above, with two Auxilia Gunners and Auxilia Artillerists.
- **Imperialis Militia Warband:** as above, with two Militia Gunners and Militia Artillerists.
- **Asuryani Warband:** Vaul's Wrath Support Batteries may be selected, with two Guardian Crew models and Guardian Artillerists, on the same terms.

**Tercios and Strength in Numbers**

- **Decurion Tercio** (replaces Tercio on Infantry): when included as a Trooper, up to two additional identical models may be bought in the same slot. They must deploy in coherency, including as part of an Ad-Hoc Unit, but are Individuals once the game begins.
- **Scattered Survivors** (replaces Strength in Numbers on Infantry): as Decurion Tercio, but up to three additional identical models.

**Pursuit Beasts and Beastmasters**

- For each model with Pursuit Beasts and Silent Sisterhood (X), one Pursuit Beast may be bought at its cost in *Liber Imperium* (page 58). It shares that model's slot, must deploy in coherency with it, and must stay in coherency with at least one such model if it can.
- For each model with Beastmasters and the Militia Sub-type, one War Beast may be bought at its cost in the Imperialis Militia PDF (page 28), on the same terms. A model with Beastmasters may only join or be joined by War Beasts and other models with Beastmasters.

**Decurion Warband Force Organisation Chart**

| Compulsory | Optional |
| --- | --- |
| 1 Hero | +2 Leaders |
| 9 Troopers | +9 Troopers |

#### Reactions in the Decurion Game Mode

No Core Reactions or Advanced Reactions may be used; all armies use only the Decurion Reactions below. A player may make one Reaction in each phase of the opponent's turn. This can never be increased, and no player may ever make more than one Reaction in a phase.

- **Contact! (Movement phase):** when an enemy unit ends a move within 12" and line of sight of a friendly unit, that unit may make a Shooting Attack at it. Every shot is a Snap Shot except Template weapons, so Blast weapons cannot fire; no indirect fire is allowed. Template weapons may only fire if the target is within 8", using Wall of Death. The reacting unit counts as Stationary.
- **Go to Ground! (Shooting phase):** when an enemy unit declares a Shooting Attack against a friendly unit, the target gains a 5+ Go to Ground save against that attack. This counts as a Cover Save that Ignores Cover cannot negate.
- **Reposition! (Shooting phase):** instead, each model in the target unit may move up to its unmodified Initiative in inches. Range and line of sight are measured to its new position; if no models are left in range or line of sight, the attacker may not pick a new target.
- **Hold Fast! (Assault phase):** when an enemy unit declares a Charge against a friendly unit, before Charge rolls, the target takes a Morale check. If failed, it Falls Back D6" and then automatically regroups. If passed, it automatically passes any Morale checks for losing the assault in the following Fight sub-phase.

#### Missions in the Decurion Game Mode

Decurion games use custom Mission Objectives instead of rulebook or Liber Panoptica missions, always with the Night Fighting mission special rule. Players agree before the game whether to score at the start of each player's turn, at the end of the game, or both.

**Mission Type.** Roll a D6 (2D6 in games of 600 points or more, using both results separately for two Mission Objectives):

| D6 | Mission Type | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Seize & Secure | After deployment and pre-game moves, players take turns placing 4 Objective Markers, Defender first. None may be in either Deployment Zone, or within 6" of a board edge or each other. Each is worth +1 Victory Point to its controller. |
| 2 | Run Them Down! | Each Scoring model wholly within the opponent's Deployment Zone is worth +2 Victory Points. |
| 3 | Lynchpin | Place one Objective Marker at the centre of the board, worth +1 Victory Point to its controller. |
| 4 | Domination | Divide the table into quarters. Each quarter where you have more Scoring units than your opponent is worth +1 Victory Point. |
| 5 | Blood Tithe | Each enemy Trooper slain is worth +1 Victory Point, Leader +2 and Hero +3. Scored only at the end of the game. |
| 6 | Overrun | Having more Scoring models than your opponent in your own Deployment Zone is worth +1 Victory Point, in no man's land +2, and in the opponent's Deployment Zone +3. |

**Deployment Map.** Roll a D3:

| D3 | Deployment Map | Layout |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Recovery Raid | The battlefield is split into four equal quarters, each a Deployment Zone. No units may deploy in the 12"-diameter circle at the centre. A player's battlefield edges are those that form part of their Deployment Zone. |
| 2 | Breaking Blow | The battlefield is split into two halves along its length. Each Deployment Zone is a 12"-wide strip across one half, and each player's battlefield edge is the long edge touching it. |
| 3 | Rearguard Assault | The battlefield is split into two halves along the diagonal. Each Deployment Zone is a triangle 12" along each side, and a player's battlefield edges are those that form part of it. |

**Attacker and Defender.** Roll off; the winner chooses.

**Night Fighting.** Roll off or agree whether Night Fighting is in effect. Unlike the normal rule, it only applies on a D6 roll of 4+, unless both players agree to use it.

**Deployment.** The Attacker picks a Deployment Zone and deploys everything except Reserves, including Fortifications. The Defender then does the same. Place any Objective Markers the Mission Objectives need.

**First Turn.** The Attacker goes first, unless the opponent Seizes the Initiative.

**Game Length.** After four turns, roll a D6: on 3+ a fifth turn is played. After a fifth turn, roll a D6: on 5+ a sixth and final turn is played.

**Victory Conditions.** The player with the most Victory Points wins. If tied, the player with more Victory Points from Secondary Objectives wins; if still tied, the game is a draw. Sudden Death Victory does not apply, so always total Victory Points, even if one player has no models left.

### Game Mode: Centurion

*Covert raids, key strikes and running skirmishes between lightly armed forces.*

Centurion expands the Narrative, Campaign and Matched Play game modes. The recommended size is 500 to 1,500 points, on a table at least 4ft x 4ft.

#### Centurion Army Construction

- Use the Incursio Force Organisation Chart. No Allied Detachments or optional Detachments may be taken.
- Legiones Astartes armies may only use the Centurion Rites of War below.
- Mechanicum armies may use the Centurion War-Covenants, Solar Auxilia armies the Centurion Cohort Doctrines, and Asuryani armies the Centurion World-Runes (no other World-Runes).
- Legio Custodes armies may not select a Chamber of the Golden Host.
- No Vehicles may be selected, except Legion Rhino Transports, Skorpius Duneriders, Kharon Pattern Acquisitors, Silent Sisterhood Rhino Transports, Aurox Transports, Solar Auxilia and Imperialis Militia Chimera Transports, and Wave Serpents, by any unit that may normally take them.
- No model with Master of the Legion, Feudal Hierarchy, Cohort Doctrines, Tribune of the Golden Legion or Path of Command may be selected, and at most one Custodian Shield Captain of any variant.
- No model with the Assassin or Abomination Sub-type may be selected.
- Dreadnought, Automata, Artillery, Mechanised and Grav-Platform units may not be expanded beyond their starting size.
- For each full 125 points in your army, you may take 1 Wound's worth of models with Toughness 6 or 7 (for example, 10 Wounds at 1,250 points, enough for two Legion Castra Ferrum Dreadnoughts). No models with Toughness 8 or more may be selected.

**Warlords.** Nominate a Warlord as normal (rulebook, page 284). In games of 500 points or less it has no Warlord Trait; in games of 501 points or more it has one Warlord Trait from the Core list (rulebook, page 285) or another list available to it.

**Incursio Force Organisation Chart**

| Compulsory | Optional |
| --- | --- |
| 1 HQ | +1 HQ |
| 2 Troops | +2 Troops, +2 Elites, +2 Fast Attack, +2 Heavy Support |

#### Reactions in the Centurion Game Mode

No Core Reactions or Advanced Reactions may be used; all armies use only the Contact!, Go to Ground!, Reposition! and Hold Fast! Reactions, as described for the Decurion game mode. In battles of 1,000 points or fewer per player, a player may make one Reaction in each phase of the opponent's turn, this can never be increased, and no player may make more than one Reaction in a phase. In battles of 1,001 points or more per player, this limit is removed.

#### Missions in the Centurion Game Mode

Instead of a rulebook or Liber Panoptica mission, you may generate custom Primary and Secondary Objectives as below. The Night Fighting and Reserves mission special rules are always used.

**Mission Type.** Roll a D6 (2D6 in games of 1,500 points or more, using both results separately for two Primary Objectives): 1-3 Tactical Raid, 4-5 War for Territory, 6 Annihilation. Then roll a D3 on the matching table.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tactical Raid | 1 | Deadly Ground | After deployment and pre-game moves, the Defender places 3 Objective Markers: one at the centre and one in each player's Deployment Zone. Each is worth +1 Victory Point to its controller at the start of that player's turn. |
| Tactical Raid | 2 | Denial | Place one Objective Marker at the centre, worth +5 Victory Points to its controller at the end of the game. |

| War for Territory | 3 | Desperate Breakout | At the end of the game, the Defender gains +1 Victory Point for each of its units outside every Deployment Zone and +2 for each in the Attacker's Deployment Zone. The Attacker gains +2 for each Defender unit completely destroyed. |

| 3 | Slay the Warlord | A side that destroyed the enemy Warlord gains +1 Victory Point. |
| 4 | Overrun | Each unit slain by a Sweeping Advance is worth +1 Victory Point at the end of the game. |

**Setting up.** Roll for a Deployment Map from the rulebook. Roll off for Attacker and Defender (the winner chooses), and roll off or agree whether Night Fighting is in effect. **Victory Conditions.** The player with the most Victory Points wins. If tied, the player with more Victory Points from Secondary Objectives wins; if still tied, the game is a draw.

#### Centurion Rites of War

A Legiones Astartes army may use any of these Rites of War without a model with Master of the Legion. They may only be used with the Incursio Force Organisation Chart.

##### Centurion Rite of War: Ambush Contingent

**Effects**

- Models with Scout or Infiltrate gain Shrouded (6+), or +1 to Shrouded if they already have it, during the first Game Turn.
- You may re-roll the dice to decide who has the first turn, and re-roll an attempt to Seize the Initiative.
- You choose whether Night Fighting is in effect instead of rolling. If both players have this effect, use the normal Night Fighting rules.
- Legion Scout Squads and Legion Reconnaissance Squads lose Support Squad.

**Limitations**

- All Compulsory Troops must be Legion Scout Squads or Legion Reconnaissance Squads.
- No models in any variant of Legion Terminator armour, and no Infantry with the Heavy or Artillery Sub-type, may be selected.
- The Compulsory HQ choice must be a Legion Centurion with the Vigilator or Pathfinder Consul upgrade.

##### Centurion Rite of War: Blooded Veterans

**Effects**

- Legion Veteran Squads may be taken as Troops, and gain the Line Sub-type when taken as Compulsory Troops.
- Legion Veteran Assault Squads may be taken as non-Compulsory Troops.
- During army building, give each Legion Veteran Squad and Legion Veteran Assault Squad either Precision Strikes (6+) or Precision Shots (6+).

**Limitations**

- All Compulsory Troops must be Legion Veteran Squads. If all of them are removed from play, the opponent gains an additional Victory Point.
- The Detachment must take a Legion Centurion with the Champion Consul upgrade as a Compulsory HQ choice. If it is removed from play, the opponent gains an additional Victory Point.

##### Centurion Rite of War: Breacher Shieldwall

**Effects**

- A model with a Boarding Shield in unit coherency with at least two other such models may re-roll Invulnerable Saves of 1.
- A model with a Boarding Shield in base contact with at least two other such models counts its Weapon Skill as 1 higher against attacks made to hit it in close combat.
- Legion Breacher Squads gain Relentless.

### Game Mode: Praetorian

*Battles between primarily infantry-based forces.*

The recommended size is 1,500 to 2,000 points. Apart from the restrictions below, Praetorian games use all the rules of the Narrative Play game mode.

- No Vehicles may be selected.
- You may select a Rite of War, meeting its requirements as normal.
- For each full 125 points in your army, you may take 1 Wound's worth of models with Toughness 6 or more (for example, 14 Wounds at 1,750 points, enough for two Legion Contemptor Dreadnoughts with 2 Wounds to spare).
- Models with Toughness 8 or more count double their Wounds for this limit; a Legion Leviathan Dreadnought with 7 Wounds counts as 14.

## Community FAQs, Errata and Balance Changes

These entries cover the core rulebook, the campaign books, the Liber faction books, the Legacies PDFs and Exemplary Battles. The source marks each entry as one of three kinds:

- **FAQ:** a clarification of how an existing rule already works.
- **Errata:** a fix for a broken or overlooked rule, without changing what it is meant to do.
- **Balance Change:** a change to make the game fairer and more fun.

The source shows removed text struck through in red and added text in green. In this doc, each entry gives the rule as it now reads, with **added text in bold** and removed text left out. Long rewrites are summarised; check the source for the exact wording if a ruling depends on it.

### Allies Matrix

Liber Panoptica replaces the Allies Matrix in every Games Workshop publication with its own chart, adding factions and adjusting some relationships. It still uses the levels of alliance from the rulebook (pages 10, 12 and 13): Sworn Brothers, Fellow Warriors, Distrusted Allies, and By the Emperor's (or the Warmaster's) Command. Special rules or Rites of War that change a relationship still take priority over the chart. Different Sub-Factions of the same Faction (such as Legio Custodes and Sisters of Silence, or Solar Auxilia and Imperialis Militia) may be taken as allies of each other. The chart itself is a graphic grid; see page 71 of the source.

### Core Rulebook

**General rulings**

- **Explosions (various pages)** – *FAQ:* explosions from vehicles, atomantic or ferromantic deflectors and similar wargear are not Blasts or Templates for any rules.
- **Special Rules Overlap (various pages)** – *FAQ:* if a weapon has several overlapping special rules (such as a graviton gun's Haywire and Graviton Pulse), the controlling player picks one to apply. With several models that each "must do X" (such as several Legion Champions with Never Back Down), pick one trigger; having several never lets you break the core rules.
- **To-Wound Rolls and Special Rules (various pages)** – *FAQ:* if a rule affects To Hit or To Wound rolls (such as Poisoned, Fleshbane, or Disruption against a mixed Automata unit), resolve the hits as if the whole unit could be affected, as in the GW Haywire FAQ. Rules that say they cannot be used in certain circumstances do not benefit.
- **Reactions (pages 158-160, 330)** – *FAQ:* if one action could trigger Reactions from several enemy units, all may react, if they have not already reacted this phase and the player has enough Reaction allotment. Shooting Attacks and Charges made as Reactions must target the triggering unit.
- **Snap Shots (page 169)** – *FAQ:* a model may never voluntarily fire Snap Shots.

**Assault phase**

- **CCWs and Bonus Attacks (page 176)** – *FAQ:* a model with a pistol but no close combat weapon uses the pistol as a close combat weapon, so it cannot claim a second one. A model with two pistols may claim +1 Attack for two close combat weapons.
- **Declare Charges (page 180)** – *FAQ:* a unit that may "charge as normal regardless of any Shooting Attacks made in the same turn" must still follow the normal rules on valid Charge targets, unless a special rule says otherwise.
- **Start of Initiative Step Pile-in (page 184)** – *Errata:* a model not in base contact at the start of its Initiative step **must** make a Pile-in Move if it can.
- **+1 Two Weapons (page 184)** – *Errata:* a weapon with Detonation never counts when working out whether a model has two melee weapons.
- **Sweeping Advances (page 188)** – *Errata:* if the winner's total (Initiative + dice roll) is **equal to or** greater than the opponent's, the Falling Back unit is caught and destroyed.

**Unit Types and Sub-types**

- **Cavalry, Dreadnought and Automata (page 195)** – *Balance Change:* for rules that count models in a unit (such as Rampage), each such model counts as a number of models equal to its starting Wounds. In addition, failed To Wound rolls against Dreadnoughts and Automata from Ordnance weapons or weapons with Sunder may be re-rolled.
- **Daemon (page 196)** – *Errata:* no unit without the Daemon Unit Type **or the Corrupted Sub-type** may join a unit containing Daemons.
- **Primarch (page 196)** – *Balance Change:* the rule letting Primarchs allocate their own hits is replaced: **Primarchs have Precision Strikes (2+) and Precision Shots (2+), and may use them even when normally prevented, including with Snap Shots.** A unit made entirely of Primarchs may ignore Heroic Stand and its penalties for declining a challenge, unless the challenge was issued by a Knight, Gargantuan, Titan, Primarch or Daemon Primarch.
- **Antigrav (page 196)** – *Balance Change:* Antigrav models may move over **friendly and enemy models (but not end on them) and over all terrain, ignoring penalties for Difficult and Dangerous Terrain**, though they test for Dangerous Terrain if they start or end their move in Difficult or Dangerous Terrain. **They may end over Impassable Terrain if the model can be placed on it, taking a Dangerous Terrain test. If forced to end over other models, move the minimum distance so none are underneath.**
- **Characters and Wound Allocation (page 198)** – *Balance Change:* when the controlling player allocates Wounds from a Shooting Attack, or in the Fight sub-phase, a Character may only be allocated as many Wounds as its unmodified Initiative. If the opponent allocates the Wounds, it may allocate them freely. If the Character is removed before all its allocated Wounds are saved, re-allocate the rest as normal. For example, a Legion Tactical Sergeant may take at most four of ten Wounds.

**Vehicles, Flyers and Super-Heavies**

- **Ramming (page 204)** – *FAQ:* if one vehicle in a squadron Rams, the others that do not or cannot Ram must still move their maximum distance to keep coherency, and suffer the same restrictions as a Ramming vehicle.
- **Crew Stunned (pages 208, 329)** – *FAQ:* if a Flyer would end its move where it may not, reposition it the shortest distance to avoid this.
- **Slow Vehicles (page 214)** – *Balance Change:* when a Slow vehicle moves at Cruising Speed, **it may only fire Snap Shots**, replacing the old rule that any movement counted as Cruising Speed.
- **Super-Heavy Vehicle Shooting (page 215)** – *FAQ:* declare all targets before any To Hit rolls.
- **Knights and Titans (page 216)** – *Balance Change:* all Knights and Titans gain Hammer of Wrath (3). They may charge a model in area terrain ignoring vertical distance, and attack a model whose base is within 1" horizontally of their own regardless of height. They gain +2 Attacks for two melee weapons instead of +1.
- **Flyers and Other Models (page 218)** – *Errata:* a Flyer forced to end within 1" of an enemy model, with nowhere else to go (such as after Crew Stunned), moves the shortest distance to end more than 1" away. *Balance Change:* all Flyers gain Countermeasures.
- **The Lumbering Sub-type (page 219)** – *Balance Change:* Lumbering Flyers may fire all their weapons in each Shooting Attack without penalty, but still follow the normal Reaction restrictions for Lumbering.
- **Lumbering Flyers and the Transport Sub-type (page 219)** – *Errata:* when a Lumbering Flyer suffers Catastrophic Damage, each embarked unit suffers one Strength 10, AP2 hit per embarked model, allocated by its controlling player; survivors are placed where the Flyer was, in coherency (the rest are removed), and take a Pinning test. If a Transport is destroyed by a Shooting Attack, units that shot at it may charge the disembarked passengers, if allowed.

**Terrain and Special Rules**

- **Battlefield Terrain (page 220)** – *FAQ:* during setup, agree what cover each ruin or terrain section gives, where line of sight is blocked, what is Impassable, and which terrain vehicles can climb. A model is only "in" terrain if its base or hull is wholly within it; use Wobbly Model Syndrome (page 165) where placement is unclear, including for assault moves.
- **Blind (page 236)** – *Balance Change:* **a non-Vehicle unit that suffers one or more unsaved Wounds** from a Blind weapon takes the Blind test **once the firing unit has finished its Shooting Attacks for that phase**. The rest of the rule is unchanged.
- **Concussive (X) (page 236)** – *Balance Change:* **a unit hit by one or more Concussive attacks takes its Leadership test once the attack is fully resolved.** **If hit by several Concussive attacks with different values, it suffers only the highest modifier.** The rest of the rule is unchanged.
- **Order of Operations for Force Weapons (pages 238, 322)** – *FAQ:* apply flat Strength bonuses (such as a force maul's) first, then the Force power's multiplier. For example, Strength 4 with a Strength 6 weapon and a successful Force power attacks at Strength 12.
- **Guided Fire (page 239)** – *Balance Change:* models wounded by a Guided Fire weapon never get a Cover Save from intervening terrain or models.
- **Hit & Run (page 240)** – *Balance Change:* the break-away move is 2D6 plus the unit's **Initiative** instead of its Movement.
- **Master-Crafted Blast and Template Weapons (page 243)** – *Balance Change:* these count as Twin-Linked.
- **Monster Hunter (page 243)** – *Errata:* also applies to units with the **Gargantuan Sub-type**.
- **Night Vision (page 243)** – *Errata:* no model may make Shrouded rolls against Wounds **or Hull Points** from the unit's attacks.
- **Precision Shots / Strikes (X) (page 245)** – *FAQ:* Precision Strikes never let a model strike into or out of a challenge. The benefit only applies to a successful Hit with a To Hit roll of X or better.
- **Shock Pulse (page 246)** – *Balance Change:* Super-Heavy, Lumbering, Knight and Titan models are not forced to Snap Shoot. Instead, their next Shooting Attack is at -2 Ballistic Skill, and they roll to hit with a Scatter dice, re-rolling any 'Hit' result. This applies once per turn.
- **Blast Markers, Templates and Multi-Level Terrain (pages 234, 248)** – *FAQ:* every model under a template or blast marker's final position is hit, whatever its height in the terrain.

**Army building and missions**

- **Crusade Force Organisation Chart (page 281)** – *FAQ:* however many Optional Detachments you may take, you may only take one Allied Detachment and one Lord of War Detachment. Other charts say how many they allow.
- **Night Fighting (page 308)** – *Balance Change:* if either player declares a night battle, Night Fighting applies in Game Turn 1 on a D6 roll of **4+**. At the start of Game Turn 2, roll again; on a 4+ it applies in Turn 2 as well. At the end of Game Turn 2 it ends, unless a special rule says otherwise.
- **Performing a Flanking Assault (page 311)** – *Errata:* units that could not deploy in the initial Flanking deployment cause no Pinning tests when they arrive, but may be targeted by Interceptor **after they arrive**. Units that disembarked from an Assault Vehicle in step 5 **may** charge in the following Assault phase, **as an exception to Assault Vehicle**.
- **Performing a Deep Strike Assault (page 311)** – *FAQ:* a model that scatters off a board edge is placed within 1" of that edge instead. *Balance Change:* after the first unit, roll a D6. On a 1 the assault is Disordered and the opponent places each other unit **wholly within 24"** of the first; on 2+ the controlling player places them **wholly within 12"** of the first. None may be within 1" of an enemy or in Impassable Terrain.
- **Blood Feud (page 312)** – *Balance Change:* add Blood Feud targets: Armiger +2 Victory Points, Knights and Titans +3, Super-Heavy and Lumbering Vehicles +4. The Price of Failure is scored as well.

**Psychic powers**

- **Telekine Dome (page 324)** – *Balance Change:* the dome now protects models **wholly within 8"** of the Psyker against attackers not also **wholly within 8"**.
- **Telepathic Hallucinations (page 325)** – *Balance Change:* the Pinning test suffers **-1 Leadership per Hit** instead of adding to the roll. Models that ignore Leadership modifiers, such as with Stubborn, ignore this.
- **Thaumaturgic Succour (page 325)** – *Balance Change:* if the Psychic check is passed, **every non-Vehicle model in the target unit regains one lost Wound** (no D6 roll), up to its starting Wounds.
